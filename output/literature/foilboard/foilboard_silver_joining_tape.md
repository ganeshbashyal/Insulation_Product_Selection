# Foilboard Silver Joining Tape

**Foilboard Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

Foilboard Silver Joining Tape is a heavy-duty reinforced aluminum foil tape engineered with a high-reflectance pure silver foil surface to match the silver reflective face of Foilboard insulation panels. Backed with an aggressive pressure-sensitive acrylic adhesive, it provides an airtight, vapor-impermeable seal across panel joins, underfloor seams, and perimeter junctions. It reflects radiant heat across the joint, preventing thermal bypass and moisture vapor migration in wall, ceiling, and underfloor assemblies.

## Key features

- Pure silver reflective foil finish matches the reflective face of Foilboard panels.
- Reinforced fiberglass scrim provides high tensile strength and tear resistance.
- High-tack pressure-sensitive acrylic adhesive ensures permanent bonding.
- Class 1 vapor barrier rating prevents moisture transmission across panel seams.
- Airtight seal eliminates drafts and convective heat loss.
- Operating temperature range from -20°C to +80°C.
- Low Flammability Index (≤ 5) compliant with AS 1530.2.
- Available in 48mm and 72mm widths for versatile on-site sealing.

4. Ensure application temperature is above +5°C.

## Limitations and warnings

- Substrates must be dry and free of sawdust or grease before application.
- Requires firm mechanical pressure with a squeegee to achieve complete adhesive activation.
- Not designed for structural mechanical holding.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure panel surfaces are dry, clean, and free of dust, grease, and oil.
2. Align Foilboard panels with edges tightly butted together.
3. Center 72mm Silver Joining Tape over the joint on the silver reflective side of the board.
4. Peel paper release liner progressively while smoothing tape down along the seam.
5. Press down firmly along the entire tape length using a plastic squeegee or roller to activate adhesive.
6. Smooth out all wrinkles and air pockets to achieve a permanent, airtight seal.
