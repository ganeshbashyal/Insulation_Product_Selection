# Foilboard Flexifast Fasteners

**Foilboard Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

Foilboard Flexifast Fasteners are specialized one-piece nylon anchor fasteners engineered to secure Foilboard rigid insulation panels directly to masonry, brickwork, concrete tilt-up slabs, and timber substrates. Featuring a large, flexible clamping head integrated with an expanding ribbed shank, Flexifast anchors eliminate thermal bridging while firmly holding panels without tearing through the aluminum foil facing. They provide an efficient, hammer-in or push-in fixing solution for residential basement walls, commercial carpark soffits, and precast facades.

## Key features

- One-piece non-conductive nylon construction eliminates thermal bridging.
- Broad flexible head clamps panels firmly without puncturing the aluminum facing.
- Expanding barbed anchor shank locks tightly into pre-drilled masonry or concrete.
- Durable, UV-stabilized, and non-corrosive in damp environments.
- Fast push-in or hammer-in installation into 8mm pre-drilled holes.
- Available in multiple lengths (45mm, 70mm, 95mm) to suit 10mm to 50mm panels.
- High pull-out resistance to withstand wind suction and panel weight.
- Ideal for precast concrete, brick, blockwork, and timber.

2. Confirm masonry drill bit size is 8mm.

4. Verify substrate is concrete, brick, blockwork, or dense timber.

## Limitations and warnings

- Requires pre-drilling into concrete and masonry.
- Not designed for hollow plasterboard or soft substrates without structural backing.
- Non-structural; cannot support secondary heavy cladding hung from the fastener.

## Installation overview

1. Position Foilboard panel flat against masonry, concrete, or timber substrate.
2. Drill an 8mm hole through the Foilboard panel into the masonry substrate to minimum 35mm depth.
3. Insert the Flexifast fastener through the panel into the drilled hole.
4. Tap gently with a hammer or press firmly until the broad head seats flush against the foil.
5. Ensure anchor head clamps the panel firmly without over-driving into the EPS core.
6. Space fasteners at 600mm centers around the perimeter and across the board face.
7. Tape over fastener heads with Foilboard 72mm reinforced foil tape if an airtight vapor seal is required.
