# Foilboard Green

**Foilboard Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Foilboard Green is an Australian-manufactured rigid insulation panel engineered from a fire-retardant expanded polystyrene (EPS) core laminated on both sides with pure aluminum foil facings. Designed for roofs, external and internal walls, retrofits, and suspended timber underfloors, it provides a 3-in-1 thermal insulation, thermal break, and moisture vapor barrier. When paired with adjacent reflective airspaces, it achieves total system R-values from R1.6 to R4.0+ across 10mm to 50mm board thicknesses.

## Key features

- 3-in-1 insulation, thermal break, and moisture barrier in a single rigid panel.
- Pure aluminum reflective facings reflect up to 97% of radiant heat.
- Fire-retardant expanded polystyrene (EPS) core.
- Achieves total system R-values up to R4.0+ when paired with sealed airspaces.
- Fiber-free, non-allergenic, non-carcinogenic, and non-itchy formulation.
- Impermeable to water and will not sag, rot, or deteriorate over time.
- Anti-glare green coating on one face for safe installation in bright sunlight.
- Extremely lightweight, rigid, and fast to cut and fix on site.
- Group 1 fire hazard classification compliant with AS 5637.1 / AS ISO 9705.
- Backed by a 25-year manufacturer performance warranty.

**Selection checklist**

1. Determine required Total R-value (R1.6 to R4.0) to meet NCC or NatHERS targets.
2. Select board thickness (10mm to 50mm) based on available cavity depth.
3. Choose sheet dimensions (2440x1200mm, 2700x1200mm, or 3000x1200mm).
4. Verify minimum 20mm reflective airspace can be maintained in the assembly.
5. Order matching installation accessories (Floor Saddles, Flexifast, 72mm tape).

## Limitations and warnings

- Requires an adjacent sealed airspace of at least 20mm to deliver rated Total R-value.
- Avoid contact with organic solvents, petroleum fuels, and solvent-based adhesives which dissolve EPS.
- Do not leave permanently exposed to direct outdoor UV weather without cladding.
- Maximum continuous service temperature is 80°C.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure structural framing or substrate is level, clean, and dry.
2. Measure and cut panels using a sharp utility knife or hand saw.
3. Maintain a minimum 20mm sealed airspace adjacent to reflective foil faces to generate full thermal R-value.
4. For walls, fix horizontally or vertically across framing with mechanical fasteners and blank plates.
5. For underfloors, install between joists supported on Foilboard Floor Saddles or Retrofit Clips.
6. Butt sheet joints tightly and tape with Foilboard 72mm reinforced aluminum tape.
7. Seal all edges around windows, doors, and service penetrations to maintain airtightness.
8. Ensure anti-glare green face faces the installer during roof and sunny external installations.
