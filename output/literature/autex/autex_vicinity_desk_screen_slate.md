# Autex Vicinity - Desk Screen - Slate

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Vicinity Desk Screen - Slate is a lightweight, semi-rigid acoustic workstation screen crafted from 100% polyester fibre. Designed to be fixed non-destructively to desks using Vicinity Workstation Clamps, the Slate design features a clean rectangular shape with chamfered or rounded corner details that enhance both visual and acoustic privacy. It absorbs speech-range frequencies right where open-plan chatter starts, delivering acoustic sanctuary for focused, independent work.

## Key features

- Lightweight, semi-rigid 12mm or 24mm 100% polyester acoustic screen.
- Slate design provides focused desk-level privacy with clean, tailored lines.
- Constructed with a minimum of 80% post-consumer recycled PET.
- Acoustic rating of 0.5 metric Sabins per unit (800mm Slate screen).
- Non-destructive fixing via Vicinity Workstation Clamps made from 100% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Durable, pin-receptive, moisture-resistant, and will not slump or fray over time.
- Safe, non-toxic, non-allergenic, and free of chemical binders or formaldehyde.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD and Declare Red List Free.
- Available in over 20 solid Cube colours and printed finishes.

## Installation overview

1. Inspect the desk edge to confirm thickness is compatible with Vicinity Workstation Clamps.
2. Position the clamps along the desk edge at recommended spacing (typically 150-200mm in from outer edges).
3. Tighten the clamp thumb-screws or hex bolts securely to the desk underside without overtightening.
4. Insert the 12mm or 24mm Vicinity Slate screen into the clamp jaws.
5. Ensure the screen sits level and is properly seated down into the bottom of each clamp channel.
6. Tighten the screen retaining screws on the clamp to firmly secure the acoustic screen in place.
