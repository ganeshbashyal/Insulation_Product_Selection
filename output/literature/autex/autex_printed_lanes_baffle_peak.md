# Autex Printed Lanes Baffle Peak

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Printed Lanes Baffle Peak combines the sculptural 3D angled peak profile of Lanes with high-definition digital UV printing. Available in realistic timber grains (Acoustic Timber) and textured stone finishes, it replicates heavy architectural materials without the weight, cost, or fire hazards. Its integrated rear air gap delivers enhanced low-frequency sound absorption (NRC 0.80), while its glue-free clipping system ensures clean, straightforward wall installation.

## Key features

- Sculptural 3D peak profile with realistic digitally printed woodgrain or stone finishes.
- Digitally printed with safe, water-based UV-cured ink without reducing acoustic absorption.
- Rear air gap cavity enhances low and mid frequency sound absorption (NRC 0.80).
- Glue-free mechanical clipping system allows fast, clean, and mess-free installation.
- Lightweight alternative to real timber battens or heavy stone fluting.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre with a minimum of 60% to 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating to AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Modular 300mm width sections available in 2400mm and 2700mm lengths.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Moisture and mould resistant (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Select print finish from Acoustic Timber woodgrains or Surface Finishes stone palettes.
2. Confirm wall height to choose 2400mm or 2700mm lengths.
3. Calculate wall width in 300mm increments (3 lanes per pack).
4. Confirm Peak ridge profile suitability for design aesthetic.
5. Verify substrate framing supports mechanical clip mounting.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor use only; avoid wet areas or direct water exposure.
- Requires mechanical clip installation; do not glue directly flat to wall.
- Handle carefully with clean gloves to prevent scuffing the printed surface during install.

## Installation overview

1. Check wall substrate is plumb, flat, and dry.
2. Inspect printed grain or pattern orientation across all lanes prior to mounting.
3. Fix horizontal mounting clips or channel track to framing studs at 300mm vertical centres.
4. Snap the printed folded Peak lanes onto mounting clips firmly without adhesive.
5. Align adjacent panels to ensure unbroken woodgrain or stone continuity along joints.
6. Trim bottom or top ends carefully using a fine-toothed saw on the offcut side.
