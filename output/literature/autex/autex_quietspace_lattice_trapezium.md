# Autex Quietspace Lattice - Trapezium

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Quietspace Lattice - Trapezium is a geometric suspended acoustic baffle system featuring clean angular lines that form an elongated diamond-like trapezium lattice. Constructed from 100% polyester fibre using 12mm semi-rigid Cube acoustic fins, it provides an expansive sound-absorbing surface area to control ambient speech and reverberation. Supplied flat-packed with a dedicated 4-point suspension kit, Trapezium delivers Class A acoustic performance (NRC 1.00 / 1.60 - 3.90 Sabins per unit) and Group 1 fire safety for open ceilings.

## Key features

- Angular geometric trapezium form creating an elongated architectural ceiling feature.
- Intersecting lattice fin design provides maximum surface area for sound absorption.
- Class A acoustic performance with NRC 1.00 (1.60 - 3.90 Sabins per unit).
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Assembled dimensions: 1180 mm x 2380 mm x 290 mm depth.
- Supplied flat-packed with complete suspension kit (4 x 1m cables, ceiling mounts, clips).
- Lightweight, structurally stable, and will not warp, sag, or crumble over time.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, and mildew (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor ceiling installation only; not suitable for exterior use or wet environments.
- Non-load bearing decorative baffle; do not suspend luminaires or mechanical services from fins.
- Must be assembled on a clean, protected floor surface to avoid marking the felt.

## Installation overview

1. Unpack Lattice Trapezium components and lay out fins on a clean, soft work surface.
2. Slot intersecting 12mm Cube fins together according to the Trapezium assembly diagram.
3. Insert alloy tube inserts and Velda cable clips into designated locking fin slots.
4. Measure and mark 4 suspension anchor points on the ceiling substrate matching cable positions.
5. Fasten ceiling mounts securely to the structural ceiling or soffit using suitable anchors.
6. Thread 1m galvanised suspension cables into ceiling mounts and adjust to desired hanging height.
7. Hook cables into the Lattice clips and level the assembled Trapezium sculpture using a spirit level.
