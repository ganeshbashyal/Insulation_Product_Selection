# Autex Bespoke Print Quietspace

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Bespoke Print Quietspace is a premium, high-absorption acoustic panel engineered to deliver maximum acoustic performance alongside completely bespoke visual graphics. Built on a 25mm thick, dual-density Quietspace Panel base with a high-density compressed face and cushioned absorber backing, custom designs and murals are digitally UV printed directly onto the surface without compromising sound absorption. Achieving an exceptional NRC of 0.85 and Group 1 fire classification, it is the ultimate acoustic art solution for reverberant interior spaces.

## Key features

- Bespoke digital UV printing on high-performance acoustic substrate.
- 25mm dual-density 100% polyester Quietspace Panel core.
- Absorbs a minimum of 85% of sound energy (NRC 0.85).
- Printed surface has zero adverse effect on acoustic absorption.
- Group 1 fire rating to AS ISO 9705 - 2003.
- Engineered for broadband sound absorption across high and low frequencies.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Contains minimum 58% post-consumer recycled PET.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, and low VOC.
- Lightweight, easy to handle and precision trim on site.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall
- ceiling

**Selection checklist**

1. Verify artwork resolution meets Autex minimum print specifications (300 DPI vector/raster).
2. Confirm total wall/ceiling area and number of sequential panel drops.
3. Confirm acoustic target requires premium broadband absorption (NRC 0.85).
4. Check clearances around light switches, GPOs, and service penetrations.
5. Determine edge treatment (butt-joined or Autex Type C aluminium profile trims).

## Limitations and warnings

- Printed face can show scuffing if subjected to repetitive heavy friction.
- Do not bend or crease panels as this causes permanent fracturing of the high-density face.
- Indoor use only; avoid direct water saturation.

## Installation overview

1. Carry panels vertically upright with two people wearing clean gloves to prevent marks.
2. Ensure substrate is clean, dust-free, and sealed if porous.
3. Verify image layout and numbering across panel sequence prior to application.
4. Acclimatise panels in the installation space for at least 24 hours.
5. Trim edges squarely from the face using a sharp knife and metal straight edge on the offcut.
6. Apply recommended construction adhesive or contact adhesive evenly to the panel back.
7. Mount panel with high-density printed face outward and softer backing against substrate.
8. Apply uniform pressure with a clean roller across the face to activate adhesive bond.
