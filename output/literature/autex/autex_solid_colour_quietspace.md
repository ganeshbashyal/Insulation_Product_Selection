# Autex Solid Colour Quietspace

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Solid Colour Quietspace is a high-performance broadband acoustic panel engineered to deliver maximum acoustic absorption finished in custom solid colours. Built on a dual-density 100% polyester Quietspace Panel core, solid colour finishes—including custom paint matching, CMYK, and Pantone specifications—are digitally applied with safe, water-based UV-cured ink or laminated with solid-colour Vertiface fabric. It absorbs between 85% and 100% of sound energy (NRC 0.85 - 1.00) while seamlessly harmonising with any interior colour scheme.

## Key features

- Limitless solid colour finishes with custom paint, CMYK, and Pantone matching.
- Available across 25mm (NRC 0.85), 50mm (NRC 1.00), 75mm, and 100mm thicknesses.
- High-performance broadband sound absorption with dual-density polyester core.
- Printed surface or fabric overlay has zero negative impact on acoustic performance.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing minimum 45% to 58% recycled PET.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, with low-VOC emissions.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Supplied with factory-applied protective spun-bond film to protect face during install.
- Backed by a 10-year manufacturer's guarantee.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor use only; avoid direct water exposure or extreme humidity.
- Do not expose to naked flames or concentrated heat sources above 80°C.
- Printed solid colours may show slight surface marking if subjected to heavy impact.

## Installation overview

1. Inspect wall or ceiling substrate to ensure it is flat, clean, dry, and dust-free.
2. Acclimatise panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours prior to fitting.
3. Keep protective spun-bond cover on panel face during handling and installation.
4. Apply recommended contact or high-grab MS polymer adhesive evenly across the panel back.
5. Position panel onto substrate, butt joins tightly, and roll firmly with a pressure roller.
6. Peel back protective film corners during painting and remove fully upon completion.
