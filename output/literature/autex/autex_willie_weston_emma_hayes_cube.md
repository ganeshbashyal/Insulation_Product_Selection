# Autex Willie Weston / Emma Hayes Cube

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Willie Weston / Emma Hayes Cube combines high-performance Cube acoustic panels with curated digital artwork from Australian First Nations artists (Willie Weston) and New Zealand landscape designs (Emma Hayes). Manufactured from 100% polyester fibre, the semi-rigid 12mm or 24mm Cube panels are digitally printed using safe, water-based, UV-cured ink that does not compromise sound absorption. Delivering an NRC of 0.45 to 0.70 (and up to 0.80 with an air gap), this collection unites cultural storytelling and tranquil natural motifs with certified Group 1 fire safety.

## Key features

- Features curated First Nations artwork (Willie Weston) and tranquil landscape patterns (Emma Hayes).
- High-definition digital printing using safe, water-based UV-cured ink.
- Engineered on semi-rigid 100% polyester Cube acoustic panels.
- Acoustic performance rating of NRC 0.45 (12mm) to 0.70 (24mm), and up to 0.80 with an air gap.
- Constructed with a minimum of 80% post-consumer recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Provides ongoing royalty income to First Nations artists and art centres for every panel sold.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD and Declare Red List Free.
- Safe, non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, and low-VOC.
- Compatible with Direct-fix, SpinFix, and Peel and Stick installation methods.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor use only; avoid wet areas or direct exposure to running water.
- Printed decorative surface can be scuffed by heavy mechanical abrasion; install in low-to-medium contact zones.
- Panel layout must follow manufacturer pattern guide to maintain artwork alignment.

## Installation overview

1. Confirm substrate is plumb, flat, clean, dry, and dust-free.
2. Acclimatise printed panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours.
3. Inspect panel pattern repeat and ensure sequence alignment across adjacent panels.
4. Handle panels with clean hands or white gloves to prevent marks on the printed artwork.
5. For adhesive direct fix, apply recommended high-grab MS polymer adhesive evenly across panel backing.
6. Alternatively, mount using Autex SpinFix clips or Peel and Stick backing.
7. Butt panel edges tightly together and apply firm, uniform roller pressure across the entire face.
8. Trim perimeter panels carefully from the face using a straight edge and sharp utility knife on the offcut side.
