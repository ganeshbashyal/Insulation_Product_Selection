# Autex Mirage Cube

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Mirage Cube is an intricately textured acoustic panel range designed to add tactile depth and acoustic comfort to interior walls and ceilings. Etched directly into semi-rigid Cube polyester felt using a precision manufacturing process, Mirage features six refined micro-pattern designs—Bouclé, Brutalist, Offset, Rammed Earth, Lapis, and Mortar. Available in 12mm and 24mm thicknesses across standard 2400mm x 1200mm and 2700mm x 1200mm panels, it provides sound absorption ratings from NRC 0.45 to 0.80 and Group 1 fire compliance.

## Key features

- Intricately etched tactile textures: Bouclé, Brutalist, Offset, Rammed Earth, Lapis, Mortar.
- Engineered on semi-rigid 100% polyester Cube acoustic panels.
- Available in 12mm and 24mm thicknesses.
- Acoustic absorption from NRC 0.45 (12mm) to 0.70 (24mm), and up to 0.80 with an air gap.
- Supplied standard with 4mm 45-degree bevelled edges for a seamless joint finish.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Constructed with a minimum of 80% post-consumer recycled PET.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Safe, non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, and low-VOC.
- Compatible with Direct-fix, SpinFix, and Peel and Stick installation methods.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor use only; avoid direct water exposure or extreme humidity.
- Pattern has a directional grain; panels must be installed in uniform orientation.
- Batch-to-batch variations may occur due to fibre blending.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure wall or ceiling substrate is flat, clean, dry, and dust-free.
2. Acclimatise panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours prior to fitting.
3. Inspect panel pattern direction and ensure consistent grain alignment across walls.
4. For adhesive fix, apply recommended high-grab MS polymer or contact adhesive evenly to the panel back.
5. Alternatively, mount using SpinFix clips or Peel and Stick backing where specified.
6. Butt bevelled edges neatly together and apply firm, even pressure with a roller across the entire face.
7. Trim perimeter panels carefully from the face using a straight edge and sharp utility knife.
