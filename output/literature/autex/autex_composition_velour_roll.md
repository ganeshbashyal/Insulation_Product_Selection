# Autex Composition - Velour Roll

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Composition® is a durable, high-performance acoustic wallcovering designed as a paint or wallpaper alternative, made from 100% polyester fibre, ideal for enhancing interior acoustics in creative and learning spaces.

## Key features

- Available in two thicknesses.
- Available in 39 colours.
- Choice of velour or smooth finish.
- Made from 100% polyester fibre.
- Contains not less than 49% recycled material.
- Carbon neutral product.
- Zero carbon manufacturing.
