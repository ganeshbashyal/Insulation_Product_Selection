# Autex Frontier Raft - Trapezoid

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Frontier Raft - Trapezoid is an innovative acoustic ceiling beam system designed for targeted sound absorption in open-plan spaces. Constructed from 100% polyester fibre using scored and folded 12mm Cube material locked with an extruded aluminium channel, Trapezoid creates a lightweight 3D acoustic feature with an internal sound-dampening air gap. It easily fixes directly or suspends from ceilings, delivering high acoustic performance across a wide frequency range without the weight of solid timber or metal beams.

## Key features

- Innovative scored and folded hollow 3D trapezoid profile (200mm wide x 137mm deep).
- Made from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 60% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Sound absorption with internal air cavity achieving high NRC (0.75 - 0.95).
- Lightweight alternative to solid wood or steel ceiling beams.
- Compatible with Frontier channel and mounting clip suspension systems.
- Highly durable material that will not bend, slump, or warp over time.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, and mildew (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).
- Safe, non-toxic, non-allergenic, and low-VOC emissions.
- Backed by a 10-year manufacturer's guarantee.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor ceiling application only; not for external use or wet environments.
- Not load-bearing; cannot support fixtures, lighting, or services.
- Spacing wider than recommended will decrease overall room acoustic absorption.

## Installation overview

1. Plan ceiling layout allowing recommended 300mm spacing between raft centres.
2. Securely install Frontier mounting clips or suspension track to structural ceiling.
3. Slide RAFNEXRT aluminium extrusion into the folded Trapezoid raft top channel.
4. Align Autex mounting clips with channel and turn 45 degrees to temporarily position.
5. Adjust along length and twist clips 90 degrees to lock firmly in place.
6. Fit black extrusion end caps to ends for a neat aesthetic finish.
