# Autex Batt

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex GreenStuf Thermal Batts are 100% polyester insulation segments engineered to provide thermal and acoustic performance in residential and commercial buildings. Manufactured from thermally bonded polyester fibre without chemical binders or added flame retardants, they are completely safe to touch and install without protective gear. Pre-cut to fit common timber and steel framed walls, ceilings, and mid-floor cavities, they will not settle, crumble, or slump over their 50-year durability life.

## Key features

- User-friendly with no protective clothing required.
- 100% polyester fibre with zero chemical binders or resins.
- Non-toxic, non-irritant, and non-allergenic.
- 50-year durability warranty.
- Fire safe, achieving Group 1 fire classification to AS ISO 9705.
- Improves wall sound transmission loss by 5-6 Rw points.
- Moisture resistant with less than 0.03% absorption by weight.
- Naturally resistant to insect and vermin attack.
- Manufactured with recycled PET bottles and 100% recyclable.
- Tested to AS/NZS 4859.1 for thermal performance.

## Applications and selection

- ceiling
- wall

- internal wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm framing centres (450mm centres require 430mm batts; 600mm centres require 580mm batts).
2. Confirm stud depth and cavity thickness (e.g. 70mm, 90mm, or 140mm).
3. Verify target R-value required by NCC climate zone or NatHERS energy report.
4. Confirm acoustic requirements between rooms or floors.
5. Ensure downlight ratings (IC-4 / IC-F) allow continuous insulation coverage.

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not compress batts significantly as this degrades thermal R-value.
- Do not cover non-IC rated light fittings or hot flues without proper clearances.
- Not designed as an exposed decorative lining; must be enclosed behind internal wall/ceiling linings.

## Installation overview

1. Install in accordance with AS 3999 and manufacturer instructions.
2. Friction-fit batts between timber or steel framing members without gaps.
3. Ensure batts are not compressed against linings to maintain full rated thickness.
4. Tear batts across width by hand or cut with a sharp serrated knife if trimming is needed.
5. Maintain minimum 50mm clearance around non-IC rated recessed downlights and flues.
6. In external walls, ensure batts do not protrude past the frame into cavity drainage space.
7. In ceilings, lay batts flat between ceiling joists and cover top plates at perimeters.
