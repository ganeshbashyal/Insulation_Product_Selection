# Autex Quietspace Lattice - Torus

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Quietspace Lattice - Torus is an architectural suspended acoustic baffle sculpture featuring an abstract, circular woven form. Built from a grid of intersecting 12mm semi-rigid Cube acoustic fins, its open geometry covers a greater surface area than traditional parallel baffle systems to effectively absorb echo and reverberation. Supplied flat-packed with a dedicated suspension kit, Torus provides Class A acoustic performance (NRC 1.00 / up to 3.90 Sabins per unit) and Group 1 fire safety for commercial, educational, and hospitality interiors.

## Key features

- Circular abstract woven form (Torus) creating a focal ceiling feature.
- Intersecting grid structure provides high surface area for optimum sound absorption.
- Class A acoustic absorption rating (NRC 1.00 / 1.60 - 3.90 Sabins per unit).
- Made from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Assembled dimensions: 1450 mm x 1450 mm x 293 mm depth.
- Supplied flat-packed with suspension kit (8 x 1m galvanised cables, ceiling mounts, clips).
- Lightweight and structurally stable; will not sag, warp, or crumble.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, and mildew (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor ceiling installation only; not suitable for exterior use or wet environments.
- Non-load bearing decorative baffle; do not suspend luminaires or mechanical services from fins.
- Must be assembled on a clean, soft surface to avoid scuffing the polyester felt.

## Installation overview

1. Unpack Lattice Torus components and lay out fins on a clean, protected surface.
2. Slot intersecting 12mm Cube fins together according to the assembly diagram.
3. Insert alloy tube inserts and Velda cable clips into designated locking fin slots.
4. Measure and mark suspension anchor points on ceiling substrate matching cable positions.
5. Fasten ceiling mounts securely to the structural ceiling or soffit using suitable anchors.
6. Thread 1m galvanised suspension cables into ceiling mounts and adjust to desired height.
7. Hook cables into Lattice clips and level the assembled circular Torus sculpture using a spirit level.
