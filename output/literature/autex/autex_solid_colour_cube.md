# Autex Solid Colour Cube

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Solid Colour Cube is a lightweight, semi-rigid acoustic panel made from 100% compressed polyester felt with solid colour throughout. Designed without the need for edge banding or capping, Cube delivers exceptional design flexibility and can be cut, bevelled, grooved, or pressed into high-performance acoustic features. Available in 12mm and 24mm thicknesses, it achieves sound absorption ratings from NRC 0.45 to 0.80 and complies with NCC Group 1 fire regulations.

## Key features

- Solid colour throughout the core with no edging or capping required.
- Lightweight, semi-rigid compressed polyester felt construction.
- Available in 12mm (NRC 0.45 - 0.70) and 24mm (NRC 0.70 - 0.80) thicknesses.
- Manufactured from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Easily cut, grooved, bevelled, and customised for bespoke wall and ceiling patterns.
- Moisture and mould resistant with an ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating of 0.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, with zero chemical binders or formaldehyde.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Extensive colour palette of 23+ contemporary solid colours.

**Selection checklist**

1. Select panel thickness (12mm for standard wall panelling or 24mm for higher acoustic absorption).

5. Verify substrate flatness and suitability for adhesive or clip fixing.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure wall or ceiling substrate is flat, clean, dry, and dust-free.
2. Acclimatise panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours prior to install.
3. Inspect panels and ensure all sheets in a continuous area are from the same batch.
4. For adhesive direct-fixing, apply recommended high-grab MS polymer or contact adhesive evenly across the panel back.
5. Alternatively, use Autex SpinFix mechanical fixings or Peel and Stick backing where specified.
6. Position panel onto substrate, butt joins tightly together, and apply uniform pressure across the surface with a roller.
7. Trim edges neatly from the face using a straight edge and sharp utility knife.
