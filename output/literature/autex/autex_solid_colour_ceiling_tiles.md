# Autex Solid Colour Ceiling Tiles

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Accent Ceiling Tiles (Solid Colour Ceiling Tiles) are a simple, high-performance acoustic tile designed to drop directly into standard suspended metric ceiling grids. Developed with two functional sides for design flexibility, they feature a smooth classic white face and a vibrant solid colour face selected from the Autex Cube palette. Made from 100% non-woven polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET, they deliver an NRC of 0.85 and Group 1 fire compliance without chipping, cracking, or breaking.

## Key features

- Dual-sided functionality: solid colour face on one side and classic white on the other.
- Engineered to drop directly into standard metric 600mm x 600mm or 1200mm x 600mm ceiling grids.
- Delivers high acoustic sound absorption with NRC 0.85 (Class A).
- Lightweight, durable polyester that will not chip, crack, or shed fibres.
- Supplied with a protective spun-bond cover to prevent soiling during installation.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, and mildew (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Available in over 20 contemporary solid Cube colours.

## Limitations and warnings

- Designed specifically for standard metric suspended ceiling grids.
- Indoor use only; avoid direct water exposure from overhead pipe leaks.
- Batch-to-batch colour variation can occur; do not mix batches in the same space.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure suspended ceiling grid is square, level, and properly supported.
2. Check that all tiles are supplied from a single production batch to avoid colour variation.
3. Tilt tile and gently lift upward through standard metric 600x600 or 1200x600 ceiling grid opening.
4. Select desired face orientation (solid colour facing downward or classic white facing downward).
5. Lower tile squarely onto exposed grid tees without crushing edges.
6. Remove the protective spun-bond cover once the tile is safely seated in the grid.
7. Where perimeter cutting is required, cut neatly using a sharp utility knife and straight edge.
