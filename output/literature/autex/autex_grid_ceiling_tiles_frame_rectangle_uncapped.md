# Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame - Rectangle - Uncapped

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame - Rectangle - Uncapped is a 3D acoustic ceiling tile designed to drop into standard 1200mm x 600mm suspended grid systems. Formed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 60% recycled PET, the Frame style creates an elegant perimeter-coffer border that defines each grid module. The uncapped design leaves the top open to the plenum, allowing ceiling airflow and lighting integration while providing targeted acoustic absorption (NRC 0.25 - 0.35) and Group 1 fire safety.

## Key features

- Drop-in 3D rectangular acoustic tile for standard 1200mm x 600mm ceiling grids.
- Frame style creates a crisp, architectural coffer border aesthetic.
- Uncapped open-top design maintains ceiling plenum airflow and lighting pathways.
- Made from 100% polyester fibre with a minimum of 60% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Sound absorption controlling reverberation and speech chatter (NRC 0.25 - 0.35).
- Lightweight, durable, and will not crack, chip, or warp over time.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, and mildew (ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating: 0).
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Available in a comprehensive palette of solid Cube colourways.

## Limitations and warnings

- Uncapped configuration does not block sightlines into the ceiling plenum.
- Lower NRC compared to capped versions; specify capped if maximum acoustic absorption is required.
- Indoor use only; avoid direct water leaks or wet areas.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure the suspended 1200mm x 600mm ceiling grid is square, level, and properly supported.
2. Check that all tiles are supplied from a single production batch to avoid colour variation.
3. Assemble the Frame tile components according to manufacturer instructions if supplied flat-packed.
4. Lift the assembled uncapped Frame tile through the grid opening at an angle.
5. Lower the tile gently onto the exposed grid flanges, ensuring perimeter notches seat squarely.
6. Where services or sprinklers occur, maintain required building code clearances.
