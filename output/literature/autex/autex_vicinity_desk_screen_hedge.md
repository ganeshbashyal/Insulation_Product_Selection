# Autex Vicinity - Desk Screen - Hedge

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Vicinity Desk Screen - Hedge is a clean, classic rectangular acoustic divider designed to sit along the rear edge of desks in open-plan offices. Featuring smooth rounded corners and clean finished edges without capping, Hedge provides acoustic separation and physical definition between facing workstations. Fixed non-destructively using Vicinity Clamps, it absorbs 1.00 to 1.10 Sabins per unit and delivers Group 1 fire compliance.

## Key features

- Classic linear rectangular screen profile with smooth rounded corners.
- Mounted non-destructively along desk rear edges with Vicinity Workstation Clamps.
- High acoustic absorption rating of 1.00 (1600mm) to 1.10 (1800mm) Sabins per unit.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Solid colour throughout with neat finished edges; no capping or framing required.
- Pinnable self-healing surface for notes and workstation personalisation.
- Clamps made from 100% recycled PET (RePET) or powdercoated zinc.
- Moisture and mould resistant with an ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating of 0.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.

## Applications and selection

- internal wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Select size based on desk width (Hedge 1600 for 1600mm desk; Hedge 1800 for 1800mm desk).
2. Verify desk edge lip allows clamp clearance without fouling desk frame.
3. Choose colour from Autex Cube palette (20+ colours).
4. Confirm pack quantity (supplied in packs of 4 screens).
5. Check that monitor arm mountings do not conflict with screen clamp locations.

## Installation overview

1. Unpack Vicinity Hedge screens and verify components.
2. Insert Vicinity Desk Clamps into the routed notches along the bottom edge of the screen.
3. Position the screen squarely along the back edge of the workstation.
4. Tighten the clamp screws from underneath the desk using an Allen key until firmly locked.
5. Confirm the screen is vertical, level, and securely positioned.
