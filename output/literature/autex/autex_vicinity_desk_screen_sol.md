# Autex Vicinity - Desk Screen - Sol

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Vicinity Desk Screen - Sol is an acoustic workstation divider characterized by an organic curved semi-circular profile. Manufactured from 100% polyester fibre with a minimum of 80% post-consumer recycled PET, Sol attaches non-destructively to workstations using circular-economy Vicinity Workstation Clamps. It provides desk-level speech absorption (0.30 Sabins per 600mm unit) and visual privacy while introducing soft, arched silhouettes into modern office environments.

## Key features

- Distinctive arched curved Sol profile adds soft architectural geometry to desks.
- Lightweight, semi-rigid 12mm or 24mm 100% polyester acoustic screen.
- Constructed with a minimum of 80% post-consumer recycled PET.
- Acoustic rating of 0.30 metric Sabins per unit (600mm Sol screen).
- Fixed non-destructively to desks with Vicinity Workstation Clamps made from 100% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Pinnable, durable, moisture-resistant, and will not warp, slump, or fray.
- Safe, non-toxic, non-allergenic, and free of chemical binders or formaldehyde.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD and Declare Red List Free.
- Available across the full Autex Cube colour palette and custom printed finishes.

## Limitations and warnings

- Designed for interior desk mounting only; not suitable for exterior use.
- Curved profile has lower total surface area compared to full rectangular screens.
- Cannot support mounted accessories or heavy equipment.

## Installation overview

1. Verify desktop thickness is compatible with the clamp jaws.
2. Position Vicinity Workstation Clamps along the desk edge at designated centres.
3. Tighten clamp screws beneath the desk to anchor clamps firmly in position.
4. Slide the curved Sol acoustic panel into the clamp receiver slots.
5. Check that the screen is seated fully down and sits plumb.
6. Tighten the screen retaining screws to lock the Sol screen firmly in place.
