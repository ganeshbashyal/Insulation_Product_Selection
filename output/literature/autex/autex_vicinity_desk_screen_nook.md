# Autex Vicinity - Desk Screen - Nook

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Vicinity Desk Screen - Nook is a comprehensive three-sided acoustic workstation enclosure designed to create an intimate, distraction-free booth around individual desks. Wrapping the back and both sides of a workstation, Nook delivers an outstanding sound absorption rating of 1.55 Sabins per unit to shield workers from peripheral noise and visual motion. Fixed non-destructively with Vicinity Clamps, it is formed from 100% polyester fibre with an 80% recycled PET core and meets Group 1 fire safety standards.

## Key features

- Three-sided wrap-around pod enclosure creating maximum physical and acoustic privacy.
- Delivers high acoustic sound absorption of 1.55 Sabins per unit.
- Non-destructive desk mounting using Vicinity Workstation Clamps.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Solid colour throughout with clean finished edges; no capping required.
- Pinnable self-healing surface on all three internal faces.
- Clamps made from 100% recycled PET (RePET) or powdercoated zinc.
- Moisture and mould resistant with an ASTM G21-15 Growth Rating of 0.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.

## Installation overview

1. Unpack the 3-piece Nook screen set and verify all components.
2. Attach Vicinity Desk Clamps to pre-routed sockets on rear and side panels.
3. Align the three panels around the back and both sides of the desktop.
4. Tighten the clamp mounting bolts from underneath the desk using an Allen key.
5. Ensure all corners fit neatly and the enclosure stands rigidly around the workspace.
