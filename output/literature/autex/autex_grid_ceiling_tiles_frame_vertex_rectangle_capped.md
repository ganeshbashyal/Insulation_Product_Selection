# Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame/Vertex - Rectangle - Capped

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame/Vertex - Rectangle - Capped is a high-performance 3D acoustic ceiling tile designed for standard 1200mm x 600mm suspended grid systems. Available in architectural Frame (coffer) or faceted Vertex styles, this capped version incorporates an integrated solid acoustic backing layer that conceals the ceiling plenum. Made from 100% polyester fibre with at least 60% recycled PET, it delivers enhanced sound absorption (NRC 0.60 - 0.85) and Group 1 fire performance.

## Key features

- Drop-in 3D rectangular acoustic tile for standard 1200mm x 600mm ceiling grids.
- Available in Frame (perimeter coffer) or Vertex (faceted prism) architectural styles.
- Capped design includes an acoustic backing layer that completely conceals the plenum.
- Delivers high acoustic absorption with NRC up to 0.60 - 0.85.
- Made from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 60% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, and free of chemical binders.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Durable, lightweight polyester construction that will not crack, chip, or crumble.
- Extensive selection of solid colourways from the Autex Cube collection.

## Limitations and warnings

- Ensure plenum services (pipes, cables) do not foul the 80-100mm depth of the capped tile.
- Not designed for exterior or high-humidity wet area use.
- Do not install different batch numbers in the same contiguous ceiling area.

## Installation overview

1. Confirm 1200mm x 600mm suspended grid is properly suspended and square.
2. Check all tiles are from the same production batch before starting installation.
3. Assemble tile and ensure the backing cap is securely locked onto the top of the tile structure.
4. Angle the assembled capped tile up through the grid aperture.
5. Carefully lower the tile until its perimeter flanges rest flat on the T-bar grid rails.
6. Ensure uniform orientation and pattern alignment across adjacent ceiling modules.
