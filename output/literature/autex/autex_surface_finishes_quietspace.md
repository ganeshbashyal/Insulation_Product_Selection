# Autex Surface Finishes Quietspace

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Surface Finishes Quietspace combines the broadband acoustic performance of dual-density Quietspace Panel with high-definition digital UV printing. Available in realistic stone, concrete, and textural finishes from the Autex Surface Finishes collection, it replicates heavy, reverberant architectural surfaces without their cost, weight, or rigidity. Printed with safe, water-based inks directly onto 100% polyester fibre, it delivers an NRC of 0.85 to 1.00 and Group 1 fire compliance across walls and ceilings.

## Key features

- Broadband sound absorption achieving NRC 0.85 (25mm) to 1.00 (50mm, 75mm, 100mm).
- Digitally printed with realistic stone, marble, concrete, and textural finishes.
- Dual-density polyester construction with compressed face and cushioned backing.
- Printed with safe, water-based, UV-cured ink without reducing acoustic absorption.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Constructed from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 45% to 58% recycled PET.
- Lightweight, impact-resistant, and seismic-safe alternative to solid stone or precast slabs.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, with low-VOC emissions.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD and Declare Red List Free.
- Backed by a 10-year manufacturer's guarantee.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor use only; avoid wet areas or direct water exposure.
- Do not expose to concentrated heat sources exceeding 80°C.
- Recommended for non-contact areas as printed surface may mark under heavy impact.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure wall or ceiling substrate is flat, clean, dry, and dust-free.
2. Acclimatise panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours prior to fitting.
3. Plan stone or texture pattern alignment across panels before applying adhesive.
4. Apply recommended contact or high-grab MS polymer adhesive evenly across the panel back.
5. Position panel onto substrate, butt joins tightly together, and roll firmly with a pressure roller.
6. Maintain the protective spun-bond film over panel face until all messy construction trades finish.
7. Trim perimeter panels carefully from the face using a straight edge and sharp utility knife on the offcut side.
