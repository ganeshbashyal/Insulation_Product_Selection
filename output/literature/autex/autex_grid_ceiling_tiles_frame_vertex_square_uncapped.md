# Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame/Vertex - Square - Uncapped

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Grid Ceiling Tiles - Frame/Vertex - Square - Uncapped is a modular 595mm x 595mm 3D acoustic ceiling tile designed for standard 600mm x 600mm suspended grid systems. Formed from 100% polyester fibre containing at least 60% recycled PET, it is available in perimeter coffer Frame or faceted Vertex profiles. The uncapped design leaves the tile open to the ceiling plenum above, allowing natural airflow, light diffusion, and targeted acoustic control (NRC 0.15 - 0.35) with Group 1 fire safety.

## Key features

- Modular 3D square acoustic tile for standard 600mm x 600mm ceiling grids.
- Frame (coffer) or Vertex (faceted prism) architectural design options.
- Uncapped open-top design facilitates ceiling plenum airflow and lighting integration.
- Sound absorption helping reduce unwanted reverberation (NRC 0.15 - 0.35).
- Manufactured from 100% polyester fibre with a minimum of 60% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating to AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, non-irritant, and free of chemical binders.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Durable, lightweight polyester construction that will not chip or break.
- Available in 20+ contemporary solid Cube colours.

## Limitations and warnings

- Uncapped design does not conceal pipes, wiring, or ducts in the ceiling plenum.
- Provides lower acoustic absorption than the capped alternative.
- Indoor ceiling use only; avoid wet areas.

## Installation overview

1. Check that the 600mm x 600mm suspended grid is level and square.
2. Verify all tiles are from the same production batch.
3. Assemble the square uncapped Frame or Vertex tile if supplied flat-packed.
4. Tilt the uncapped tile and pass it upward through the grid opening.
5. Lower the tile gently so its edges sit evenly on the T-bar flanges.
6. Ensure aligned orientation across the ceiling grid array.
