# Autex Surface Finishes Frontier Raft beam

**Autex Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Autex Surface Finishes Frontier Raft - Beam (including Acoustic Timber Raft Beam) is an innovative acoustic ceiling beam system that combines the warmth and appearance of real timber or architectural stone with high-performance sound absorption. Built from scored and folded 12mm Cube polyester felt digitally printed with high-resolution woodgrains or stone textures using water-based UV-cured ink, it is available in Beam 100 (87mm high) and Beam 250 (227mm high) profiles. It delivers an NRC of 0.75 to 0.95 and Group 1 fire safety without the weight, cost, or warping of solid timber beams.

## Key features

- Realistic digitally printed timber woodgrains (Acoustic Timber) and stone textures.
- Available in Beam 100 (87mm depth) and Beam 250 (227mm depth) profiles.
- Internal sound-dampening air gap enhances acoustic performance (NRC 0.75 - 0.95).
- Printed with safe, water-based, UV-cured ink that does not compromise acoustics.
- Lightweight, seismic-safe alternative to solid wood or steel ceiling beams.
- Made from 100% polyester fibre containing a minimum of 60% to 80% recycled PET.
- Group 1 fire rating compliant with AS ISO 9705 - 2003 and AS 5637.1.
- Compatible with Frontier mounting clips and adjustable suspension systems.
- Will not warp, crack, splinter, or slump over time.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor ceiling application only; not for external use or wet environments.
- Non-load-bearing beam; cannot support luminaires or heavy ceiling services directly.
- Handle with clean hands or white gloves during installation to prevent marking the printed surface.

## Installation overview

1. Plan ceiling layout allowing recommended spacing (150mm centres for Beam 100; 300mm centres for Beam 250).
2. Install Frontier mounting clips or suspension track securely to structural ceiling.
3. Slide RAFNEXRT aluminium extrusion into the folded Beam raft top channel.
4. Align Autex mounting clips with channel and turn 45 degrees to temporarily position.
5. Adjust along length, check grain alignment, and twist clips 90 degrees to lock firmly in place.
6. Fit black extrusion end caps to beam ends for a clean architectural finish.
