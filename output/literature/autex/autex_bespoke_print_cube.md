# Autex Bespoke Print Cube

**Autex Panel** — acoustic panels for sound absorption and interior finish.

Autex Bespoke Print Cube is a fully customisable acoustic wall and ceiling panel that combines the acoustic performance of Cube solid polyester felt with bespoke digital printing. High-resolution imagery, brand graphics, patterns, or custom artwork are printed directly onto 12mm or 24mm Cube panels using safe, water-based, UV-cured inks. It delivers an authentic, high-definition visual finish with an NRC of 0.45 to 0.70 while maintaining Group 1 fire compliance and carbon-neutral credentials.

## Key features

- Full customisation with high-resolution digital UV printing.
- Substrate is semi-rigid 100% polyester Cube panel.
- Available in 12mm and 24mm thicknesses.
- Printed surface has zero negative impact on acoustic sound absorption.
- Group 1 fire rating to AS ISO 9705 - 2003.
- Printed using safe, water-based, UV-cured inks.
- Minimum 80% recycled post-consumer PET fibre content.
- Zero chemical binders and low VOC emissions.
- Certified Carbon Neutral product with third-party verified EPD.
- Direct-fix, SpinFix, or Peel 'n' Stick installation options.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall
- ceiling

**Selection checklist**

1. Provide high-resolution artwork (minimum 300 DPI at 1:1 scale or vector format).
2. Confirm panel thickness (12mm for standard wall art vs 24mm for higher NRC 0.70 absorption).
3. Confirm total wall dimensions and required panel seam alignments.
4. Choose mounting system (direct adhesive fix vs SpinFix demountable fixings).
5. Ensure area is non-contact or low-impact to protect print quality.

## Limitations and warnings

- Printed face can show scuffing or surface damage if subjected to hard mechanical contact.
- Not recommended for high-impact sports zones or heavy corridor traffic without protection.
- Ensure high-resolution artwork meets Autex print guidelines to prevent pixelation.
- For interior applications only.

## Installation overview

1. Review the Bespoke Print layout diagram before handling or cutting panels.
2. Clean and prepare the substrate ensuring it is flat, dry, and dust-free.
3. Acclimatise panels in the installation environment for at least 24 hours prior to install.
4. Handle with clean cloth gloves to prevent soiling the printed decorative surface.
5. Use a sharp utility knife and straight edge on the offcut side when trimming.
6. Apply construction adhesive evenly across the panel back or use SpinFix mounting hardware.
7. Align printed pattern or imagery seams carefully across adjacent panels.
8. Press firmly with a clean roller across the surface to secure adhesive contact.
