# Sisalation Tuff Wrap and Multipurpose Sarking

**Fletcher Reflective membrane** — reflective membrane insulation product.

Sisalation Tuff Wrap and Multipurpose Sarking (Sisalation 497 Tuff Wrap Standard / Breather and Multipurpose EHD 456) is a renowned range of heavy-duty reflective foil building membranes designed to protect homes and buildings against radiant heat, drafts, dust, and moisture. Manufactured with woven polymer (polyweave) fabric laminated to highly reflective pure aluminium foil with flame-retardant adhesives, it delivers exceptional tear and puncture resistance during installation on exposed jobsites. When installed adjacent to an air cavity, it blocks up to 95% of radiant heat, contributing to total building R-values.

## Key features

- High-strength woven polyweave reinforcement delivering exceptional puncture and tear resistance.
- Reflects up to 95% of radiant heat when installed facing a minimum 20mm air space.
- Available in Standard (Class 1/2 Vapour Barrier) and Breather (pin-perforated) options.
- Extra Heavy Duty (EHD 456) and Medium Duty (497) classifications to AS/NZS 4200.1.
- Water barrier protection shielding timber and steel frames from weather penetration.
- Effectively seals building envelopes against drafts and dust ingress.
- Low flammability index compliant with AS 1530.2.
- Suitable for bushfire-prone zones up to BAL-40 in roof applications and BAL-FZ in walls.
- Durable foil laminate that will not shrink, delaminate, or crack over time.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- external wall
- roof
- roofline
- shed

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application (wall wrap vs roof sarking vs shed lining).
2. Choose between Standard (vapour barrier for climate zones 1-3) and Breather (perforated).
3. Select duty rating (Medium Duty 497 vs Extra Heavy Duty 456 for high wind/roof traffic).
4. Measure total area allowing 150mm overlaps (40.5m² or 81m² rolls).
5. Order matching Vapastop 883 foil tape.

## Limitations and warnings

- Standard 497 is not suitable in cold climate zones where vapour-permeable Class 4 wraps are required.
- Breather 497 is not suitable where a strict water barrier is required under lightweight cladding in high rainfall.
- Must not be left exposed to direct sunlight beyond recommended exposure window.

## Installation overview

1. Install horizontally on exterior wall studs or over roof rafters with anti-glare face facing outward.
2. Overlap upper layers over lower layers by minimum 150mm to ensure water shedding.
3. Secure to timber frame using broad-headed galvanized clouts or staples; use self-drilling screws for steel.
4. Fix at maximum 300mm centres to studs and plates to withstand wind pressure.
5. Ensure a minimum 20mm air cavity is maintained adjacent to the reflective face to achieve rated thermal R-values.
6. Seal all overlaps, edges, and penetrations with Vapastop 883 aluminium foil tape.
7. Ensure wrap is protected by external cladding within manufacturer UV exposure limits.
