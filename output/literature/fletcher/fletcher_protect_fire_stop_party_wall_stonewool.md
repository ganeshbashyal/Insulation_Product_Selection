# Fletcher Protect / Fire Stop Party Wall Stonewool

**Fletcher Fire and acoustic** — fire and acoustic insulation product.

Fletcher Protect Party Wall Stonewool Batts and Blankets are non-combustible mineral wool insulation products engineered to provide passive fire protection in party and separating wall systems between adjacent tenancies in NCC Class 1 and Class 10 buildings. Manufactured from molten volcanic rock (basalt) and recycled blast furnace slag, they provide exceptional resistance to shrinkage under extreme fire temperatures up to 1000°C. They are designed to compress-fill vertical joints, service penetrations, and top-of-wall junctions beneath roof membranes to prevent flame and hot gas bypass.

## Key features

- Non-combustible stonewool material compliant with AS 1530.1.
- Engineered specifically for NCC Class 1 inter-tenancy separating party walls.
- High resistance to shrinkage at temperatures exceeding 1000°C.
- Provides fire protection, acoustic noise dampening, and thermal insulation.
- Available as uncompressed batts (100mm x 168mm x 1200mm) or foil-faced blankets.
- Compress-fits into joints with minimum 20% compression to ensure tight void-free seal.
- Non-hygroscopic, water repellent, and will not promote rot or fungal growth.
- Bio-soluble mineral wool fibres safe to handle and install.
- Made from abundant basalt rock and recycled blast-furnace slag.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall
- roof
- roofline

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application is Class 1 separating wall (townhouse/duplex party wall).
2. Verify gap width to calculate required thickness allowing 20% compression.
3. Choose batts for vertical joint cavities or foil-faced blanket for top-of-wall roof junctions.
4. Determine linear meterage needed along party wall lines.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be installed with at least 20% compression to ensure fire seal integrity.
- Not designed as primary weatherproofing; must be sheltered by roof cladding or wall linings.
- Protect from continuous pooling water during construction.

## Installation overview

1. Measure the vertical cavity width or top-of-wall gap between the separating wall and roof cladding.
2. Select batts or blanket ensuring thickness allows a minimum of 20% compression when installed.
3. For vertical party wall cavities, friction-fit 100mm x 168mm batts tightly between wall leaves.
4. Butt consecutive batts tightly with Vee cuts at joining ends to form continuous fire seal without voids.
5. For top-of-wall junctions, lay blanket along the top of party wall framing beneath roof battens and cladding.
6. Fasten roof sheeting down over blanket ensuring minimum 20% compression beneath corrugations.
7. Pack stonewool firmly around all pipe penetrations and services crossing the party wall line.
