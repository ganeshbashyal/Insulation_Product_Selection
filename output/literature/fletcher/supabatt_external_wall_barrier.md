# supaBATT External Wall Barrier

**Fletcher External wall board** — external wall board insulation product.

supaBATT is a high-density, rigid glasswool external wall insulation board engineered specifically to enhance the thermal performance of brick veneer walls in response to NCC 2022 7-star energy efficiency standards. Available unfaced or pre-faced with a Class 4 vapour-permeable membrane (supaBATT VP4), it is installed externally onto timber or steel studs before brickwork is laid. By insulating over the exterior face of framing, it eliminates thermal bridging, controls moisture vapour transfer, and boosts total wall system R-values.

## Key features

- Rigid, high-density glasswool board designed for external face-of-stud wall insulation.
- Developed specifically to achieve 7-star energy ratings in residential brick veneer construction.
- Available unfaced or pre-faced with a Class 4 vapour-permeable membrane (VP4).
- Pre-faced VP4 version combines bulk insulation and weather wrap in a single installation step.
- Eliminates thermal bridging across timber and light-gauge steel framing studs.
- Non-combustible glasswool substrate complying with AS 1530.1.
- Manufactured in Australia using up to 80% recycled glass with bio-soluble FBS-1 formulation.
- Low-itch, safe to handle, and will not slump or sag over time.
- Backed by Fletcher Insulation Consumer Lifetime Warranty.

## Applications and selection

- wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm brick veneer cavity width accommodates supaBATT thickness while maintaining 40-50mm masonry air cavity.
2. Choose between supaBATT plain (requires separate wrap) or supaBATT VP4 (integrated Class 4 wrap).
3. Verify required R-value to achieve 7-star NatHERS rating in conjunction with internal wall batts.
4. Order compatible face-mount brick ties and seaming tape.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be installed on external face of stud framing behind masonry or cladding with a drained cavity.
- Cladding/brickwork should be installed without prolonged delay to prevent weather damage.
- Must not be compressed into internal stud bays designed for soft batts.

## Installation overview

1. Fix supaBATT boards horizontally or vertically to the outside face of timber or steel stud framing.
2. Secure boards mechanically with approved insulation fixings or brick tie brackets into framing.
3. For supaBATT VP4, ensure the vapour-permeable membrane faces outward towards the brick cavity.
4. Overlap the integrated membrane flap of upper rows over lower rows and tape with 3M Seaming Tape / VapaSeam Tape.
5. Where unfaced supaBATT is used, butt boards tightly together and install an approved external wall wrap over the face.
6. Tape and seal all cuts, penetrations, and corners to maintain air and water barrier integrity.
7. Fasten brick ties through supaBATT into structural studs, maintaining required cavity clearance to brickwork.
