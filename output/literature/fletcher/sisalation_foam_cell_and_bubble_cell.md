# Sisalation Foam Cell and Bubble Cell

**Fletcher Reflective composite** — reflective composite insulation product.

Sisalation Foam Cell and Bubble Cell is an advanced 3-in-1 reflective composite insulation range providing thermal insulation, a thermal break, and a vapour barrier. Available as Foam Cell Multipurpose (8.4mm cross-linked closed-cell foam core) or Bubble Cell (4.0mm polyethylene bubble core) laminated between reflective aluminium foil layers with an anti-glare coating, it reflects up to 95% of radiant heat. Engineered for steel and timber-framed roofs and walls in residential, commercial, shed, and double-brick applications, it features a 150mm foil overlap flap for seamless jointing and complies with NCC thermal break requirements.

## Key features

- 3-in-1 multipurpose solution: thermal insulation, thermal break, and vapour barrier.
- Cross-linked closed-cell foam core (Foam Cell 8.4mm) or sealed polyethylene bubble core (Bubble Cell 4mm).
- Reflects up to 95% of radiant heat, providing cooler interior conditions in hot climates.
- Delivers in-situ material thermal break (R0.20 to R0.25) to satisfy NCC steel framing requirements.
- Integrated 150mm foil flap on one edge to maximize coverage and minimize taping.
- Group 2 fire hazard property rating (Foam Cell Multipurpose) for commercial wall/ceiling linings.
- Low Flammability Index compliant with AS 1530.2; suitable for BAL 0-40 in roofs and BAL 0-FZ in walls per AS 3959.
- Anti-glare coating on outward-facing surface for safe installation in direct sunlight.
- High moisture and condensation resistance with Class 2 vapour barrier rating.
- Extra Heavy Duty rating providing superior strength and tear resistance.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm requirement for thermal break (Foam Cell 8.4mm delivers R0.20 to R0.25 on steel studs).
2. Verify if building class 2-9 requires a Group 2 fire hazard rating (select Foam Cell Multipurpose).
3. Choose between foam core (Foam Cell) and bubble core (Bubble Cell) based on specification.
4. Calculate roll quantities (each roll covers 30m² inclusive of the 150mm overlap).
5. Order matching Vapastop 883 foil tape.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be kept dry prior to and during installation.
- Do not use in direct contact with wet concrete or alkaline mortar to prevent foil corrosion.
- Not suitable for applications subject to continuous water immersion.

## Installation overview

1. Roll out over roof purlins or wall framing with anti-glare face oriented towards the installer/outward.
2. Align roll edges so the 150mm factory foil flap overlaps the adjoining foam roll.
3. Secure to framing using mechanical fasteners or wafer-head screws with washers.
4. Ensure a minimum 20mm air cavity exists adjacent to the reflective face to maximize system R-values.
5. Tape all overlapping seams and penetrations with Vapastop 883 aluminium foil tape for continuous air/vapour seal.
6. For steel-framed walls, fix cladding directly through the foam cell to create a compliant R0.20+ thermal break.
7. At roof eaves, ensure membrane drains freely into the gutter without pooling.
