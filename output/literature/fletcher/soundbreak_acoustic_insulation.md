# Soundbreak Acoustic Insulation

**Fletcher Acoustic bulk** — acoustic bulk insulation product.

Fletcher Soundbreak is a range of high-density, flexible and resilient glasswool batts designed to provide acoustic absorption within framed residential walls, ceilings and midfloors. The batts also provide documented material thermal resistance. Soundbreak works primarily as porous absorptive insulation within a cavity.
