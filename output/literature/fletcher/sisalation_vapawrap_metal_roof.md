# Sisalation Vapawrap Metal Roof

**Fletcher Roof membrane** — roof membrane insulation product.

Sisalation Vapawrap Vapour Permeable Metal Roof is a synthetic, Class 4 vapour-permeable roof sarking membrane specifically engineered for use under residential and commercial metal roof claddings. Formulated with a microporous water-resistant film sandwiched between two layers of mould- and shrink-resistant spun-bonded polyolefin, it provides superior heat shrink resistance compared to traditional polymer-weave membranes under hot metal decks. It allows water vapour to escape from roof cavities while functioning as an air and liquid water barrier that safely drains condensation into gutters.

## Key features

- Specifically designed for use under metal roof claddings in residential and commercial buildings.
- High heat shrink resistance, preventing thermal degradation and stretching under hot metal roofs.
- Class 4 Vapour Permeable membrane preventing interstitial condensation accumulation.
- Acts as a continuous water barrier directing leaks and condensation into the gutter.
- Triple-layer construction: microporous core between spun-bonded polyolefin layers.
- High tensile strength reduces tearing and scuffing during metal roofing installation.
- Resists mould, mildew, and biological attack.
- Low flammability index compliant with AS 1530.2.
- Suitable for bushfire-prone areas up to BAL-40 under AS 3959.

## Limitations and warnings

- Designed specifically for metal roof applications; do not use as a primary roof cladding.
- Must not be left exposed to direct UV sunlight beyond manufacturer recommended exposure time.
- Ensure membrane has continuous fall to gutters without reverse laps or ponding.

## Installation overview

1. Install as a continuous membrane over rafters/trusses beneath metal roof battens, or draped between battens.
2. Roll out horizontally starting from the eaves, ensuring lower edge drains directly into the gutter.
3. Allow a minimum 150mm overlap for horizontal laps (or 50mm if fully taped with VapaSeam tape).
4. Allow a nominal 40mm drape between framing members to create a drainage channel for condensation.
5. Fix temporarily with staples or clouts to rafters, then secure permanently with roof battens.
6. Tape all laps, penetrations, and ridgelines with Sisalation VapaSeam Tape for complete airtightness.
7. Fix metal roof cladding directly to battens above the membrane.
