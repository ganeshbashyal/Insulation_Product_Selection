# Sisalation Vapawrap Metal Roof

**Fletcher Roof membrane** — roof membrane insulation product.

This is Fletcher's vapour-permeable membrane family for suitable metal-roof assemblies. Its function is moisture/water management within a designed roof, not bulk thermal insulation and not Permastop glasswool blanket. Roof slope, support, drainage, ventilation, climate and condensation analysis determine suitability.
