# Pink Partition Insulation

**Fletcher Commercial acoustic and thermal** — commercial acoustic and thermal insulation product.

Fletcher Pink Partition insulation is a lightweight to medium-density glasswool insulation batt specifically designed for commercial steel and timber framed partition walls and ceilings. Manufactured from FBS-1 bio-soluble glasswool with up to 80% recycled glass content, it delivers tailored acoustic attenuation and thermal comfort across low- and high-rise commercial fitouts, offices, healthcare, aged care, and multi-residential projects. With densities ranging from 11 kg/m³ to 32 kg/m³ and thicknesses up to 165mm (R1.2 to R3.5), it is non-combustible and maintains cavity firmness to prevent slumping.

## Key features

- Engineered specifically for commercial steel and timber stud partition walls and ceilings.
- Wide range of densities available: 11 kg/m³, 14 kg/m³, 24 kg/m³, and 32 kg/m³.
- Non-combustible material when tested in accordance with AS 1530.1.
- Reduces background noise and airborne sound transmission between offices and tenancies.
- Thermal insulation performance with R-values ranging from R1.2 up to R3.5.
- Firm friction fit ensures insulation remains in position without slumping over time.
- Manufactured from up to 80% recycled glass with bio-soluble FBS-1 formulation.
- CodeMark certified (Certificate of Conformity CM 30006) for NCC compliance.
- Suitable for healthcare, aged care, education, office, and transport hub fitouts.
- Backed by Fletcher Insulation 50-year commercial warranty.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall
- ceiling
- between floors

**Selection checklist**

1. Identify target acoustic specification (Rw / STC) and select density (11, 14, 24, or 32 kg/m³).
2. Confirm steel stud depth (50mm, 64mm, 75mm, 92mm, 150mm).
3. Confirm stud centres (450mm or 600mm).
4. Check fire rating requirements (AS 1530.1 non-combustible compliance).
5. Order matching pack quantities based on area calculations.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure framing studs are erected and cavity spaces are dry and clear of debris.
2. Select batt dimensions matching stud width (450mm or 600mm).
3. Friction-fit Pink Partition batts snugly between steel or timber studs.
4. Ensure batts butt tightly against top and bottom tracks and adjacent batts with zero gaps.
5. Cut neatly around electrical conduits, plumbing, and wall penetrations using a serrated knife.
6. Maintain batt thickness to match cavity depth without crushing or over-compressing.
7. Fasten plasterboard or wall lining according to fire and acoustic system specifications.
