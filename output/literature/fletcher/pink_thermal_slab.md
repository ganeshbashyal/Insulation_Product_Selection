# Pink Thermal Slab

**Fletcher Commercial board** — commercial board insulation product.

Pink Thermal Slab is a high-performance commercial insulation board consisting of a semi-rigid glasswool slab factory-laminated with a Sisalation reflective foil laminate on one side. Engineered for commercial concrete under-slab soffits, concrete roofs, floors, and plant room walls, it controls temperature fluctuations and dampens structural and airborne noise. Achieving an AS 5637.1 Group 1 NCC fire classification for ceiling linings and CodeMark certification, it satisfies thermal performance, acoustic absorption, and strict non-combustibility requirements.

## Key features

- High-density semi-rigid glasswool board combined with Sisalation reflective foil facing.
- Group 1 fire classification compliant with AS 5637.1 and AS ISO 9705 for wall and ceiling linings.
- CodeMark certified (Certificate of Conformity CM 30006) for NCC compliance.
- Delivers superior thermal performance to stabilize concrete roof, floor, and wall temperatures.
- Exceptional acoustic sound absorption reducing reverberation under concrete slabs.
- Non-combustible glasswool core complying with AS 1530.1.
- Manufactured in Australia using up to 80% recycled glass with bio-soluble FBS-1 formulation.
- Lightweight and easy to cut and install using insulation anchors or pins.
- Low-VOC emissions supporting Green Star indoor air quality requirements.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm NCC Section J target Total R-value for the carpark, basement, or suspended slab.
2. Verify Group 1 fire rating requirement for exposed ceiling/soffit linings.
3. Measure slab area to calculate board quantities (each 1200x2400mm board covers 2.88 m²).
4. Select correct thickness based on required thermal R-value (50mm R1.5 to 110mm R3.5).
5. Order compatible insulation anchors and 72mm reinforced foil tape.

## Limitations and warnings

- Not designed for external weather exposure without protective cladding.
- Must be mechanically fixed; adhesive alone is not recommended for overhead soffit installations.
- Avoid puncturing the foil facing during maintenance to preserve the vapour barrier.

## Installation overview

1. Clean and inspect underside of cured concrete slab, ensuring surface is dry and free of release oils.
2. Mark grid lines on soffit to align board joints neatly across the ceiling.
3. Position Pink Thermal Slab with foil facing downward (exposed) against the concrete soffit.
4. Fasten mechanically using approved insulation anchors with large retaining washers (minimum 5 to 6 fixings per board).
5. Locate fixings approximately 100mm from edges and evenly distributed across the board center.
6. Butt boards tightly together to eliminate thermal bridging.
7. Tape all exposed foil butt joints with 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape to form a continuous vapour barrier.
8. Neatly cut boards around service pipes, sprinklers, and penetrations using a sharp serrated knife.
