# Permastop Building Blanket

**Fletcher Roof and wall blanket** — roof and wall blanket insulation product.

Permastop Building Blanket is a reflective glasswool insulation blanket factory-laminated with Sisalation reflective foil facing, designed for thermal performance, condensation control, and acoustic absorption under residential and commercial metal roofs and in external walls. Composed of bio-soluble glasswool bonded to light, medium, or heavy-duty aluminium foil with an anti-glare finish, it reflects radiant heat while dramatically reducing rain impact drumming noise. Available in R-values from R1.3 to R3.6 (including an industry-leading R3.6 in 130mm), it satisfies NCC requirements and bushfire ratings up to BAL-40.

## Key features

- Factory-laminated with Sisalation reflective foil facing with 150mm overlap flap.
- High thermal performance with R-values from R1.3 up to R3.6 (130mm).
- Outstanding acoustic absorption significantly reducing rain noise drumming on metal roofs.
- Controls condensation formation on the underside of metal roof sheeting.
- Non-combustible glasswool core complying with AS 1530.1.
- Suitable for bushfire-prone areas with BAL Low-40 ratings in roof systems per AS 3959.
- Manufactured in Australia from up to 80% recycled glass.
- Features an anti-glare coating on one side for installer safety.
- Bio-soluble safe-to-use FBS-1 formulation with low VOC emissions.
- Compatible with Fletcher Roof Razor system to minimize thermal bridging.

## Applications and selection

- roofline

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm roof type (pitched or curved metal deck roof, shed, or commercial warehouse).
2. Select required R-value (R1.3 to R3.6) to meet NCC roof energy compliance.
3. Choose foil duty rating (Light, Medium, or Heavy Duty) based on span and traffic.
4. Calculate total roof area including 150mm overlap allowance.
5. Order matching 72mm reinforced foil tape and safety wire mesh.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must not be left exposed to rain or weather prior to roof sheet installation.
- Foil lap joints must be taped continuously to maintain vapour barrier performance.
- For tropical high-humidity climates, specify Permastop Tropic with outward-facing foil.

## Installation overview

1. Roll out Permastop over roof framing purlins or safety wire mesh with reflective foil facing downward towards building interior.
2. Ensure 150mm factory overlap flap extends over the adjacent roll.
3. Tape all foil lap joints securely with 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape to form a continuous vapour barrier.
4. Allow a nominal 40mm sag between purlins to promote condensation drainage towards the eaves if unmeshed.
5. Fasten metal roof sheeting directly over the glasswool blanket through to purlins.
6. Ensure blanket terminates neatly at the gutter line without obstructing stormwater flow.
7. Use Roof Razor spacers in commercial roofs to avoid blanket compression over purlins.
