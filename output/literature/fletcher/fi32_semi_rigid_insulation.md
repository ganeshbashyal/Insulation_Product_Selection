# FI32 Semi-Rigid Insulation

**Fletcher HVAC and industrial** — hvac and industrial insulation product.

FI32 Semi-Rigid Insulation is a versatile glasswool board and roll insulation engineered for thermal and acoustic performance in HVAC and industrial systems. Designed for internal lining of sheet metal air conditioning ductwork, it is also widely used on storage tanks, process vessels, plant rooms, and acoustic baffles. Manufactured from up to 80% recycled glass with an FBS-1 bio-soluble formulation, it is available unfaced or factory-laminated with Sisalation Heavy Duty Perforated foil, Black Matt Facing, or Vapastop 883.

## Key features

- Nominal density of 32 kg/m³ providing superior structural rigidity and acoustic absorption.
- Excellent sound absorption with NRC values up to 1.00 in ductwork and plant rooms.
- Thermal conductivity of 0.032 W/m·K at 23°C.
- Available unfaced or with Vapastop 883, Sisalation HDP foil, or Black Matt Facing.
- Non-combustible glasswool substrate compliant with AS 1530.1.
- Manufactured in Australia from up to 80% recycled glass.
- Accommodates flat and curved surfaces on tanks and vessels.
- Certified under CodeMark Certificate of Conformity CM 30006.
- Biosoluble safe-to-use FBS-1 formulation with low VOC emissions.

## Applications and selection

- duct
- pipe
- wall
- internal wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application (internal duct lining vs tank/vessel lagging vs plant room acoustic wall).
2. Select facing material (unfaced, Sisalation HDP perforated foil, Black Matt Facing, or Vapastop 883).
3. Determine required R-value and thickness (25mm R0.71 up to 100mm R3.0) per AS 4254 / NCC energy tables.
4. Choose sheet or roll format based on duct dimensions and fabrication machinery.

## Limitations and warnings

- Maximum continuous operating temperature for facing materials is 120°C (unfaced glasswool operates up to 340°C).
- Black Matt Facing (BMF) is unsuitable for mechanical brush cleaning inside ducts.
- Not designed for external weather exposure without protective sheet metal cladding.

## Installation overview

1. Clean internal sheet metal duct or vessel surface ensuring it is free of oil and dust.
2. Cut FI32 sheets or rolls accurately to fit internal duct dimensions using a sharp insulation knife.
3. Apply approved water-based or solvent-based duct liner adhesive covering 100% of the metal surface.
4. Press FI32 firmly into the wet adhesive with facing material exposed to the air stream.
5. Fasten with mechanical weld pins or grip nails at recommended spacings per AS 4254.
6. Seal all leading edges, transverse joints, and longitudinal seams with Vapastop 883 tape or edge-seal mastic.
7. For curved vessels or tanks, wrap sheets or rolls tightly and secure with metal banding straps.
