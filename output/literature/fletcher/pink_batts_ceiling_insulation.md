# Pink Batts Ceiling Insulation

**Fletcher Bulk** — bulk insulation product.

Pink® Batts are made from lightweight, flexible and resilient glasswool for thermal and acoustic insulation of residential ceilings. Pink® Batts are available in various sizes to fit standard timber and steel joist spacings.

## Key features

- Lightweight.
- Flexible.
- Resilient.
- Thermal insulation.
- Acoustic insulation.
- Available in various sizes.
- Fit standard timber and steel joist spacings.

## Applications and selection

- ceiling
- new homes
- retrofitting existing buildings
