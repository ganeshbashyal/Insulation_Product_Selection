# Pink Batts Ceiling Insulation

**Fletcher Bulk** — bulk insulation product.

Pink® Batts are lightweight, flexible, and resilient glasswool batts designed for thermal and acoustic insulation of residential ceilings.

## Key features

- Made from lightweight, flexible, and resilient glasswool.
- Available in various sizes to fit standard timber and steel joist spacings.
- Low itch.
- Easy to cut.
- Friction fit standard joists.
- Provides energy savings all year round.
- Renowned for their firmness.

## Applications and selection

- Residential ceiling applications

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm the application (wall, ceiling, floor, roof, pipe or service) matches the family.
2. Confirm the target rating and construction build-up with the project team.
3. Confirm available cavity or fixing depth against the product dimensions.
4. Check NCC, fire, BAL or acoustic requirements with a qualified reviewer before specifying.
5. Record the suburb/postcode so climate-zone requirements can be checked.
