# Knauf Batt

**Knauf Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Earthwool Wall and Ceiling Batts are high-performance glass mineral wool insulation batts manufactured by Knauf Insulation using ECOSE Technology. Engineered with a bio-based binder containing no added formaldehyde, phenol, or acrylics, Earthwool batts are soft to handle, low-dust, and non-combustible. Featuring TwinTech dual-forming manufacturing for a smooth finish on both sides, they improve the thermal comfort and energy efficiency of residential and commercial buildings while absorbing airborne acoustic sound.

## Key features

- ECOSE Technology bio-based binder with no added formaldehyde.
- TwinTech dual-forming technique ensures a smooth finish on both sides.
- Non-combustible when tested in accordance with AS 1530.1.
- Sound absorbing, reducing external traffic and internal room-to-room noise.
- Soft to handle, easy to install, and significantly less itchy than traditional glasswool.
- Manufactured using up to 80% recycled glass.
- Compression packed into robust poly packs for easier transport and handling.
- Low VOC emissions and Eurofins Indoor Air Comfort Gold certified.
- Certified under BRANZ Appraisal No. 873.
- Backed by a 50-year product warranty.

## Applications and selection

- ceiling
- wall
- external wall
- internal wall
- between floors

**Selection checklist**

1. Determine application (ceiling vs external walls vs internal acoustic walls).
2. Select required R-value to achieve 7-star NatHERS energy compliance for climate zone.
3. Measure framing centers (430mm for 450mm centers; 580mm for 600mm centers).
4. Check wall cavity depth (75mm for 70mm studs; 90mm for 90mm studs).
5. Verify downlight ratings (IC/IC-4 rated allows continuous insulation coverage per AS 3999).

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not compress batts into cavities narrower than nominal thickness as this reduces thermal R-value.
- Must be protected from continuous water exposure and outdoor weather.
- Not designed as an exposed decorative lining; must be enclosed behind plasterboard or internal linings.

## Installation overview

1. Isolate electrical power at the main switchboard before entering ceiling spaces or touching wall cavities.
2. Friction fit batts snugly between timber or steel framing members without gaps or folds.
3. In ceilings, lay batts flat between ceiling joists ensuring complete, continuous coverage over plasterboard.
4. At roof eaves, maintain minimum 20mm clearance between insulation and roof cladding/sarking.
5. Maintain minimum 50mm clearance around non-IC rated recessed downlights and heat flues as per AS 3999.
6. In walls, ensure batts butt tightly against top and bottom plates and fill the cavity without bulging plasterboard.
7. Cut neatly around electrical boxes, plumbing, and wiring using a sharp utility knife.
