# Stinger Ch

**Stinger Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

The STINGER CH38-2 is an award-winning, non-pneumatic cap hammer tacker engineered to deliver the superior holding power of a plastic cap combined with the speed and simplicity of a manual hammer tacker. Specifically designed for securing housewrap, synthetic roofing underlayment, and rolled insulation, it automatically feeds and drives a full 25mm plastic cap with a 10mm crown staple with every swing. By eliminating compressors and hoses, it provides an economical, portable solution that seals fastener penetrations and prevents tearing in windy conditions.

## Key features

- Non-pneumatic hammer tacker delivering both a plastic cap and staple in a single strike.
- High-capacity magazine holds 168 plastic collated caps and 168 staples.
- Drives full 25mm (1 inch) diameter plastic caps with 10mm (3/8 inch) crown staples.
- Eliminates the cost, weight, and setup of air compressors, hoses, and power cords.
- Caps create a seal over staple penetrations to prevent water and moisture ingress.
- Significantly reduces blow-offs, tearing, and lateral slippage of building wraps and felts.
- Lightweight 1.17 kg ergonomic design reduces arm fatigue during all-day use.
- Easy trigger-advance cap mechanism prepares each cap instantly for driving.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application is fixing pliable building wrap or roof underlayment to timber framing or plywood decking.
2. Verify need for airless manual installation without compressor hoses on roofs or scaffolding.
3. Ensure genuine STINGER 10mm StaplePac refills (#136420) are ordered.
4. Choose between CH38-2 (manual trigger advance) and CH38A (automatic swing advance).

## Limitations and warnings

- Designed for timber framing and wood substrates only; cannot penetrate steel framing or masonry.
- Must be loaded exclusively with genuine STINGER 10mm StaplePac fasteners.
- Not recommended where local high-wind codes mandate ring-shank nails (use CN100B).

## Installation overview

1. Open the staple magazine at the base and insert a strip of STINGER 10mm staples.
2. Open the cap gate, place a reel of 168 collated plastic caps into the basket, and feed the lead cap into the guide.
3. Close and latch both magazines securely before operation.
4. Hold the hammer handle firmly and pull the trigger to advance a cap into the strike zone.
5. Strike the target building wrap or roof underlayment firmly against the timber substrate with a standard hammer motion.
6. Ensure the staple drives fully through the center of the plastic cap flush against the membrane.
7. Repeat across the wall or roof at the spacing recommended by the membrane manufacturer (typically 300mm to 600mm centers).
