# Stinger Cn

**Stinger Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

The STINGER CN100B is a high-production pneumatic cap nailer engineered for rapid, code-compliant installation of roofing underlayments, roofing felt, and housewrap. Firing up to 5 cap nails per second, it automatically feeds and drives full 25mm plastic collated caps with 25mm ring-shank nails. Tested and approved for wind speeds up to 180 mph and certified for IBHS FORTIFIED Roof standards, it provides maximum holding power and seals fastener penetrations against water intrusion.

## Key features

- Pneumatic cap nailer firing up to 5 cap nails per second for maximum jobsite speed.
- High-capacity magazine holds 200 collated plastic caps and 200 coil ring-shank nails.
- Drives full 25mm (1 inch) plastic caps with 25mm x 2.1mm electro-galvanized or stainless nails.
- Tested and approved for severe winds up to 180 mph and IBHS FORTIFIED Home standards.
- Selectable bumpfire and sequential firing modes to suit operator workflow.
- Tool-free depth-of-drive adjustment ensures caps seat perfectly flush.
- 360-degree tool-free adjustable air exhaust.
- Lightweight 2.3 kg (4.9 lbs) ergonomic design minimizes arm fatigue.
- Plastic caps seal out moisture and eliminate material pull-through or lateral slippage.

- For timber substrates only; not designed for steel framing or solid concrete.

## Installation overview

1. Connect CN100B tool to a clean, dry, regulated compressed air supply set between 70 and 100 psi.
2. Open the nail canister and load a 200-nail coil of STINGER 25mm ring-shank nails.
3. Open the cap basket, load a 200-cap reel of 25mm collated plastic caps, and feed into the guide track.
4. Close and latch both magazines securely.
5. Select desired firing mode: bumpfire for high production speed or sequential for single-shot precision.
6. Position the nosepiece perpendicular against the roof underlayment or housewrap over timber substrate.
7. Depress safety contact and actuate trigger to drive the cap nail flush against the membrane.
8. Adjust the tool-free depth-of-drive wheel so caps seat firmly without overdriving or crushing.
