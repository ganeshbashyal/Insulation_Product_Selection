# Kingspan Universal Insulation Retaining Clips

**Kingspan Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Kingspan Universal Insulation Retaining Clips (Insu-loc discs) are 80mm diameter polypropylene retaining discs engineered to hold rigid insulation boards (such as Kooltherm K8 Cavity Board) firmly against the inner leaf of masonry cavity walls. Designed with a universal grip center, they fit securely over standard wall ties including butterfly, double triangle, and vertical twist profiles without slipping. By clamping the insulation board flush against the inner wall, they prevent board dislodgement, eliminate cavity bridging, and maintain the required clear drainage airspace.

## Key features

- 80mm diameter circular disc provides broad clamping area across insulation boards.
- Universal central gripping teeth fit butterfly, double triangle, and vertical twist wall ties.
- Engineered from high-durability, impact-resistant polypropylene.
- Firm grip design prevents clips from moving, vibrating, or sliding along the wall tie.
- Ensures rigid boards remain flush against the inner masonry leaf without slumping.
- Maintains a clear, unobstructed residual drainage cavity in masonry walls.
- Prevents mortar droppings from pushing boards into the cavity.
- Supplied in trade packs of 250 pieces for convenient jobsite handling.

## Limitations and warnings

- Designed for cavity wall ties; not suitable for direct masonry screw or nail fixing.
- Must not be forced onto oversized ties exceeding clip aperture to prevent cracking.
- Non-structural accessory; does not carry structural wall loads.

## Installation overview

1. Bed wall ties into inner masonry leaf mortar joints at specified vertical and horizontal intervals.
2. Push Kooltherm K8 Cavity Board over the wall ties and position flat against the inner leaf.
3. Slide Universal Insulation Retaining Clip over the protruding end of each wall tie.
4. Push clip firmly along the tie until it presses tightly against the outer face of the insulation board.
5. Ensure clip locks onto tie notches, holding the board flat and immovable.
6. Maintain a minimum of 2 to 3 retaining clips per 1200x450mm board (at least one clip per tie).
7. Verify the residual drainage cavity between the board and outer brick leaf remains completely clear.
