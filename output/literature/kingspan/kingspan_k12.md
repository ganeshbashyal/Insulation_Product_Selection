# Kingspan K12

**Kingspan Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Kingspan Kooltherm K12 Framing Board is an ultra-thin, fiber-free rigid thermoset phenolic insulation board engineered for timber and steel frame walls, masonry cavity walls, and concrete wall lining. With a super-low thermal conductivity of 0.021 - 0.023 W/m·K, it delivers high thermal performance at half the thickness of traditional bulk insulation batts, allowing homes to reach 7-star NatHERS ratings within standard framing depths. Laminated with composite foil facings on both sides, it minimizes thermal bridging and is fully CodeMark certified.

## Key features

- Super high thermal performance with thermal conductivity of 0.021 - 0.023 W/m·K.
- Ultra-thin profile enables slimmer walls and maximizes internal usable floor space.
- Solves 7-star NatHERS energy compliance within standard 90mm timber and steel wall studs.
- Low-Rise Insulated Wall Solution (Type C construction) replaces timber framing with adhesive mounting.
- Composite foil facings on both sides increase thermal resistance of unventilated cavities.
- Non-respirable, fiber-free closed-cell phenolic core; resistant to moisture and sagging.
- CodeMark certified for NCC compliance.
- Unaffected by air infiltration and resistant to water vapor.
- Locally manufactured in Victoria, Australia with zero ODP blowing agents.
- Global GreenTag GreenRate Level A certified with third-party verified EPD.

- For Class 2-9 buildings of Type A or B fire-resisting construction, a Performance Solution is required.
- Must not be left exposed to direct weather without protective wall wrap and cladding.
- Must not bridge damp-proof courses.

## Installation overview

1. Cut boards to width to fit tightly between framing studs or install continuously across framing face.
2. For friction-fit between studs, cut boards 5mm wider than opening and push firmly into cavity.
3. For continuous installation over framing, fasten with screws and broad washers at 300mm perimeter centers.
4. For Low-Rise Wall Solution on concrete/masonry, apply Kooltherm K12 Adhesive in vertical beads and press board to wall.
5. Tape all board joints with 72mm reinforced aluminum foil tape to maintain continuous vapor and thermal barrier.
6. Maintain minimum 20mm still air cavity where reflective air-space R-value is calculated.
