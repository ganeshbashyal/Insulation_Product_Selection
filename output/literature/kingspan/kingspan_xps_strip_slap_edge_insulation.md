# Kingspan XPS Strip Slap Edge Insulation

**Kingspan Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Kingspan GreenGuard GG350 (XPS Strip Slab Edge Insulation) is a high-performance rigid extruded polystyrene (XPS) insulation strip purpose-engineered to insulate exposed concrete slab edges in residential and commercial buildings. With a high compressive strength of 350 kPa and near-zero water absorption, it stops thermal bridging at the perimeter of concrete foundation slabs where up to 49% of ground heat loss occurs. Sized in convenient 2400mm x 300mm x 40mm strips (R1.0), it delivers durable thermal resistance to achieve 7-star NatHERS ratings without deteriorating from soil contact or moisture.

## Key features

- Purpose-designed XPS strip for insulating concrete slab edges and foundation perimeters.
- High compressive strength of 350 kPa (tested to AS 2498.3) resists heavy soil and backfill pressure.
- Fiber-free rigid extruded polystyrene core; non-deleterious and safe to handle.
- Exceptional moisture resistance with water absorption less than 1% by volume.
- Thermal conductivity of 0.028 W/m·K delivering R1.0 in a slim 40mm profile.
- Eliminates thermal bridging at concrete slab edges to meet 7-star NatHERS requirements.
- Pre-cut strip dimensions (2400mm x 300mm x 40mm) match standard footing and slab depths.
- Unaffected by ground moisture, freeze-thaw cycles, and soil microorganisms.
- Lightweight and easy to cut, shape, and secure inside slab formwork before pouring.
- Zero ODP and low GWP manufacturing process.

## Limitations and warnings

- Exposed above-ground XPS must be protected from UV sunlight using a parge coat, render, or metal flashing.
- Must not come into direct contact with solvent-based waterproofing, coal tar, or petrol.
- Maximum service temperature is 75°C.

## Installation overview

1. Position GreenGuard XPS strips vertically against the inside face of perimeter slab formwork prior to pouring concrete.
2. Ensure strips span the full depth of the slab edge (typically 300mm height).
3. Butt strip ends tightly together and seal joins with heavy-duty waterproof tape.
4. Secure strips to formwork with temporary double-sided tape or mechanical pins.
5. Place damp-proof membrane (DPM) under slab, turning it up behind the XPS strip.
6. Pour concrete slab directly against the XPS strip, ensuring full consolidation without dislodging panels.
7. Strip formwork after concrete curing; ensure exposed outer face of XPS is protected by parging or flashing.
