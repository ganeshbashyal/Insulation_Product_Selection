# Kingspan K3

**Kingspan Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Kingspan Kooltherm K3 Floorboard is a premium performance, fiber-free rigid thermoset phenolic insulation board engineered specifically for concrete ground-bearing and suspended floors. Featuring an ultra-low thermal conductivity of 0.021 - 0.023 W/m·K, it provides maximum thermal efficiency with minimal thickness, making it ideal for new build concrete slabs and retrofit floor systems. With a closed-cell structure and glass tissue facings autohesively bonded to the core, it resists ground moisture and helps achieve 7-star NatHERS ratings.

## Key features

- Thermal conductivity of 0.021 - 0.023 W/m·K, the thinnest commonly used underfloor board.
- Purpose-designed for installation beneath concrete slab-on-ground or above suspended concrete floors.
- Simplifies the path to 7-star NatHERS residential energy efficiency compliance.
- High compressive strength (≥ 150 kPa) supports residential and light commercial slab loads.
- Fiber-free closed-cell phenolic core; resistant to ground moisture and mold growth.
- Autohesively bonded glass tissue facings on both sides.
- CodeMark certified for NCC compliance.
- Reduces ground heat loss (which accounts for up to 49% of heat loss in cold climate zones).
- Locally manufactured in Australia with zero ODP blowing agents.
- Global GreenTag GreenRate Level A certified with verified EPD.

## Limitations and warnings

- For concentrated industrial heavy forklift loads, specify high-compressive XPS (e.g. GreenGuard GG500).
- Must be installed with a continuous damp-proof membrane beneath.
- Protect boards from site traffic prior to concrete pouring.

## Installation overview

1. Prepare level, well-compacted sub-base blinding layer (sand or lean-mix concrete).
2. Lay damp-proof membrane (DPM / 0.2mm polyethylene vapor barrier) over the blinding layer with 150mm taped overlaps.
3. Lay Kooltherm K3 Floorboards tightly butted in a staggered brick-bond pattern over the DPM.
4. For suspended floors, lay boards over flat concrete slab surface.
5. Cut boards neatly around internal plumbing and service penetrations with a fine-toothed hand saw.
6. Place a separating slip-sheet (0.2mm polythene) over the boards with taped 150mm overlaps to prevent concrete slurry ingress.
7. Place bar chairs and reinforcement mesh over the separating layer, taking care not to puncture the insulation.
8. Pour concrete slab directly over the assembly.
