# Acoustica Gl

**Acoustica Pipe** — pre-formed pipe insulation for thermal efficiency and condensation control.

GreenLAG pipe lagging has a noise barrier with a microcellular structure designed to contain intrusive noise generated by the turbulent flow of liquids through pipes, fittings and ductwork break-out [3]. It incorporates a patented visco-elastic acoustic barrier using QuietWave technology bonded to a 15mm or 25mm solid foam decoupling layer and an outer durable aluminium foil facing [3]. Engineered for multi-storey residential, commercial and industrial buildings, it is the thinnest and lightest NCC-compliant acoustic pipe lagging on the Australian market [3].

- Reduced installation time resulting from flexibility and resilience [3].

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm pipe application (habitable room requiring Rw+Ctr 40 vs non-habitable requiring Rw+Ctr 25) [3].
2. Confirm pipe diameter and total lineal meterage required [3].
3. Select foam thickness (18mm total thickness / 15mm foam vs 25mm foam) [3].
4. Check ceiling void / cavity clearance around pipework [3].
5. Ensure adequate supply of reinforced aluminium tape and cable ties [3].

## Limitations and warnings

- Foam decoupling layer must always face the pipe surface [3].
- All overlaps and penetrations must be fully sealed to prevent acoustic flanking [3].
- Not load-bearing [3].

## Installation overview

1. Measure pipe outside diameter and cut GreenLAG to size allowing for 50mm overlaps [3].
2. Ensure foam decoupling layer faces inward directly against the pipe surface [3].
3. Wrap GreenLAG tightly around the pipe, elbow, branch or ductwork [3].
4. Overlap all longitudinal joints by a minimum of 50mm [3].
5. Seal all longitudinal and circumferential joints with reinforced aluminium foil tape [3].
6. Secure lagging with cable ties or tape at 200-300mm centres and at all joints [3].
7. Ensure complete coverage around pipe brackets, bends and penetrations with no acoustic flanking gaps [3].
