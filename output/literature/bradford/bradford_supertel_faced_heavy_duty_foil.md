# Bradford Supertel Faced - Heavy Duty Foil

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Supertel Faced - Heavy Duty Foil is a high-density (32kg/m3) glasswool insulation blanket and board factory-laminated with a Heavy Duty Thermofoil reflective aluminium foil facing. Engineered for commercial and industrial applications where superior thermal and acoustic performance is required at minimal thickness, it is widely utilized for internal and external HVAC duct lining, acoustic enclosures, and concrete under-soffit insulation. Its non-combustible glasswool core and puncture-resistant Heavy Duty foil provide a clean, reflective, and airtight vapor barrier.

## Key features

- High-density 32kg/m3 glasswool core provides exceptional thermal and acoustic properties at minimal thickness.
- Factory-laminated with Heavy Duty Thermofoil puncture-resistant reflective aluminium foil facing.
- Forms an effective vapour barrier and clean, light-reflective surface finish.
- Non-combustible glasswool core complying with AS 1530.1.
- Early Fire Hazard properties complying with AS/NZS 1530.3 (0/0/0/0-1).
- Maximum operating service temperature up to 150°C (faced).
- Manufactured from bio-soluble FBS-1 glasswool containing up to 65% recycled glass.
- Resistant to moisture penetration and fungal growth.
- Zero Ozone Depleting Potential (ODP) and low VOC emissions.
- Available in thicknesses from 25mm to 100mm (R0.8 to R3.0).

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application (HVAC duct wrap, acoustic enclosure, or concrete soffit lining).
2. Confirm required thermal R-value under NCC Section J (e.g. R1.2 for internal ducts, R1.5/R2.0 for external ducts).
3. Verify 32kg/m3 density requirement for acoustic damping and structural integrity.
4. Select thickness (25mm to 100mm).
5. Ensure matching reinforced aluminium foil tape (Bradford 493) is ordered.

## Limitations and warnings

- Maximum continuous service temperature of facing is 150°C.
- Protect from exterior weather and rain until metal cladding or outer jacketing is installed.
- Tape all penetrations to prevent vapor ingress and condensation formation behind the foil.

## Installation overview

1. Clean sheet metal duct or concrete slab surface to ensure it is free from dust, oil, and moisture.
2. Weld or adhere insulation stick pins to duct or substrate at 300mm to 400mm centres.
3. Impale Supertel Heavy Duty Foil blanket or board over the pins with the foil facing outward.
4. Secure in place with speed clips / retaining washers pushed firmly over pins.
5. Butt all insulation seams tightly together.
6. Seal all joints, seams, and pin penetrations using matching Bradford 493 Reinforced Aluminium Foil Tape.
7. Use a plastic squeegee to firmly press tape down, ensuring an airtight, continuous vapour seal.
