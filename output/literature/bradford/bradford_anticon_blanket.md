# Bradford Anticon Blanket

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Anticon Roofing Blanket is a premium Australian-made glasswool insulation blanket factory-bonded to a reflective foil laminate facing. Engineered specifically for residential and commercial metal roofs, it combines thermal insulation, acoustic damping of heavy rain noise, and effective condensation control in a single under-roof layer. It incorporates a 150mm foil overlap edge to ensure a continuous vapour barrier, mitigating condensation damage while offering bushfire ember protection up to BAL-FZ.

## Key features

- 3-in-1 solution: thermal insulation, acoustic rain noise damping, and condensation control.
- Factory-bonded reflective foil laminate with a 150mm overlap to seal vapor joints.
- Reduces heavy rain impact noise by up to 7 dB (over 75% sound power reduction).
- Non-combustible glasswool core compliant with AS 1530.1.
- Suitable for bushfire attack levels from BAL 12.5 to BAL FZ under AS 3959.
- Reduces radiant heat transfer into the roof space by up to 97%.
- FBS-1 bio-soluble glasswool formulation approved by National Asthma Council's Sensitive Choice.
- Available in thicknesses from 60mm to 145mm (R1.3 to R3.6).
- Offered in Light Duty (LD), Medium Duty (MD), and Heavy Duty (HD) foil grades.
- Australian made from up to 65% recycled glass.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be installed dry; do not install during rain or leave exposed to weather before cladding.
- Foil must face downward toward the ceiling/attic cavity to function as a vapor barrier and control condensation.
- Avoid over-compressing blanket directly under roof sheets unless using roof spacer systems.

## Installation overview

1. Install roof safety wire mesh over purlins if required for commercial spans or fall safety.
2. Roll out Bradford Anticon blanket across or down rafters/purlins with the foil facing down into the building and the glasswool facing up.
3. Allow a slight sag (approx 25-40mm) between supports to avoid crushing the blanket against roof sheeting.
4. Overlap consecutive runs of Anticon by 150mm using the factory-provided foil overlap edge.
5. Seal all longitudinal and end overlaps thoroughly with matching reinforced aluminium foil tape to maintain vapor barrier integrity.
6. For roofs requiring roof spacers (such as Ashgrid or SpacerX), install brackets and spacer bars over the blanket.
7. Fix metal roofing sheets directly over the blanket or spacer bars without over-compressing the blanket over purlins.
