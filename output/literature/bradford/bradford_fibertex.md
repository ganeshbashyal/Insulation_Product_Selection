# Bradford Fibertex

**Bradford Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Bradford Fibertex is a range of robust, non-combustible rockwool (stone wool) industrial insulation boards and blankets engineered for high-temperature and fire-critical applications. Manufactured by spinning molten natural basalt rock and recycled slag into fine inorganic fibres bonded with a thermosetting resin, Fibertex operates continuously at service temperatures from 350°C up to 820°C with minimal shrinkage. It provides thermal conservation, fire protection, condensation prevention, and acoustic absorption for industrial process vessels, tanks, furnaces, fire-rated curtain walls, and building envelopes.

## Key features

- Non-combustible stone wool complying with AS 1530.1.
- Continuous maximum service temperatures from 350°C up to 820°C with minimal thermal shrinkage.
- Available in multiple density grades: Fibertex 350 (60kg/m3), 450 (80kg/m3), 650 (100kg/m3), and 820 (120-130kg/m3).
- Excellent acoustic absorption properties across low, mid, and high frequencies.
- Performance is not adversely affected by contact with water.
- Low water-soluble chloride content to prevent stress-corrosion cracking of austenitic stainless steel.
- Bio-soluble FBS-1 formulation certified non-hazardous under Safe Work Australia criteria.
- Supplied as semi-rigid boards, flexible blankets, and specialty pre-cut strips.
- Zero Ozone Depleting Potential (ODP) and non-toxic.
- Ideal for industrial process vessels, boilers, autoclaves, and building fire stops.

**Selection checklist**

1. Determine maximum continuous operating temperature (≤350°C select 350; ≤450°C select 450; ≤650°C select 650; ≤820°C select 820).
2. Confirm required density and mechanical load-bearing capacity.
3. Check required thickness for personnel protection (touch temperature < 60°C) or process thermal control.
4. Verify compatibility with stainless steel surfaces (confirm ASTM C795 low chloride compliance).
5. Select facing if needed (plain unfaced, foil faced, or wire mesh faced).

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not exceed maximum continuous service temperature rating for the selected grade (350°C, 450°C, 650°C, or 820°C).
- Dense grades (Fibertex 820) are semi-rigid and do not conform easily to tight curved surfaces without pre-cutting.
- Outdoor installations must be clad with metal jacketing or weatherproofing cladding to prevent water pooling.

## Installation overview

1. Clean and inspect the hot vessel, duct, or framing surface to ensure it is free from grease, loose scale, and water.
2. Weld insulation pins or studs to the substrate surface at recommended centres (typically 300mm to 450mm).
3. Impale the Fibertex boards or blankets firmly over the pins with the unfaced side against the hot surface.
4. Secure boards in place using non-corrosive metal speed clips / washers pushed over the pins.
5. Butt all board edges tightly together to eliminate thermal bridging and acoustic leaks.
6. For piping or curved vessels, secure using wire mesh, steel banding, or metal sheet cladding.
7. Trim neatly around flanges, nozzles, and structural penetrations using an insulation saw or knife.
