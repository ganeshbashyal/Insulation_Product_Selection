# Bradford Fibertex

**Bradford Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Part of the CSR family with 100+ years of Australian building solutions. Made from natural volcanic rock, delivering superior fire resistance and acoustic performance that lasts the lifetime of the building. Maintains system efficiency and prevents condensation issues.

## Key features

- Non-combustible stonewool made from natural volcanic rock.
- superior fire resistance up to 1000°C.
- excellent acoustic absorption properties.
- fully recyclable at end of life.
- prevents condensation on cold services.
- maintains system efficiency.
- 50 Year Warranty backed by CSR.
- ACCC certified and tested to Australian Standards.
