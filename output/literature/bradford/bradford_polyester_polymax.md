# Bradford Polyester - Polymax

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Polymax thermal and acoustic polyester insulation is manufactured from 100% thermally bonded polyester fibres containing up to 80% recycled content. Specifically engineered for walls and partitions in residential and commercial buildings, it provides non-irritant, easy-to-install thermal and acoustic performance. It contains no chemical binders or added flame retardants, will not pack down or deteriorate, and is approved by the National Asthma Council's Sensitive Choice program.

## Key features

- 100% thermally bonded polyester fibre with no chemical binders.
- Made from up to 80% recycled PET fibres.
- Non-irritant and completely safe to touch without protective gear.
- Acoustic and thermal dual-performance for walls and partitions.
- Tested to AS/NZS 4859.1 for thermal resistance.
- Early Fire Hazard properties complying with AS 1530.3 (Ignitability 0, Spread of Flame 0, Smoke Developed 0-2).
- Approved by National Asthma Council Australia's Sensitive Choice program.
- Resistant to moisture, mould, rot, and vermin.
- Zero Ozone Depleting Potential (ODP) and low VOC emissions.
- Backed by a 70-year performance warranty.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm framing stud spacing (430mm for 450mm centres, 580mm for 600mm centres).
2. Confirm stud depth (nominal 90mm).
3. Select required thermal R-value (R1.5, R2.0, or R2.5) to comply with NCC / NatHERS energy rating.
4. Confirm non-combustibility requirements (polyester is combustible; if AS 1530.1 non-combustible is mandated, specify glasswool).
5. Check allergy and sensitive household requirements.

## Limitations and warnings

- Not non-combustible under AS 1530.1; do not use where non-combustible insulation is required by NCC.
- Do not compress into stud cavities shallower than the nominal thickness.
- Indoor/enclosed wall application only; protect from weather and wet conditions.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure stud cavity is clean and electrical services are roughed in prior to installing insulation.
2. Friction-fit Polymax batts snugly between timber or steel wall framing studs.
3. Ensure batts fit tightly against studs, top plates, and bottom plates without gaps or sagging.
4. Avoid excessive compression of batts to preserve rated thermal thickness.
5. Cut or slit neatly around electrical wiring, nogging, and plumbing using a sharp utility knife or shears.
6. Install external wall wraps or internal plasterboard linings over framed batts.
