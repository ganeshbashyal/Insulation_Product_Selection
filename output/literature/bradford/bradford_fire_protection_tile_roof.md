# Bradford Fire Protection - Tile Roof

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Fire Protection - Tile Roof (Bradford Fireseal Party Wall Sealer for Tiled Roofs / Fireseal FZ Tile Roof Batts) is a non-combustible stone wool insulation product designed to seal the junction between the top of a fire-rated party wall and concrete or terracotta roof tiles. Available as a 50mm thick roll (4000mm x 300mm or 360mm) or 100mm x 115mm x 1200mm batts, it compresses under the tiled roof build-up to prevent fire, sparks, and embers from traveling between adjoining occupancies in Class 1 buildings. It delivers high thermal stability, resisting shrinkage under extreme fire exposure, and meets BAL-FZ bushfire standards.

## Key features

- Non-combustible stone wool construction complying with AS 1530.1.
- 50mm thickness optimised for profile depths of concrete and terracotta roof tiles.
- Seals top-of-wall junctions to prevent flame and spark spread between tenancies.
- Outstanding resistance to shrinkage under extreme fire conditions up to 1000°C.
- Certified for Bushfire Attack Level BAL-FZ construction under tiled roofs.
- Bio-soluble FBS-1 rockwool formulation.
- Water-resistant fibres that do not degrade upon contact with moisture.
- Satisfies NCC / BCA Deemed-to-Satisfy provisions for Class 1 separating walls.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm roof covering is concrete or terracotta tiles (requiring 50mm thickness).
2. Select width based on party wall structure (300mm standard, 360mm for wider cavity party walls).
3. Measure total linear metres needed along the top of party walls.
4. Check if property is in a BAL-FZ bushfire attack zone.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be compressed minimum 10-15% by roof tiles to ensure fire and smoke seal.
- Not suitable for deep-rib metal roofing where 75mm is required.
- Must not be left exposed to pooling water or continuous outdoor weathering.

## Installation overview

1. Roll Fireseal 50mm Party Wall Sealer along the top of the fire-rated party wall prior to roof tile installation.
2. Ensure the blanket spans the full width of the wall and timber/steel framing.
3. Lay roof tile battens and tiles over the sealer, ensuring continuous 10-15% compression beneath tile profiles.
4. Butt joints in blanket tightly together with no gaps.
5. Verify no void exists where tile profiles create contours above the party wall line.
