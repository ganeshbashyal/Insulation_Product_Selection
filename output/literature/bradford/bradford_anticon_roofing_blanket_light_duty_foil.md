# Bradford Anticon Roofing Blanket - Light Duty Foil

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Anticon Roofing Blanket - Light Duty Foil is an economical glasswool insulation blanket laminated with a Light Duty reflective foil backing. Engineered for domestic metal roofs with closely spaced timber battens or steel rafters (up to 600mm centres) or roofs fully supported by safety wire mesh, it provides thermal insulation, dampens rain noise, and controls condensation. It features a 150mm foil overlap edge to maintain vapour seal continuity across domestic roofs and sheds.

## Key features

- Faced with Light Duty reflective foil laminate for cost-effective domestic applications.
- Designed for closely spaced framing supports (up to 600mm centres) or mesh-supported roofs.
- Factory-bonded with a 150mm foil overlap edge to facilitate vapour-tight sealing.
- Non-combustible glasswool insulation core meeting AS 1530.1.
- Bushfire ember protection compliant from BAL 12.5 to BAL FZ under AS 3959.
- Damps heavy rain noise by up to 7 dB to improve acoustic comfort inside.
- Reflects radiant heat away from the roof space to enhance energy efficiency.
- Bio-soluble FBS-1 glasswool approved by Sensitive Choice for allergy sufferers.
- Available in 60mm, 80mm, and 100mm thicknesses (R1.3 to R2.3).
- Cost-effective solution to stop roof condensation in residential metal roofs.

## Applications and selection

- roof
- roofline
- shed
- ceiling

**Selection checklist**

1. Verify framing spacing is ≤ 600mm centres (if spacing is 600-900mm use MD; if > 900mm use HD).
2. Check that safety mesh is installed if used over wider industrial spans.
3. Confirm thermal R-value requirement (R1.3, R1.8, or R2.3).
4. Ensure reinforced foil tape is included for sealing the 150mm overlap.
5. Confirm application is under metal roof cladding.

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not use on framing spans exceeding 600mm without wire mesh support (foil may tear or sag).
- Glasswool must be kept completely dry during storage and installation.
- Foil must face downward into the building space to prevent condensation.

## Installation overview

1. Confirm roof batten spacing does not exceed 600mm centres (or that safety wire mesh is installed).
2. Roll out Anticon Light Duty over framing with the foil facing down into the attic or room.
3. Ensure a gentle sag of 25mm to 40mm between battens to prevent compression against sheeting.
4. Overlap adjoining blanket runs by 150mm using the pre-extended foil edge.
5. Seal all overlaps and edges with Bradford 493 Reinforced Foil Tape to create a continuous vapor seal.
6. Fix metal roof sheets over battens, taking care not to puncture or excessively stretch the light-duty foil.
7. Complete installation prior to rainfall to keep the glasswool dry.
