# Bradford Anticon Roofing Blanket - Medium Duty Foil

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Anticon Roofing Blanket - Medium Duty Foil is the benchmark residential and light-commercial metal roof insulation blanket, factory-laminated with a Medium Duty reflective foil facing. Engineered for support spacings up to 900mm centres without safety mesh, it combines a bio-soluble glasswool blanket with a robust vapour-barrier foil to minimise condensation risk, attenuate rain drumming noise, and reflect radiant summer heat. With a 150mm foil overlap edge, it delivers reliable thermal efficiency and bushfire protection up to BAL-FZ.

## Key features

- Faced with Medium Duty reflective foil laminate for standard residential and light commercial builds.
- Engineered for batten and purlin spans up to 900mm centres without requiring wire mesh.
- Factory-bonded 150mm foil overlap edge ensures a continuous, airtight vapour barrier.
- Non-combustible glasswool core compliant with AS 1530.1.
- Bushfire ember attack protection certified from BAL 12.5 to BAL FZ under AS 3959.
- Damps heavy rain noise by up to 7 dB for a significantly quieter living environment.
- Reflects up to 97% of radiant heat to lower household heating and cooling loads.
- Made with up to 65% recycled glass using FBS-1 bio-soluble formulation.
- Sensitive Choice approved for allergy and asthma sufferers.
- Available in thicknesses from 60mm to 130mm (R1.3 to R3.0).

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm framing spacing is ≤ 900mm centres (standard for most domestic metal roofs).
2. Verify total system R-value calculation meets NCC energy compliance.
3. Select thickness (60mm R1.3, 80mm R1.8, 100mm R2.3, 110mm R2.5, or 130mm R3.0).
4. Ensure Bradford 493 tape is ordered for sealing the 150mm overlap.
5. Confirm bushfire BAL requirements (BAL 12.5 to BAL FZ).

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not span greater than 900mm centres without wire mesh (specify Heavy Duty for spans up to 1200mm).
- Must be kept dry prior to and during installation.
- Foil must always face down toward the building interior.

## Installation overview

1. Confirm roof batten spacing is 900mm centres or less.
2. Roll out Anticon Medium Duty over rafters or battens with the foil facing downwards into the roof space.
3. Allow a nominal 25-40mm sag between battens to prevent compression against the roof sheet.
4. Overlap adjacent rolls by 150mm using the pre-extended foil edge.
5. Tape all foil joins with Bradford 493 Reinforced Aluminium Foil Tape using a squeegee to achieve an airtight vapour barrier.
6. Fasten metal roofing sheets directly over the blanket using recommended screws with neoprene washers.
7. Ensure installation is covered before rain occurs.
