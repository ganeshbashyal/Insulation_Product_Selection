# Bradford Gold Batts

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Gold Batts are Australia's leading glasswool thermal and acoustic insulation batts for residential ceilings and walls. Manufactured from up to 80% recycled glass with FBS-1 bio-soluble formulation and low-itch Comfort Touch technology, they form a continuous, resilient thermal barrier that reduces winter heat loss and summer heat gain by up to 45%. Available in R-values from R1.5 to R2.0 for walls and R2.5 to R4.1 for standard ceilings, they are non-combustible, low allergen, Sensitive Choice approved, and backed by a 70-year warranty.

## Key features

- Reduces home heating and cooling energy use by up to 45%.
- Manufactured from up to 80% recycled glass.
- Low-itch Comfort Touch technology for softer handling and reduced airborne dust.
- Non-combustible when tested in accordance with AS 1530.1.
- Approved partner of National Asthma Council Sensitive Choice program.
- Will not slump, shrink, pack down, or deteriorate over the building lifetime.
- Wall batts treated with water repellent to resist moisture wicking.
- Available in standard 430mm and 580mm widths to suit 450mm and 600mm framing centres.
- Sized to fit standard Australian timber and steel stud framing.
- Backed by CSR Bradford's 70-year product performance warranty.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm application area (ceiling vs external walls).
2. Determine required R-value to comply with 7-star NatHERS / NCC climate zone targets.
3. Measure joist or stud spacing (450mm centres require 430mm batts; 600mm centres require 580mm batts).
4. Check wall cavity depth (75mm for 70mm studs, 90mm for 90mm studs).
5. Verify downlight IC ratings to establish required clearances.

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not compress batts into cavities narrower than nominal thickness as this reduces thermal R-value.
- Must be protected from continuous water exposure and prolonged outdoor weather.
- Maintain electrical safety clearances around recessed electrical equipment.

## Installation overview

1. Turn off power at the main switchboard before entering ceiling space or touching wall cavities.
2. Fit batts snugly between timber or steel framing members without gaps or compression.
3. For ceilings, lay batts flat between ceiling joists covering the plasterboard completely.
4. At ceiling eaves, maintain a 20mm gap between insulation and roof cladding/sarking.
5. Maintain minimum 50mm clearance around non-IC rated downlights, exhaust fans, and flues as per AS 3999.
6. For external walls, install water-repellent batts between studs and secure with wall wrap before brickwork or cladding.
