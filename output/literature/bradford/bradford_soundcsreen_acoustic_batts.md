# Bradford Soundcsreen Acoustic Batts

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford SoundScreen is Australia's most popular high-density acoustic insulation batt, specially engineered to reduce sound transfer through internal walls, external walls, and mid-floors. Manufactured from high-density FBS-1 bio-soluble glasswool containing up to 65% recycled glass, SoundScreen absorbs airborne sound and acoustic vibrations to reduce loud noises to a whisper. It is non-combustible, provides thermal resistance up to R3.1, and is approved by the National Asthma Council's Sensitive Choice program.

## Key features

- Reduces sound power transfer by up to 75% in interior walls and mid-floors.
- High-density glasswool formulation designed specifically for acoustic absorption.
- Non-combustible in accordance with AS 1530.1.
- Early Fire Hazard indices of 0/0/0/0-1 to AS/NZS 1530.3.
- Thermal ratings from R1.7 to R3.1 to provide dual thermal-acoustic benefits.
- Approved by National Asthma Council Australia's Sensitive Choice program.
- FBS-1 bio-soluble glasswool formulation, non-hazardous and safe to handle.
- Will not pack down, shrink, mould, rot, or deteriorate.
- Manufactured in Australia from up to 65% recycled glass.
- Backed by a 70-year performance warranty.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm stud or joist cavity width (430mm for 450mm centres, 580mm for 600mm centres).
2. Confirm stud cavity depth (e.g. 70mm, 75mm, or 90mm stud cavity determines R1.7, R2.0, or R2.5).
3. Check required acoustic sound transmission rating (Rw 40 to Rw 55+).
4. Confirm non-combustibility requirement (SoundScreen passes AS 1530.1).
5. Determine if thermal R-value is required for external walls or mid-floors.

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not compress batts into cavities narrower than the nominal batt thickness.
- Acoustic performance depends on airtight wall construction; seal all perimeter gaps and penetrations.
- Indoor/enclosed cavity application only; protect from weather.

## Installation overview

1. Friction-fit SoundScreen batts tightly between timber or steel wall framing studs.
2. Ensure batts completely fill the stud or joist cavity without gaps or voids around edges.
3. Cut batts neatly around electrical conduits, plumbing pipes, and junction boxes using an insulation knife.
4. For between-floor applications, lay batts between mid-floor joists or hold in place before ceiling lining installation.
5. In party walls or home theatres, pair with acoustic plasterboard (e.g. Gyprock Soundchek) and resilient mounts for maximum Rw performance.
6. Fasten internal wall linings directly over the insulated framework.
