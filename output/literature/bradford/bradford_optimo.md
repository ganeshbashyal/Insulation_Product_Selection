# Bradford Optimo

**Bradford Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Bradford Optimo is a rigid, high-density glasswool insulation batt specifically engineered for suspended timber floors in new builds and retrofits. Sized deliberately narrower than wall batts (415mm and 565mm) to fit snugly between floor joists, Optimo reduces subfloor heat loss by up to 20%, blocks draughts, and dampens footstep resonance and external noise transmission by up to 6dB. Non-combustible, low-allergen, and Sensitive Choice certified, it is secured from below or above using proprietary Optimo Saddles, Straps, or StayFast brackets and backed by a 70-year warranty.

## Key features

- Specifically designed for suspended timber floors to eliminate cold floors and subfloor draughts.
- Sized narrower (415mm and 565mm) for a tight, friction-fit grip between timber joists.
- Rigid, high-density glasswool construction prevents sagging and holds thickness.
- Cuts heat loss through suspended timber floors by up to 20%.
- Reduces acoustic reverberation and foot traffic noise through floorboards by up to 6dB.
- Non-combustible when tested in accordance with AS 1530.1.
- National Asthma Council Sensitive Choice approved for low-allergen indoor air quality.
- Simple installation using proprietary Optimo Saddles (new builds), Straps, or StayFast brackets (retrofits).
- Manufactured from up to 80% recycled glass with bio-soluble FBS-1 formulation.
- Backed by CSR Bradford's 70-year product performance warranty.

**Selection checklist**

1. Measure joist centres (450mm vs 600mm) to select 415mm or 565mm batt width.
2. Confirm required R-value (R2.1 or R2.5) based on climate zone and energy rating targets.
3. Check installation method (new build from above vs retrofit from subfloor crawlspace).
4. Order corresponding fixing accessories (Optimo Saddles, Optimo Straps, or Optimo StayFast brackets).
5. Ensure minimum crawl space clearance exists for safe access in retrofit jobs.

## Limitations and warnings

- Must be mechanically supported with Saddles, Straps, or StayFast brackets to prevent gravity sagging over time.
- Do not cover electrical subfloor wiring that may be susceptible to derating without checking AS/NZS 3000.
- Do not block subfloor wall vents which are critical for moisture dissipation.

## Installation overview

1. Turn off power at main switchboard before entering subfloor space.
2. Measure joist spacings (use 415mm batts for 450mm centres; 565mm batts for 600mm centres).
3. In new builds, fit Optimo Saddles across joists at max 500mm spacing before laying flooring, then place batts on saddle feet from above.
4. For retrofit beneath existing floors, push batts up tightly against underside of floorboards ensuring no air gaps.
5. Secure retrofitted batts using Optimo Straps fixed to joists at max 500mm intervals and swivelled 90 degrees to support batts.
6. Alternatively, use self-adhesive Optimo StayFast brackets (2 per side per batt) fixed 300mm in from batt ends.
7. Trim batts 5mm wider than opening if joist spacing is irregular to maintain a snug friction fit.
8. Take care not to block subfloor ventilation openings.
