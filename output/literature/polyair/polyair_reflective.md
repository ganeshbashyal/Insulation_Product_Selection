# Polyair Reflective

**Polyair Reflective** — reflective foil insulation for radiant heat control.

Polyair Reflective is an Australian-manufactured thermo-reflective insulation range engineered with an advanced closed-cell polyethylene foam core laminated between pure aluminium foil facings. Designed for roofs, walls, and sheds in residential and commercial buildings, it provides a 3-in-1 thermal insulation, thermal break, and vapour barrier. By reflecting up to 97% of radiant heat and delivering an in-situ thermal break, it significantly reduces summer heat gain and prevents winter heat loss.

## Key features

- 3-in-1 performance: thermal insulation, thermal break, and vapour barrier.
- Pure aluminium reflective foil facings reflect up to 97% of radiant heat.
- Cross-linked closed-cell polyethylene foam core prevents moisture absorption.
- Provides compliant thermal break on steel-framed roofs and walls.
- Anti-glare coating on one face for installer safety in bright sunlight.
- Non-toxic, non-allergenic, and fiber-free with zero itch during handling.
- Advanced foam cell structure resists delamination and moisture degradation.
- Low Flammability Index compliant with AS 1530.2.
- Suitable for bushfire-prone areas up to BAL-40 under AS 3959.
- Proudly Australian made for local climate conditions.

## Limitations and warnings

- Requires a minimum 20mm sealed airspace adjacent to foil face to achieve rated Total R-value.
- Do not allow direct contact with wet concrete or alkaline mortar to prevent foil corrosion.
- Not designed as a permanent foot-traffic surface or exposed outdoor weather membrane.

## Installation overview

1. Roll out Polyair over roof purlins or wall framing with anti-glare face oriented outward toward the installer.
2. Ensure roll edges overlap by a minimum of 50mm (or butt join if fully taped with 72mm foil tape).
3. Secure mechanically to steel or timber framing using wafer-head self-drilling screws with washers.
4. Maintain a minimum 20mm still air cavity adjacent to the reflective face to achieve rated Total R-values.
5. Tape all seams, joins, and penetrations with 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape to form a continuous vapour barrier.
6. At roof eaves, allow membrane to drain freely into gutters without ponding.
