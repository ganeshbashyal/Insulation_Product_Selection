# Ecowool Wrap

**Ecowool Wrap** — reflective membrane for weather protection and condensation control.

Ecowool ClimaWrap (ClimaWrap CW / ClimaWrap BMX800) is a durable, triple-layer Class 4 vapour-permeable building wrap engineered to provide weather defense and moisture control in external walls. Constructed with two spunbonded non-woven outer layers protecting a microporous vapour-permeable inner membrane core, it acts as a water and air barrier while allowing internal water vapour to escape. Compliant with AS/NZS 4200.1:2017 and the National Construction Code across climate zones 2 to 8, it reduces condensation risks behind brick veneer, fibre cement, timber, and metal claddings.

## Key features

- Triple-layer spunbond non-woven construction for high tensile and tear strength.
- Class 4 Vapour Permeable membrane specially designed to minimise condensation risk.
- Functions as a water barrier protecting the building frame from external weather penetration.
- Effective air barrier reducing air leakage and enhancing thermal efficiency.
- Flammability Index Low (<5) tested to AS 1530.2-1993.
- Suitable for a wide range of climatic conditions across Australia (Zones 2 to 8).
- Puncture and tear resistant during construction on site.
- Protects on both faces by non-woven structures against corrosive environments.
- Available in convenient 1350mm and 1500mm widths.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- external wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm wall construction requires a Class 4 breathable vapour-permeable membrane.
2. Select roll width (1350mm/1370mm or 1500mm).
3. Calculate roll quantities based on wall area allowing 150mm overlaps.
4. Check climate zone suitability (certified for climate zones 2 to 8).
5. Order complementary EcoSeal 201 Cloth Tape for joint sealing.

## Limitations and warnings

- Not suitable for roofing applications.
- Claddings must provide a drained cavity; provision must be given for drainage of trapped moisture behind steel cladding.
- Must not be left exposed to direct sunlight beyond recommended UV exposure limits.

## Installation overview

1. Install horizontally on external timber or steel studs starting from the bottom plate.
2. Fasten mechanically to framing members at maximum 300mm centres using broad-head nails or staples.
3. Ensure upper layers overlap lower layers by at least 150mm to shed water outward.
4. Vertical joints must overlap by at least 150mm over a stud.
5. Seal all overlaps and penetrations with EcoSeal 201 Cloth Tape for airtight weather resistance.
6. Provide adequate drainage cavity between ClimaWrap and exterior cladding (especially non-absorbent claddings like steel).
7. Seal neatly around window and door openings prior to installing joinery.
