# Ecowool Ecoseal Tape

**Ecowool Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

EcoSeal 201 Cloth Tape is a heavy-duty, high-tack polyethylene-coated cloth tape specially designed for bonding, sealing, and mending overlapped edges of ClimaWrap, vapour barriers, and building membranes. Formulated with a high-tack pressure-sensitive acrylic/synthetic adhesive, it provides outstanding adhesion, shear strength, and weather resistance across Australian climatic conditions. It seals overlaps, joins, and penetrations to maintain airtight and watertight building envelope integrity.

## Key features

- Specially designed for bonding, sealing, and mending building membranes and ClimaWrap.
- High-tack adhesive provides outstanding adhesion and cohesion.
- Polyethylene-coated cloth backing resists tears, punctures, and abrasion.
- Forms a durable, moisture-resistant and airtight seal.
- Conforms easily to irregular surfaces, corners, and penetrations.
- Hand tearable for fast, convenient installation on site.
- Reliable bonding across wide temperature variations in Australia.
- 48mm width provides ample lap coverage.

## Applications and selection

- wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm tape is being paired with ClimaWrap, vapour barriers, or building membranes.
2. Measure total linear metres of horizontal and vertical membrane overlaps and penetrations.
3. Ensure surface temperature during installation allows proper adhesive bonding.

## Limitations and warnings

- Surfaces must be dry and clean before application.
- Pressure-sensitive adhesive requires firm pressure to achieve full bonding.
- Not designed for structural mechanical fastening.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure building wrap surfaces are clean, dry, and free of dust, grease, and moisture.
2. Align membrane lap with recommended overlap (minimum 150mm or 50mm if taped per AS 4200.2).
3. Centre EcoSeal 201 Cloth Tape evenly over the joint seam.
4. Press tape firmly along the entire length using hand pressure or a squeegee to ensure 100% surface contact.
5. Smooth out any air bubbles or wrinkles.
6. Tape around window flashings, penetrations, and frame junctions to ensure airtightness.
