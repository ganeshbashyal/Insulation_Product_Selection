# Rockwool Pipe Insulation

**Thermotec High-temperature pipe** — high-temperature pipe insulation product.

Thermotec Rockwool is a heavy duty foil faced, pre-formed pipe lagging suitable for applications not exceeding 650°C steam lines and most industrial and commercial applications.

## Key features

- Heavy duty.
- Foil faced.
- Pre-formed.
- Nominal density 115 ~ 140 kg/m3.
- Service temperature max. 650°C.
- Non-combustible.
- Moisture absorption M ≤0.2% by weight.

## Applications and selection

- Steam lines
- Industrial applications
- Commercial applications

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm the application (wall, ceiling, floor, roof, pipe or service) matches the family.
2. Confirm the target rating and construction build-up with the project team.
3. Confirm available cavity or fixing depth against the product dimensions.
4. Check NCC, fire, BAL or acoustic requirements with a qualified reviewer before specifying.
5. Record the suburb/postcode so climate-zone requirements can be checked.
