# Rockwool Pipe Insulation

**Thermotec High-temperature pipe** — high-temperature pipe insulation product.

Thermotec Rockwool Pipe Insulation is a heavy-duty, pre-formed mineral wool pipe lagging engineered for industrial and commercial pipework carrying high-temperature fluids and steam at continuous temperatures up to 650°C. Manufactured from non-combustible volcanic rock fibres with a nominal density of 115 to 140 kg/m³, it is factory-laminated with a reinforced aluminium foil facing to act as a vapour barrier and protect against moisture ingress. It delivers exceptional thermal insulation and acoustic noise attenuation on steam lines, boilers, high-pressure hot water, and exhaust systems.

## Key features

- High-density pre-formed stone wool pipe sections (nominal 115 to 140 kg/m³).
- Extreme continuous service temperature up to 650°C (outer foil limit 80°C - 100°C).
- Non-combustible stone wool substrate complying with AS 1530.1 and BS 476 Part 4.
- Factory-applied reinforced aluminium foil facing provides a tough vapour barrier.
- Specifically engineered for high-pressure steam pipes, boilers, and industrial processes.
- Provides substantial acoustic noise dampening of fluid rush and turbulent knocking.
- Water-repellent, rot-proof, and resistant to fungal and bacterial growth.
- Chemically inert and will not promote stress corrosion cracking on copper or stainless steel.
- Pre-slit one-meter hinged sections allow rapid snap-on installation.
- Complies with Australian commercial and industrial insulation specifications.

## Applications and selection

- pipe
- duct
- wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm pipe operating temperature exceeds 100°C (up to 650°C continuous).
2. Measure outside diameter of pipe to match internal diameter of Rockwool section.
3. Select wall thickness (25mm, 38mm, or 50mm) based on required surface touch temperature and energy limits.
4. Order matching 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape and metal banding straps.

## Limitations and warnings

- Outer aluminium foil facing must not exceed 80°C - 100°C (insulation wall thickness must be calculated to keep surface temperature within limits).
- Outdoor installations exposed to weather require metal cladding or UV-resistant VentureClad jacketing.
- Handle with gloves and dust mask during cutting to avoid mechanical fiber irritation.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure pipe surface is cool, dry, clean, and rust-treated before lagging.
2. Open the pre-slit hinged 1m Rockwool section and snap over the pipe.
3. Align longitudinal seams along the least visible side (typically top or back).
4. Peel release paper from the factory self-adhesive foil overlap (or tape with reinforced foil tape).
5. Smooth down the foil lap firmly with a squeegee to establish an airtight vapour seal.
6. Butt consecutive 1m sections tightly together and seal circumferential joints with 72mm reinforced foil tape.
7. Secure lagging with stainless steel or aluminium bands or tie wire at 300mm to 400mm centres for high-temperature pipework.
8. Mitre cut sections neatly around elbows, valves, and tees, sealing all cut edges with foil tape.
