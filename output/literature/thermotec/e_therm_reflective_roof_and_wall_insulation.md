# E-Therm Reflective Roof and Wall Insulation

**Thermotec Reflective** — reflective foil insulation for radiant heat control.

Thermotec E-Therm is a high-performance, Australian-made double-sided reflective foam roof, wall, and underfloor insulation engineered to replace traditional bulk insulation and sarking. Formulated with a closed-cell low-density polyethylene (LDPE) foam core laminated on both sides with pure aluminum foil facings (one side featuring a green anti-glare coating), it delivers an in-situ thermal break of up to R0.25 to prevent thermal bridging on steel-framed buildings. It incorporates a patented 150mm foil overlap flap, effectively manages condensation, and reduces acoustic rain and airborne noise by up to 12 dB.

## Key features

- 3-in-1 solution: thermal insulation, R0.20+ thermal break, and vapor barrier in one product.
- Pure aluminum reflective foil facings reflect up to 97% of radiant heat (emittance 0.03).
- Available in 5mm (R0.16), 6.5mm (R0.20), and 8mm (R0.25) closed-cell foam core thicknesses.
- Patented 150mm foil overlap flap eliminates the cost of purchasing extra product for laps.
- Reduces radiated rain, aircraft, and traffic noise by up to 12 dB.
- Completely waterproof closed-cell polyethylene foam will not rot, shrink, or sag over time.
- Anti-glare green coating on one face ensures safe daytime installation on roofs.
- Zero fibers, non-allergenic, non-toxic, and itch-free handling without PPE.
- Manufactured in Australia incorporating up to 80% recycled foam content.
- GreenStar and BASIX compliant meeting international ODP-EMI9 requirements.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm building type (residential home, steel shed, commercial warehouse, or underfloor).
2. Select thickness (6.5mm E-Therm 65 for R0.20 thermal break on steel framing; 8mm for higher R0.25 rating).
3. Calculate roll quantities based on 30 m² net coverage per roll (patented 150mm flap eliminates overlap wastage).
4. Order matching 72mm reinforced aluminium foil sealing tape.
5. Verify minimum 20mm airspace is maintained in roof and wall cavities to achieve rated system R-values.

## Limitations and warnings

- Requires an adjacent sealed airspace of at least 20mm to generate rated total system R-values.
- Avoid direct contact with wet alkaline concrete or mortar to prevent aluminum foil etching.
- Not designed as a trafficable roof membrane; do not walk directly on unsupported insulation.

## Installation overview

1. Roll out E-Therm over roof purlins, rafters, or wall framing with anti-glare green face facing outward toward the installer.
2. Allow a nominal 40mm drape between purlins on roofs to create an airspace and facilitate condensation drainage.
3. Align adjacent rolls using the patented 150mm factory foil overlap flap.
4. Tape all longitudinal and transverse seams using 48mm or 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape to form a continuous seal.
5. Fasten metal roof sheets or wall cladding directly through the E-Therm into steel or timber framing.
6. Ensure E-Therm terminates cleanly at the gutter line to drain condensation into gutters.
7. For steel framed walls, use E-Therm 65 or 80 to satisfy the mandatory R0.20 in-situ thermal break requirement.
8. For suspended timber floors, fix beneath floor joists maintaining a 20mm still air cavity.
