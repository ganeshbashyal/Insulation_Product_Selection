# ProctorGeo IRMA

**Proctor Drainage / Ventilation** — drainage / ventilation insulation product.

ProctorGeo IRMA is a heavy-duty, water-vapour permeable non-woven geotextile membrane specifically engineered as a protective and water-dispersing separation layer for Inverted Roof Membrane Assemblies (IRMA), green roofs, and protected membrane roofs. Manufactured from high-tenacity, UV-stabilised virgin polypropylene fibers, it is installed loose over extruded polystyrene (XPS) insulation boards beneath gravel ballast or pavers. It prevents dirt, ballast fines, and silt from washing into insulation joints while allowing water vapour to vent freely, mitigating rain water cooling and maintaining roof thermal efficiency.

## Key features

- Engineered specifically for Inverted Roof Membrane Assemblies (IRMA) and ballast green roofs.
- High water-vapour permeability prevents moisture entrapment above insulation boards.
- Functions as an effective filter layer preventing silt and ballast fines from bridging joints.
- Reduces rain water cooling effect by draining up to 98% of runoff above insulation.
- High tensile and CBR puncture resistance withstands rough gravel ballast and pavers.
- UV-stabilised virgin polypropylene construction resists rot, mildew, and soil chemicals.
- Dimensionally stable, lightweight, and easy to roll out over XPS insulation boards.
- Non-toxic, chemically inert, and safe for rainwater catchment systems.

## Applications and selection

- roof
- roofline
- floor
- subfloor

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm roof design is an Inverted Roof Membrane Assembly (IRMA) with XPS insulation above waterproofing.
2. Verify ballast type (washed round river gravel 16-32mm or pavers on pedestals).
3. Calculate total roof area allowing for minimum 300mm overlaps and parapet upturns.
4. Confirm drainage outlets have gravel guard cages installed.

## Limitations and warnings

- Not a waterproofing membrane; designed strictly as a filtration, water-deflecting, and separation geotextile.
- Must be weighed down with ballast or pavers to prevent wind uplift.
- Do not use crushed, sharp aggregate ballast that could pierce the fabric.

## Installation overview

1. Complete primary roof waterproofing membrane and ensure watertightness testing is verified.
2. Lay extruded polystyrene (XPS) rigid insulation boards tightly butted over the waterproofing.
3. Roll out ProctorGeo IRMA geotextile loose directly over the top of the XPS insulation.
4. Ensure all adjoining geotextile runs overlap by a minimum of 300mm.
5. Turn geotextile up all parapets, upstands, and penetrations to the height of the ballast finish.
6. Place rounded gravel ballast (minimum 50mm depth) or paving slabs on pedestals directly over the fabric.
7. Ensure ballast is placed progressively to prevent wind uplift of the geotextile during installation.
