# Proctor Passive SmartVap

**Proctor Membrane** — membrane insulation product.

ProctorPassive SmartVap 100 is an internal air barrier and variable vapour control layer designed to control moisture movement and air leakage on the warm interior side of building envelopes. Manufactured from an air-tight, spunbonded polyolefin with a functional polymer layer, its moisture-variable design adapts its vapour resistance according to ambient humidity. In winter it limits vapour diffusion into cold wall cavities, while in summer it opens up to permit inward drying, protecting timber framing from mould and rot.

## Key features

- Variable vapour resistance: adapts permeability based on relative humidity.
- Functions as an internal airtight barrier to satisfy blower door airtightness targets.
- Limits winter vapour diffusion into external wall and roof cavities.
- Allows inward drying in summer when interior humidity levels reverse.
- High mechanical strength resistant to tearing and stretching during installation.
- Translucent material allows timber stud and insulation placement visibility.
- Ideal for Passive House, mass timber, and cold climate building envelopes.
- Low Flammability Index compliant with AS 1530.2.

2. Verify membrane will be installed on the interior warm side of bulk insulation.
3. Calculate total internal wall and ceiling area allowing 100mm overlaps.
4. Ensure airtight seaming tapes and edge sealants are ordered.

## Limitations and warnings

- Internal application only; not for external weather-facing envelope installation.
- Do not leave exposed to interior UV sunlight for longer than 60 days before lining.
- Must not be punctured without subsequent airtight taping.

## Installation overview

1. Install on the warm interior side of wall framing or ceiling joists over bulk insulation.
2. Fix tautly across studs or rafters with staples or clouts at 150mm centres.
3. Ensure printed side faces inward toward the interior living space.
4. Overlap consecutive sheets by a minimum of 100mm.
5. Tape all overlaps with an airtight tape such as Wraptite Tape or DriFlash Tape.
6. Seal membrane securely to adjoining concrete floors, window frames, and ceilings with compatible adhesives.
7. Seal all pipe, duct, and electrical cable penetrations with flexible airtight grommets or tape.
8. Install interior plasterboard or timber lining over the membrane using battened service cavities.
