# Proctor YouRippa Curing Tape

**Proctor Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

Proctor YouRippa Curing Tape is a heavy-duty, hand-tearable polyethylene cloth protection and curing tape engineered for demanding jobsite applications. Formulated with a natural rubber adhesive, it delivers strong, residue-free adhesion to concrete, timber, metal, and floor protection sheets during construction. Designed with an embossed backing for effortless straight hand-tearing, it secures floor protection boards and concrete curing covers without leaving sticky residue upon removal.

## Key features

- Hand tearable in both directions without cutting tools or scissors.
- Clean, residue-free removal from sensitive finishes and cured concrete.
- Aggressive natural rubber adhesive bonds firmly to multiple surfaces.
- Heavy-duty embossed polyethylene cloth carrier resists tears and abrasion.
- Waterproof backing protects joins from spills, foot traffic, and washdowns.
- Ideal for holding floor protection boards, films, and concrete curing covers.
- High conformability around corners, thresholds, and irregular profiles.
- Suitable for indoor and temporary outdoor construction use.

## Limitations and warnings

- Temporary protection tape; not designed for permanent building envelope weather-sealing.
- Do not leave on sensitive timber finishes beyond recommended removal period.
- Not an air-barrier or vapour-permeable membrane joining tape.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure floor protection sheets, concrete, or surfaces are clean, dry, and free of dust.
2. Lay out floor protection boards or curing covers with butted edges.
3. Tear YouRippa tape by hand across the roll to desired length.
4. Center tape over the seam between protection sheets.
5. Press down firmly along the tape line to activate adhesive.
6. Smooth down edges to prevent tripping hazards during foot traffic.
7. Peel tape cleanly away upon project handover without leaving adhesive residue.
