# Proctor YouRippa Protect Tape

**Proctor Accessory** — installation accessories and fixings.

Proctor YouRippa Heavy Duty Curing & Protection Tape is an embossed polyethylene cloth-backed protection tape coated with a natural rubber-based adhesive, engineered specifically for temporary jobsite surface protection. Designed with an advanced carrier that tears straight and cleanly by hand in both directions without cutting tools, it secures floor protection boards and concrete curing covers against heavy foot traffic. Formulated for clean, residue-free removal, it protects delicate finished timber, stone, metal, and cured concrete surfaces during active building works.

## Key features

- Tears straight and cleanly by hand in both directions without knives or scissors.
- Clean, residue-free removal from sensitive finishes and cured concrete.
- Aggressive natural rubber adhesive bonds firmly to multiple substrates.
- Heavy-duty embossed polyethylene cloth carrier resists tears and puncture.
- Waterproof backing protects seams from construction spills and wet washdowns.
- Secures floor protection boards, films, and concrete curing blankets.
- Conforms readily to corners, thresholds, and irregular profiles.
- Suitable for indoor fitouts and short-term outdoor construction protection.

## Limitations and warnings

- Temporary protection tape; not designed for permanent building envelope weather-sealing.
- Do not leave on sensitive porous timber finishes beyond recommended removal period.
- Not a vapour-permeable building wrap joining tape.

## Installation overview

1. Ensure floor protection sheets, concrete, or finishes are dry and free of loose grit and dust.
2. Lay out floor protection boards, hardboard, or concrete curing blankets with tightly butted edges.
3. Tear YouRippa tape cleanly by hand across the roll to the required length.
4. Centre the tape directly over the seam between adjacent protection sheets.
5. Press down firmly along the tape line using hand pressure or a squeegee.
6. Ensure all edges are flat to eliminate tripping hazards from foot traffic.
7. Peel tape cleanly away upon project handover without leaving sticky residue.
