# Proctor BAL

**Proctor Mesh** — mesh insulation product.

Proctor BAL represents specialized bushfire ember protection mesh components and BAL-ready passive roof and wall ventilation products engineered by Proctor Group Australia. Comprising corrosion-resistant woven metal ember guard mesh with maximum aperture openings under 2mm, it integrates directly into ProctorVent over-fascia vents (FV10 BAL, FV25 BAL), ridge vents (RV10 BAL), and drainage battens (DB-FR BAL). Designed to meet Australian Standard AS 3959 for bushfire attack levels from BAL-12.5 up to BAL-40 and BAL-FZ, it prevents burning embers and debris from entering the roof space or wall cavity while maintaining continuous passive ventilation and condensation drainage.

## Key features

- Corrosion-resistant metal ember protection mesh with aperture size ≤ 2mm.
- Complies with Australian Standard AS 3959 for bushfire-prone areas up to BAL-40 / BAL-FZ.
- Pre-fitted as a BAL-ready option on ProctorVent Over-Fascia Vents (FV10 BAL, FV25 BAL).
- Integrated into ProctorVent Ridge Vents (RV10 BAL) for natural convection roof venting.
- Available as integrated BAL ember protection in ProctorPassive DB-FR drainage battens.
- Prevents wind-borne burning embers, leaves, and large insects from entering roof cavities.
- Maintains calculated, unimpeded passive airflow to exhaust moisture and prevent condensation.
- Non-combustible metal mesh construction resisting high ember attack temperatures.
- Durable, rot-proof, and designed for the lifetime of the roofing structure.

**Selection checklist**

1. Confirm designated Bushfire Attack Level (BAL-12.5, BAL-19, BAL-29, or BAL-40).
2. Select vent model (FV10 for roof pitch >22°; FV25 for pitch <22° and cathedral roofs).
3. Ensure the order specifies the 'BAL Ready Option' with pre-fitted metal mesh.
4. Calculate total linear meterage of eaves and ridges requiring ventilation.

## Limitations and warnings

- Mesh openings must be kept clear of paint, sealant, and construction dust during building works.
- Do not install without roof underlay; designed as part of a complete roof envelope system.
- Ensure all fascia and ridge junctions are tightly fitted with no bypass gaps greater than 2mm.

## Installation overview

1. Select ProctorVent BAL-ready vents pre-fitted with ≤ 2mm metal mesh.
2. For eaves ventilation, fix FV10 BAL or FV25 BAL over the top edge of the fascia board prior to installing roof sheeting or gutters.
3. For ridge ventilation, install RV10 BAL continuously along the roof ridge over the cut roof underlay.
4. Ensure the BAL mesh completely covers all ventilation openings with zero gaps exceeding 2mm.
5. Secure vents mechanically into timber or metal fascia and rafters using non-corrosive fasteners.
6. Fix roof cladding over the assembly ensuring the ember-guarded ventilation pathway remains open and unobstructed.
