# ProctorWrap Plasti-Grip Washers

**Proctor Wrap** — reflective membrane for weather protection and condensation control.

The ProctorWrap PlastiGrip is a 45mm diameter polypropylene washer designed for the mechanical attachment of building wraps and rigid insulation boards to timber and steel framing. Engineered with a flat base, flexible perimeter, and a 5mm deep cupped head, it distributes clamping force over a wide area to eliminate tear-out and wind blow-offs compared to traditional staples. Tested in conjunction with ProctorWrap and ProctorPassive membranes for calculating fixing span tables, it provides a weather-tight, airtight mechanical anchor for high-wind building envelopes.

## Key features

- 45mm wide diameter distributes clamping force to prevent membrane tearing.
- 5mm deep cupped head seals the washer to the substrate and protects fastener heads.
- Flexible outer perimeter reduces risk of abrasion damage to building wraps.
- Flat base design ensures flush mounting under claddings and battens.
- Superior alternative to staples, greatly reducing wind blow-off and pull-out risk.
- Compatible with countersunk screws (8g-10g) for steel frames and flat-head clouts for timber.
- Tested with ProctorWrap CW, HTS, and ProctorPassive RS-IT for fixing span tables.
- Allows fastener spacing to be increased to 600mm horizontal by 750mm vertical (4 per m²).
- Manufactured from durable, non-corrosive polypropylene material.

## Limitations and warnings

- Do not over-tighten fasteners; excessive torque can crack the washer or distort the substrate.
- Not recommended for permanent exposure to direct sunlight after installation.
- Cladding must be installed within the UV exposure window of the underlying membrane.

## Installation overview

1. Position building wrap or rigid insulation board flat against timber or steel wall framing.
2. Insert 8g-10g countersunk screw (for steel) or flat-head clout nail (for timber) through the PlastiGrip washer center.
3. Fasten through the membrane into the structural stud at recommended 600mm horizontal by 750mm vertical spacing.
4. Tighten screws only sufficiently to apply clamping force around the perimeter of the washer.
5. Do not over-tighten to avoid distorting the washer, membrane, or substrate.
6. If a washer is damaged or cracked as a result of over-tightening, replace it prior to project completion.
7. Use corrosion-resistant screws or galvanised nails.
