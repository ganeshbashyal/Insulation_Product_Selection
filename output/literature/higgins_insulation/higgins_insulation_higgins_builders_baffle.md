# Higgins Insulation Higgins Builders Baffle

**Higgins Insulation Batt** — bulk insulation batts for thermal and acoustic performance.

Higgins Builders Acoustic Baffle for Party Walls is a high-density, custom-manufactured polyester sound absorber engineered to stop sound transfer through ceiling voids above party walls. Manufactured in Queensland, Australia from 100% thermally bonded polyester fibers containing up to 85% recycled PET, it acts as a resilient acoustic barrier in multi-residential inter-tenancy wall-to-roof junctions. Safe and itch-free to install without protective clothing, it provides high sound transmission loss and will not slump or deteriorate over time.

## Key features

- Custom high-density polyester sound absorber engineered for party walls.
- Controls flanking noise traveling through ceiling cavities over inter-tenancy walls.
- Made from 100% polyester fiber with up to 85% recycled PET content.
- Non-allergenic, non-toxic, and itch-free with no glass fibers or chemical dust.
- Free from formaldehyde, phenol, and ammonia binders.
- Zero Ozone Depleting Potential (ODP) and negligible VOCs.
- Naturally moisture resistant and hydrophobic; will not rot or grow mold.
- Tested for fire safety in accordance with AS/NZS 1530.3.
- Approved by Asthma Foundations Australia for low-allergen buildings.

## Limitations and warnings

- Indoor ceiling space use only; not for direct external weather exposure.
- Does not substitute for fire-rated stonewool where a specific FRL is required by structural engineers.
- Ensure proper clearances around non-IC rated light fittings and hot flues.

## Installation overview

1. Measure the ceiling cavity void above the fire-rated party wall or inter-tenancy partition.
2. Position Builders Acoustic Baffle directly above the wall line spanning from the top plate to the underside of roof cladding.
3. Ensure the baffle compresses snugly against rafters, purlins, and ceiling linings without gaps.
4. Overlap or butt adjoining baffle blocks tightly together to eliminate acoustic flanking paths.
5. Secure in place using friction-fit pressure or mechanical fasteners/strapping where required.
6. Ensure baffle does not obstruct mandatory roof space ventilation pathways.
