# Higgins Insulation Roll

**Higgins Insulation Roll** — roll insulation product.

Higgins Polyester Sound Blankets (Polyester Acoustic Partition Rolls) are lightweight, continuous acoustic insulation rolls designed as an economical solution to reduce unwanted noise transfer through walls, floors, and ceilings. Manufactured in Queensland from 100% thermally bonded polyester fiber containing 80% recycled PET packaging materials, they provide high tensile strength, robustness, and resistance to moisture. Itch-free and requiring no protective clothing, they deliver safe acoustic sound absorption and thermal resistance across residential and commercial buildings.

## Key features

- Continuous roll format for fast, joint-free installation in walls, ceilings, and underfloors.
- Made from 100% polyester fiber with 80% recycled material from plastic packaging.
- Completely itch-free with no formaldehyde binders or toxic dust.
- Unaffected by moisture, hydrophobic, and naturally quick-drying.
- High tensile strength and robustness prevents tearing and sagging.
- Zero Ozone Depleting Potential (ODP) and negligible VOC emissions.
- Meets Australian Standard AS/NZS 1530.3 for fire safety.
- Available in widths of 450mm and 610mm across thicknesses from 50mm to 100mm.
- Excellent sound absorption for light-to-medium noise reduction.

## Applications and selection

- wall
- internal wall

**Selection checklist**

1. Select roll width (450mm or 610mm) to match framing spacing.
2. Choose thickness (50mm to 100mm) based on acoustic and cavity depth requirements.
3. Calculate required square meterage to order roll quantities.
4. Confirm preference for continuous roll installation over individual batts.

## Installation overview

1. Measure stud or joist bay width (450mm or 610mm).
2. Unroll the polyester sound blanket vertically or horizontally along framing bays.
3. Friction-fit between timber or steel studs, or secure to floor joists from below.
4. Cut roll to length using a sharp utility knife or heavy-duty scissors.
5. Ensure continuous coverage with tightly butted ends and no gaps.
6. Enclose behind wall, ceiling, or floor linings promptly after installation.
