# Metecno Board

**Metecno Board** — rigid insulation boards for continuous thermal performance.

Quality insulation engineered for Australian conditions. Highest R-value per millimetre means more thermal performance in thinner profiles. Perfect when wall depth is limited.

## Key features

- Premium PIR rigid foam with excellent thermal efficiency.
- highest R-value per unit thickness in the market.
- fire-modified polyisocyanurate core.
- zero ODP, low GWP blowing agents.
- rigid design prevents sagging between joists.
- reduces drafts and cold floors.
- excellent thermal performance for roof/ceiling applications.
- helps meet NCC energy efficiency requirements.
