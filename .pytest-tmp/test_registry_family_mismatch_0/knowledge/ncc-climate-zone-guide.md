# NCC climate-zone and wall-wrap guide

Last reviewed: 2026-09-04  
Provision basis: NCC 2022 ABCB Housing Provisions, clauses 10.8.1 and 10.8.3

## Use and limitation

This is a conversation and product-screening aid. It is not a compliance certificate or a substitute for the project energy report, condensation analysis, building classification, relevant NCC volume, state/territory variations or a building certifier's decision.

The customer should provide the project suburb and postcode. Use the official ABCB Climate Map to confirm the exact zone because climate-zone boundaries do not follow state, postcode or local-government boundaries. The applicable NCC edition and transition rules must also be checked for the jurisdiction and approval date.

## Rules the bot must preserve

1. Do not infer a definitive climate zone from a state or broad city region. Confirm the address/postcode using the ABCB map.
2. Do not provide a universal roof, wall or floor R-value by climate zone. Required Total R-values depend on building class, construction, energy-compliance pathway, glazing/services trade-offs, jurisdiction and project documentation.
3. Membrane vapour class is not the same as water-barrier classification, duty, fire performance, UV exposure allowance or BAL suitability.
4. For zones 4–8, verify every layer outside the primary wall insulation—not only the branded wall wrap—where clause 10.8.1(2) applies.
5. If no pliable membrane is installed, clause 10.8.1(3) generally requires the primary water-control layer to be separated from water-sensitive materials by a drained cavity, except for its single-skin masonry/concrete exclusions.
6. Roof insulation location matters: ceiling-level insulation and roofline insulation produce different roof-space and condensation conditions.

## Product-family implications

- **Fletcher Vapawrap Residential Wall Wrap:** documented as Class 4, so it can meet the permeance threshold identified for zones 4–8 when the exact product, construction and installation fall within the supporting evidence. This does not prove whole-wall compliance.
- **Other Sisalation products:** do not assume permeability from the brand. Confirm the exact variant and TDS.
- **Foil-faced and reflective products:** many have low vapour permeance. Their location relative to the primary insulation requires condensation review, especially in zones 4–8.
- **Roof membranes and blankets:** distinguish vapour-permeable roof membrane, reflective layer, bulk blanket and ceiling batt before applying any zone guidance.
