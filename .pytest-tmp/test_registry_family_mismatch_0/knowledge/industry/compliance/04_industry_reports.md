## 1. About the AIIC

The **Affiliated Insulation Industry Coalition (AIIC)** is a collective of insulation trade associations:
- **AMBA** — Australian Modern Building Alliance (polymer/foam insulation)
- **IA** — Insulation Australasia (manufacturers)
- **ICANZ** — Insulation Council of Australia & New Zealand (suppliers)

Informed by the **2021/2024 Roadmap for quality control and safety in insulation installation** (signed by 40+ organisations).

---

## 2. Value of insulation (key statistics)

- Reduces Australia's annual emissions by up to **7.1 million tonnes CO₂e**.
- **$7–10 in benefits** returned for every $1 invested in vulnerable households.
- Energy efficiency + electrification = **14% + 26%** of Australia's emissions reductions at low cost.
- Insulation enables right-sizing of HVAC, batteries, and infrastructure.
- **8+ million Australian homes** built before mandatory energy efficiency requirements; many lack basic insulation.
- Ceiling insulation can save up to **45%** on heating/cooling costs.
- Insulated home can lose up to **42%** of heat through ceiling in winter (Bradford).

## 3. AIIC's 5 core recommendations

1. **Government programs/schemes** that increase insulation uptake.
2. **Comprehensive insulation retrofits** to all social housing (public + community).
3. **Regulatory interventions** — minimum energy efficiency standards, incl. minimum rental standards for insulation.
4. **Mandatory energy rating disclosure** when homes are sold.
5. **Improve compliance & enforcement** for insulation installation in new builds.

## 5. Policy Statement (Nov 2024) — 11 recommendations

Organised under themes:

**Energy Efficiency First principle**
1. Apply "Energy Efficiency First" to all building/energy policy.

**Insulation in government programs & schemes**
2. Include insulation in all energy efficiency programs.
3. Use insulation in government procurement.

**Insulation in social & community housing**
4. Comprehensive insulation retrofits to all social housing.

**Regulatory & compliance measures**
5. Minimum energy efficiency standards (incl. rental).
6. Mandatory energy rating disclosure at sale.
7. Improve compliance/enforcement for new builds.
8. Use certified installers + pre-installation electrical safety assessments.

**Training & certification for workforce**
9. Support EEC Certified Insulation Installer (CII) program.
10. National training standards for installers.
11. Digital tools for compliance monitoring & conformance.

## 6. Quality & safety (Roadmap priorities)

- **Certified installers** — EEC Certified Insulation Installer (CII) certification.
- **Pre-installation electrical safety assessment** — required before insulation work (per AS 3999 + AS/NZS 3000).
- **Product conformance** — insulation must meet AS/NZS 4859.1, AS 4200.1.
- **Digital tools** — monitor compliance, assess conformance.
- **Foil insulation safety** — electrically conductive; critical electrical safety protocols.
