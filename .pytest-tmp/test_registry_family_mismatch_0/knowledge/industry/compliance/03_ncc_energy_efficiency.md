## 2. 7-Star NatHERS requirement

- All new Class 1 buildings must achieve **minimum 7-star NatHERS rating** (raised from 6 stars in NCC 2022).
- Class 2 (apartments): average 7 stars across units, no individual unit below 6 stars.
- Achieved via **house energy rating software** (NatHERS-accredited) OR DTS provisions.

**Compliance paths for Class 1 (H6P1):**
1. House energy rating software (NatHERS) achieving ≥ 7 stars, OR
2. DTS: comply with Housing Provisions Part 13.2 (fabric), 13.3 (glazing/shading), 13.4 (sealing), 13.5 (ceiling fans).

## 3. Building fabric — Housing Provisions Part 13.2

### 3.1 General requirements (13.2.2)
- Insulation must comply with **AS/NZS 4859.1** and be installed per AS 3999.
- Insulation must maintain position and thickness (not compressed except where necessary).
- Ceiling insulation must overlap wall by ≥ 50 mm where no bulk/reflective insulation in wall beneath.

### 3.2 Thermal breaks (13.2.3(7), 13.2.5(5))
- Required for **steel-framed** construction to mitigate thermal bridging.
- NCC 2022 introduced explicit thermal break requirements.

### 3.3 Total R-Value calculation
- Total R-Value must be calculated per **AS/NZS 4859.2** (accounts for thermal bridging).
- AS/NZS 4859.2 uses parallel-path (upper limit) + series-path (lower limit) method; design value = geometric mean.

### 3.4 Slab edge insulation (13.2.6)
- **Mandatory in climate zones 7 & 8:** R0.64 (zone 7), R1.0 (zone 8).
- Any slab with in-slab heating/cooling: minimum R1.0 perimeter edge insulation.

### 3.5 Ceiling insulation — minimum R-values by climate zone

Ceiling R-value depends on: climate zone, roof ventilation (vented vs standard), reflective insulation under roof, under-roof insulation R-value, and **solar absorptance (SA)** of roof surface.

**Climate zone descriptions:**
| Zone | Description | Key cities |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| 1 | Hot humid summer, warm winter | Darwin, Cairns, Townsville |
| 2 | Warm humid summer, mild winter | Brisbane, Mackay, Coffs Harbour |
| 3 | Hot dry summer, warm winter | Alice Springs, Mt Isa, Charleville |
| 4 | Hot dry summer, cool winter | Broken Hill, Mildura, Dubbo, Tamworth |
| 5 | Warm temperate | Sydney, Perth, Adelaide, Newcastle |
| 6 | Mild temperate | Melbourne, Canberra, Ballarat |
| 7 | Cool temperate | Hobart, most of Tasmania |
| 8 | Alpine | Alpine VIC/NSW/TAS |

**Typical minimum ceiling R-value ranges (pitched roof, horizontal ceiling):**
| Zone | Typical ceiling R-value range |
|------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | R1.5–R4.5 (varies widely with SA, venting, reflective) |
| 2 | R2.5–R4.0 |
| 3 | R2.5–R4.0 |
| 4 | R3.0–R3.5 |
| 5 | R2.5–R3.0 |
| 6 | R3.0–R4.0 |
| 7 | R3.5–R5.0+ |
| 8 | R4.0–R5.0+ |

**Key rules:**
- R-value of **reflective insulation is NOT included** in the declared R-value of bulk insulation.
- R-values listed are for the **labelled, declared R-value**.
- **"X = not permitted"** in some cells (e.g., dark roofs with no reflective insulation in hot zones).
- **Solar absorptance (SA):** light roofs (SA ≤ 0.23) allow lower R-values; dark roofs (SA > 0.64) require higher R-values. Zones 1–5 limit roof SA to ≤ 0.64.

**Roof ventilation definitions:**
- **Vented** = one wind-driven roof ventilator per 50 m² ceiling area (with gable/eave/ridge vents), OR one powered ventilator per 200 m², OR tiled roof without sarking.
- **Standard** = not vented.

## 4. Walls & floors

### 4.1 External walls
- Minimum Total R-Value varies by climate zone (typically R2.0–R2.8 in zones 6–8).
- Thermal bridging (esp. steel framing) significantly reduces effective wall R-value.
- NCC 2022+ requires thermal breaks for steel framing.

### 4.2 Floors
- Suspended timber floors: minimum R-value by climate zone (typically R1.0–R2.0 in zones 6–8).
- Slab-on-ground edge insulation: mandatory in zones 7 & 8.

## 5. Section J (Volume One — commercial/apartments)

### 5.1 Key parts
- **J1** Energy efficiency (Performance Requirements)
- **J2** Energy efficiency (DTS)
- **J3** Elemental provisions for a sole-occupancy unit
- **J4** Building fabric (J4D3 thermal construction general; J4D4 walls; J4D6 roofs; J4D7 floors)
- **J5** Building sealing
- **J6** Air-conditioning and ventilation
- **J7** Artificial lighting and power
- **J8** Access for maintenance / monitoring

### 5.2 Building fabric (J4D3)
- Insulation must comply with AS/NZS 4859.1, tested and labelled accordingly.
- Total R-Value / Total System U-Value must be calculated per **AS/NZS 4859.2** (roof/floor), Specification 37 (wall-glazing), or Specification 39 / CIBSE Guide A (soil/sub-floor).
- Thermal bridging must be accounted for.

### 5.3 Compliance methods (Section J)
- **DTS** (elemental provisions)
- **JV3** (verification using reference building — whole-building energy modelling)
- **Performance Solution**

## 6. Ductwork & HVAC insulation (Part 13.7 / J6)

- Ductwork carrying conditioned air must be insulated to minimum material R-values.
- Refrigerated cooling ductwork needs a **vapour barrier** (condensation).
- 11 mm polyurethane typically provides R0.4 for fittings.
- AS/NZS 4859.1 confirms in-situ values.

## 7. Whole-of-Home Energy Budget (NCC 2022, residential)

In addition to the 7-star thermal (shell) rating, NCC 2022 introduced a **Whole-of-Home energy budget** for Class 1 and Class 2 buildings.

**What it covers:** heating/cooling, hot water, artificial lighting, swimming pool/spa pumps, minus on-site solar PV generation.

**Scoring (out of 100):**
- Class 1 (houses): minimum score **60**
- Class 2 (apartments): minimum score **50**
- Score 100 = net-zero energy home; under 40 = poor performer.

**NatHERS software tools (4 approved):** BERS Pro, AccuRate Home, HERO (Home Energy Rating Optimisation), FirstRate5.

**Compliance paths:**
1. NatHERS software (star rating + Whole-of-Home score), OR
2. DTS elemental provisions + separate Whole-of-Home calculator.

**Key relationship:** a higher building shell (star) rating reduces heating/cooling load, which raises the Whole-of-Home score. Insulation directly improves both.

**State variations (QLD QDC 4.1):** 7 stars (most homes), 5 stars (eligible small homes), 6 stars (raised floor construction).

## 8. NCC 2025 Commercial Energy Efficiency Changes (Section J)

**Key point:** NCC 2025 made **major changes to commercial buildings (Volume One) but NO changes to residential (Volume Two)**. NCC 2022 was the reverse (residential changes).

**Scope:** Applies to Class 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 buildings. **Class 2 (apartments) NOT affected** — still uses NCC 2022 Amendment 2 Section J.

### 8.1 Halved energy allowances (J1P1)

| Building class | NCC 2022 | NCC 2025 | GHG limit (NCC 2025) |
|----------------|----------|----------|----------------------|
| Class 6 (office) | 80 kJ/m².hr | **40 kJ/m².hr** | 4 g CO₂-e/m².hr |
| Class 5, 7b, 8, 9a (non-ward), 9b school | 43 kJ/m².hr | **22 kJ/m².hr** | 2 g CO₂-e/m².hr |
| All other | 15 kJ/m².hr | **8 kJ/m².hr** | 1 g CO₂-e/m².hr |

### 8.2 Solar PV mandate (J9D5)
- NCC 2025 requires **actual installation of solar PV** (not just providing space), covering 100% or a substantial portion of available rooftop area.

### 8.3 Building fabric changes (J4)
- **Roof/ceiling insulation** now depends on **building class + climate zone** (not just climate zone).
- **Façade (wall + glazing) stricter** for Class 5, 6, 7, 8, 9b (and 9a non-ward) — 5–20% stricter across zones (except zone 8 unchanged).
- **Wall insulation increased** — up to 157% increase in zone 8; ≥43% in zones 3, 4, 7, 8.
- **Window tint/SHGC increased** — solar admittance reduced 9–72% (more tinted glass).
- **New thermal emittance rules for roofs** (metal roofs with rooftop AC equipment).
- **New defined terms:** Solar Reflectance Index (SRI), Total Solar Reflectance.
- **Floors:** no change.

### 8.4 Verification method changes
- **J1V1 (NABERS Energy):** higher star rating targets; room temperature modelling replaces PMV for Class 2, 3, 6.
- **J1V2 (Green Star):** no changes.
- **J1V3 (was JV3):** 3% GHG improvement for building services; **10% total annual GHG** improvement; new **98th percentile rule**; PMV only for Class 5; removes ability to offset envelope with onsite PV.
- **J1V4 (sealing):** no changes.

### 8.5 Other changes
- **J6:** enhanced HVAC requirements (chillers, heat pumps, fans, pumps, cooling towers).
- **J7:** broader lighting control requirements.
- **J8:** future electrification — gas systems designed for replacement with electrical capacity.
- **J9:** optional energy monitoring pathway (Environmental Management Information System).
- **Emission factors reduced** (e.g., VIC electricity 279 → 135 kgCO₂/GJ).

### 8.6 Section J compliance costs (practical)
- **DTS assessment:** $2,000–$5,000 (1–2 weeks turnaround).
- **JV3/J1V3 modelling:** $5,000–$15,000+ (3–6 weeks).
- Engage the energy assessor at **concept stage** (not after drawings) to avoid costly redesign.
