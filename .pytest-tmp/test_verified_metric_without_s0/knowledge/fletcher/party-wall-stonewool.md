# Fletcher Protect Party Wall Stonewool

## Canonical description

This family groups Fletcher Protect stonewool components used in specified party-wall/fire/acoustic systems, including party-wall batts, foil-faced stonewool blanket and Fire Stop party-wall batts. These are not one interchangeable SKU. Their role depends on the proprietary/tested wall detail, location within the system, thickness, density, facing and required fire/acoustic performance.

## System rule

Recommend only as a family when the customer has a party-wall system or recognised detail. Never promise an FRL, Rw/Rw+Ctr or BAL result from the insulation component alone. Plasterboard, shaftliner, framing, junctions, roof-space continuation, penetrations, cavity barriers and workmanship determine the system result.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Measure the vertical cavity width or top-of-wall gap between the separating
wall and roof cladding.
2. Select batts or blanket ensuring thickness allows a
minimum of 20% compression when installed.
3. For vertical party wall cavities,
friction-fit 100mm x 168mm batts tightly between wall leaves.
4. Butt consecutive
batts tightly with Vee cuts at joining ends to form continuous fire seal without
voids.
5. For top-of-wall junctions, lay blanket along the top of party wall
framing beneath roof battens and cladding.
6. Fasten roof sheeting down over
blanket ensuring minimum 20% compression beneath corrugations.
7. Pack stonewool
firmly around all pipe penetrations and services crossing the party wall line.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- Must be installed with at least 20% compression to ensure fire
seal integrity.
- Not designed as primary weatherproofing; must be sheltered by
roof cladding or wall linings.
- Protect from continuous pooling water during
construction.
