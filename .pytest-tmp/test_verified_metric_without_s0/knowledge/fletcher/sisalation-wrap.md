# Sisalation Wrap and Foil Membrane Families

## Canonical description

This catalogue family contains several distinct membranes, including products such as Tuff Wrap and Multipurpose. They differ in duty, permeability, water-barrier role, reflective facing, application and exposure. “Sisalation” is a brand/range label, not enough information for product selection.

## Selection workflow

First identify wall, roof, shed or other application. Then determine required vapour classification, water-barrier duty, reflective airspace, strength/duty, UV exposure, wind region and fire/BAL construction. Use the 2025 selection guide and exact TDS to choose the subfamily. Keep Vapawrap Wall and Vapawrap Metal Roof as separate dedicated records.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Install horizontally on exterior wall
studs or over roof rafters with anti-glare face facing outward.
2. Overlap upper
layers over lower layers by minimum 150mm to ensure water shedding.
3. Secure to
timber frame using broad-headed galvanized clouts or staples; use self-drilling
screws for steel.
4. Fix at maximum 300mm centres to studs and plates to
withstand wind pressure.
5. Ensure a minimum 20mm air cavity is maintained
adjacent to the reflective face to achieve rated thermal R-values.
6. Seal all
overlaps, edges, and penetrations with Vapastop 883 aluminium foil tape.
7. Ensure wrap is protected by external cladding within manufacturer UV exposure
limits.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- Standard 497 is not
suitable in cold climate zones where vapour-permeable Class 4 wraps are
required.
- Breather 497 is not suitable where a strict water barrier is
required under lightweight cladding in high rainfall.
- Must not be left exposed
to direct sunlight beyond recommended exposure window.
