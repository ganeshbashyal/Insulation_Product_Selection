# Pink Thermal Slab

## Canonical description and range

Pink Thermal Slab is semi-rigid foil-faced glasswool for suitable soffits, concrete floors, walls and roof applications. The current Fletcher range lists R1.5 at 50 mm, R2.0 at 68 mm, R2.2 at 75 mm and R3.0 at 100 mm. These are material R-values; the completed slab/soffit construction has a separate Total R-value.

## Selection logic

Use where a robust semi-rigid faced board is required against concrete or in a specified commercial construction. FI32 is the broader fabrication/HVAC family with multiple facings; Pink Batts are cavity batts; Permastop is a flexible roof blanket. Confirm whether the foil faces an airspace, is a vapour control layer, or is simply protective—those functions are not interchangeable.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Clean and
inspect underside of cured concrete slab, ensuring surface is dry and free of
release oils.
2. Mark grid lines on soffit to align board joints neatly across
the ceiling.
3. Position Pink Thermal Slab with foil facing downward (exposed)
against the concrete soffit.
4. Fasten mechanically using approved insulation
anchors with large retaining washers (minimum 5 to 6 fixings per board).
5. Locate fixings approximately 100mm from edges and evenly distributed across the
board center.
6. Butt boards tightly together to eliminate thermal bridging.
7. Tape all exposed foil butt joints with 72mm reinforced aluminium foil tape to
form a continuous vapour barrier.
8. Neatly cut boards around service pipes,
sprinklers, and penetrations using a sharp serrated knife.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- Not
designed for external weather exposure without protective cladding.
- Must be
mechanically fixed; adhesive alone is not recommended for overhead soffit
installations.
- Avoid puncturing the foil facing during maintenance to preserve
the vapour barrier.
