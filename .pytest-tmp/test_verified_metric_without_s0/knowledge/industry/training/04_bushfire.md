# Bushfire (AS 3959) — Insulation Requirements

*Insulation considerations for construction in bushfire-prone areas, per AS 3959:2018.*

---

## 1. Bushfire Attack Level (BAL)

AS 3959 classifies bushfire risk into six BAL levels:

| BAL | Description | Radiant heat exposure |
|-----|-------------|----------------------|
| **BAL-LOW** | Very low risk | < 12.5 kW/m² |
| **BAL-12.5** | Low risk | 12.5 kW/m² |
| **BAL-19** | Moderate risk | 19 kW/m² |
| **BAL-29** | High risk | 29 kW/m² |
| **BAL-40** | Very high risk | 40 kW/m² |
| **BAL-FZ** | Flame Zone (extreme) | > 40 kW/m² + direct flame |

---

## 2. Insulation & bushfire

### Non-combustible insulation (preferred)
- **Glasswool** — non-combustible, suitable for all BAL levels.
- **Rockwool** — non-combustible, highest temperature resistance (~1200°C), ideal for BAL-FZ.

### Combustible insulation (restricted)
- **Polystyrene (EPS/XPS)** — combustible, restricted at higher BAL levels.
- **Polyester** — combustible, restricted at higher BAL levels.
- **PIR/phenolic** — combustible (foil-faced), restricted at higher BAL levels.

### Membranes & sarking
- Must meet specific **flammability indices** for BAL-rated construction.
- Ember protection critical — gaps and openings are the main entry points.

---

## 3. Key principles

1. **Non-combustible insulation** (glasswool/rockwool) is generally suitable for all BAL levels.
2. **Combustible insulation** may be restricted or prohibited at BAL-29 and above.
3. **Ember attack** (not direct flame) causes most house losses — seal all gaps and openings.
4. **Sarking/membranes** must meet flammability requirements for the BAL level.
5. **Verify** the specific BAL for the site (local council, AS 3959 assessment).

---

## 4. Material suitability by BAL (general guidance)

| Material | BAL-LOW | BAL-12.5 | BAL-19 | BAL-29 | BAL-40 | BAL-FZ |
|----------|---------|----------|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Glasswool | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Rockwool | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Polyester | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ⚠ | ✗ | ✗ |
| EPS/XPS | ✓ | ✓ | ⚠ | ✗ | ✗ | ✗ |
| PIR/phenolic | ✓ | ✓ | ⚠ | ⚠ | ✗ | ✗ |
| Reflective foil | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ⚠ | ⚠ | ⚠ |

*⚠ = restricted/conditional; ✗ = generally not permitted. Always verify against current AS 3959 and NCC requirements for the specific BAL.*
