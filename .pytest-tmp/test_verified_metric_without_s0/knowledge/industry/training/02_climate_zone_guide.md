# Climate Zone Guide — Detailed Insulation Strategy per Zone

*Detailed insulation and building envelope strategy for each of the 8 NCC climate zones.*

---

## Zone 1 — Hot humid summer, warm winter

**Cities:** Darwin, Cairns, Townsville, Broome.

**Climate:** High temp year-round, high humidity, low diurnal range, wet season. Cooling-dominated.

**Insulation strategy:**
- **Reflective insulation is primary** — reject radiant heat (foil under roof, sarking).
- Bulk insulation only where air-conditioned (avoid trapping heat in unconditioned homes).
- Use **highly breathable reflective vapour barriers** in walls.
- Ventilate roof spaces in summer; seal in winter.
- Light-coloured roofs (SA ≤ 0.23) critical.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R1.5–R3.0 (with reflective), wall R1.5–R2.0.

**Key risks:** inward vapour drive (humid exterior) — use lower-permeance exterior layers; mould from trapped humidity.

---

## Zone 2 — Warm humid summer, mild winter

**Cities:** Brisbane, Mackay, Coffs Harbour.

**Climate:** Warm humid summer, mild winter, some heating in winter. Mixed cooling/heating.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Balanced bulk + reflective.
- Ceiling R2.5–R4.0.
- Wall R2.0–R2.5.
- Reflective foil under roof for radiant heat.
- Ventilation to manage humidity.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R2.5–R4.0, wall R2.0–R2.5, floor R1.0.

---

## Zone 3 — Hot dry summer, warm winter

**Cities:** Alice Springs, Mt Isa, Charleville.

**Climate:** Hot dry summer, warm winter, high diurnal range, low humidity.

**Insulation strategy:**
- **High thermal mass + insulation** — exploit diurnal range.
- Ceiling R2.5–R4.0.
- Wall R2.0–R2.5.
- Light-coloured roofs.
- Insulate internal walls from external thermal mass.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R2.5–R4.0, wall R2.0–R2.5.

**Key point:** high diurnal range makes thermal mass very effective here.

---

## Zone 4 — Hot dry summer, cool winter

**Cities:** Broken Hill, Mildura, Dubbo, Tamworth, Albury-Wodonga.

**Climate:** Hot dry summer, cool winter, high diurnal range. Both heating and cooling.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Ceiling R3.0–R3.5.
- Wall R2.0–R2.5.
- Thermal mass + insulation.
- **Condensation:** Class 3 vapour-permeable membrane (≥0.143 µg/N·s).
- Light roofs.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R3.0–R3.5, wall R2.0–R2.5, floor R1.0–R1.5.

---

## Zone 5 — Warm temperate

**Cities:** Sydney, Perth, Adelaide, Newcastle, Wollongong.

**Climate:** Warm temperate, mild winters, warm summers. Moderate heating + cooling.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Ceiling R2.5–R3.0 (minimum; 7-star may need R4.0–R5.0).
- Wall R2.0–R2.5.
- **Condensation:** Class 3 vapour-permeable membrane (≥0.143 µg/N·s).
- Balanced approach — manage both summer heat gain and winter heat loss.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R2.5–R3.0 (DTS), wall R2.0–R2.5, floor R1.0.

**Note:** Sydney = Zone 5. Most common NCC zone for Australian population.

---

## Zone 6 — Mild temperate

**Cities:** Melbourne, Canberra, Ballarat, Albany.

**Climate:** Mild temperate, cool winters, warm summers. Heating-dominated.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Ceiling R3.0–R4.0.
- Wall R2.0–R2.5+ (Total R-Value).
- **Condensation:** Class 4 vapour-permeable membrane (≥1.14 µg/N·s); NCC 2025 requires drained & ventilated cavity.
- Higher insulation for heating.
- Slab edge insulation recommended.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R3.0–R4.0, wall R2.0–R2.5, floor R1.5–R2.0.

**Key point:** Melbourne = Zone 6. Highest condensation risk of major cities.

---

## Zone 7 — Cool temperate

**Cities:** Hobart, most of Tasmania, Glen Innes.

**Climate:** Cool temperate, cold winters, mild summers. Strongly heating-dominated.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Ceiling R3.5–R5.0+.
- Wall R2.5–R2.8+.
- **Slab edge insulation mandatory** (R0.64).
- **Condensation:** Class 4 membrane + drained & ventilated cavity.
- Maximum bulk insulation.

**Typical levels:** ceiling R3.5–R5.0, wall R2.5–R2.8, floor R2.0, slab edge R0.64.

**Key point:** Hobart = Zone 7. Use Tasmanian Designers' Guide (CBOS) for condensation.

---

## Zone 8 — Alpine

**Cities:** Alpine VIC/NSW/TAS (e.g., Thredbo, Mt Buller).

**Climate:** Alpine, cold winters with snow, warm summers. Extreme heating demand.

**Insulation strategy:**
- Ceiling R4.0–R5.0+.
- Wall R2.5–R2.8+.
- **Slab edge insulation mandatory** (R1.0).
- **Condensation:** Class 4 membrane + drained & ventilated cavity.
- Maximum insulation everywhere; highly insulated lightweight floors.
- Dark roofs acceptable (solar gain helps winter).

**Typical levels:** ceiling R4.0–R5.0+, wall R2.5–R2.8+, floor R2.0+, slab edge R1.0.
