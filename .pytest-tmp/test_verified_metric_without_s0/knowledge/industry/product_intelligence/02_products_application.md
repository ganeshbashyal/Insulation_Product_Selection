# Product Intelligence — Application-Specific

*Organised by building application. For each application: recommended products, R-values, key manufacturers, and selection notes.*

---

## 1. Ceiling Insulation (flat ceiling, attic space)

**Purpose:** Primary thermal barrier (up to 45% of heating/cooling loss through ceiling).

| Product | Manufacturer | R-Value range | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Earthwool Ceiling | Knauf | R3.5–R8.0 | Highest residential R-values; ECOSE/TwinTech |
| Bradford Gold Ceiling | CSR Bradford | R2.5–R6.0 | Flagship; Comfort Touch |
| Pink Batts Ceiling | Fletcher | R1.5–R7.0 | Iconic pink |
| Bradford Polymax Ceiling | CSR Bradford | R1.5–R4.0 | Polyester option |
| GreenStuf Thermal | Autex | R1.5–R3.5 | Polyester (VIC) |

**Selection guidance:**
- Climate zones 1–3: R2.5–R4.0 (reflective under roof reduces bulk need)
- Climate zones 4–5: R3.0–R4.0 (Sydney/Perth/Adelaide)
- Climate zones 6–8: R4.0–R5.0+ (Melbourne/Hobart/Alpine)
- 7-star NatHERS may require R5.0–R8.0 depending on whole-of-home design
- Higher R-value = thicker batt (120–290mm)

## 2. External Wall Insulation (cavity walls)

**Purpose:** Thermal + acoustic; must account for thermal bridging (esp. steel frame).

| Product | Manufacturer | R-Value range | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------------|-------|
| Bradford Gold Wall | CSR Bradford | R2.0–R2.7 | Premium wall batt |
| Earthwool Wall | Knauf | R1.5–R2.8 | Thermal + acoustic |
| Pink Batts Wall | Fletcher | R1.5–R2.7 | High-density thermo-acoustic |
| Kooltherm K12 Framing Board | Kingspan | R1.10–R3.60 | Rigid phenolic, between studs |
| Bradford Polymax Wall | CSR Bradford | R1.5–R2.5 | Polyester |
| GreenStuf Thermal | Autex | R1.5–R2.5 | Polyester |

**Selection guidance:**
- 90mm stud: highest residential batt = R2.7
- Common wall R-values: R2.0–R2.5
- Steel frame: use thermal breaks + higher R to compensate bridging
- K12 framing board (R3.6 @ 80mm) replaces R2.5 glasswool → helps achieve 7-star without increasing ceiling insulation
- Pair with vapour-permeable wall wrap (Class 3/4 per climate zone)

## 3. Internal Wall / Partition Insulation (acoustic)

**Purpose:** Reduce airborne noise between rooms; NOT a thermal requirement.

| Product | Manufacturer | Density | Notes |
|---------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Bradford Soundscreen | CSR Bradford | High | Acoustic walls + midfloors |
| Pink Soundbreak | Fletcher | High | Acoustic batts |
| Pink Partition | Fletcher | Medium | Partition acoustic |
| Earthwool Sound Shield | Knauf | High | Acoustic |
| Earthwool Ultra Acoustic | Knauf | High | Premium acoustic |
| ROCKWOOL Safe'n'Sound | ROCKWOOL | High | Acoustic + fire |
| GreenStuf AAB | Autex | Variable | Acoustic panels |

**Selection guidance:**
- Party walls (Class 1): Rw+Ctr ≥ 50 (Type A) or Rw ≥ 45 (Type B)
- Higher density (30–80 kg/m³) = better mid-frequency absorption
- Must be installed during construction (not retrofittable)

**Selection guidance:**
