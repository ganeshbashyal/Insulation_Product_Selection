# Material Intelligence — Material-Specific & Application

*Organised by insulation material. For each material: physical properties, R-value per thickness, thermal/acoustic/fire/moisture behaviour, best applications, manufacturers, and compliance notes.*

---

## 1. Glasswool (Fibreglass)

**Description:** Millions of glass fibres bound into batts/rolls/blankets. Most widely used insulation in Australia.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R2.2–R2.8 (density-dependent) |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.032–0.044 W/mK |
| Combustibility | Non-combustible (AS 1530.1) |
| Fire hazard (AS/NZS 1530.3) | Ignitability 0, Spread 0, Heat 0, Smoke 0–1 |
| Vapour permeance | High (open-cell) |
| Moisture absorption | <5% by weight |
| Recycled content | Up to 80% recycled glass |
| Health | Bio-soluble fibres (FBS-1); mechanical irritation only |

**Key manufacturers:** Knauf Earthwool, CSR Bradford, Fletcher Pink Batts.

**Best applications:** Ceilings, walls, underfloor, HVAC ductwork, acoustic cavities.

**Pros:** Low cost, non-combustible, high recycled content, thermal + acoustic, widely available.
**Cons:** Can irritate skin (mechanical), loses R-value if compressed, moisture-sensitive if wetted.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1; FBS-1 bio-solubility (ICANZ); non-combustible.

---

## 2. Rockwool (Stone Wool)

**Description:** Fibres spun from molten basalt rock. Higher density and temperature resistance than glasswool.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R2.5–R3.0 |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.033–0.040 W/mK |
| Melting point | ~1200°C (vs ~600°C glasswool) |
| Combustibility | Non-combustible |
| Vapour permeance | High (open-cell) |
| Water resistance | Water repellent, doesn't rot/mould |
| Density | 40–80+ kg/m³ |

**Key manufacturers:** ROCKWOOL, Bradford Acoustigard.

**Best applications:** Fire-rated walls, acoustic partitions, commercial/industrial, single-skin roofs, HVAC high-temp, passive fire protection.

**Pros:** Superior fire resistance, excellent acoustic (high density), water-resistant, durable.
**Cons:** Higher cost, heavier, can irritate skin.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1; AS 1530.1 non-combustible; fire-rated assemblies.

---

## 3. Polyester

**Description:** Synthetic fibres (same material as pillows/doonas), thermally bonded into batts.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R1.8–R2.2 (lower than glasswool) |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.038–0.045 W/mK |
| Combustibility | Combustible (flame-retardant treated) |
| Vapour permeance | High (open-cell) |
| Moisture | Moisture-resistant, non-absorbent |
| Recycled content | Up to 50–80% recycled |
| Health | Non-irritant, non-allergenic, no VOCs |

**Key manufacturers:** CSR Bradford (Polymax), Autex (GreenStuf), Higgins, Polyester Solutions.

**Best applications:** Ceilings, walls, underfloor, midfloors; ideal where non-irritant handling matters (DIY, sensitive occupants).

**Pros:** Non-irritant, low allergen, easy to cut, moisture-resistant, recyclable, clean to work with.
**Cons:** Lower R per mm (needs more thickness), combustible, higher cost.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1; Sensitive Choice; Red List Chemical Free (Autex).

---

## 4. PIR (Polyisocyanurate) Rigid Board

**Description:** Rigid thermoset foam board, foil-faced both sides.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R4.5–R5.5 (highest per mm) |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.022–0.026 W/mK |
| Combustibility | Combustible (foil-faced, fire-retardant) |
| Vapour permeance | Low (closed-cell + foil = vapour barrier) |
| Moisture | Closed-cell, water-resistant |

**Key manufacturers:** Kingspan (Therma), Pirmax.

**Best applications:** Walls (between studs or sheathing), roofs, floors, insulated plasterboard, where thin profile + high R needed.

**Pros:** Highest R per mm, thin profile, foil-faced (vapour barrier), rigid (no sag).
**Cons:** Higher cost, combustible, thermal drift (R declines slightly over time).

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1; CodeMark (Kingspan); reaction-to-fire (AS 5113).

---

## 5. Phenolic (Kooltherm)

**Description:** Advanced rigid thermoset phenolic foam — the thinnest common insulation board per R-value.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R4.5–R5.0 |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.021 W/mK (lowest common) |
| Combustibility | Combustible (foil-faced) |
| Vapour permeance | Low (closed-cell + foil) |

**Key manufacturer:** Kingspan (Kooltherm).

**Specific products:**
- K12 Framing Board: R1.10 (25mm) – R3.60 (80mm)
- K3 Floorboard: R1.10 (25mm), R2.30 (50mm)
- K8 Cavity Board, K17 Insulated Plasterboard

**Best applications:** Framing (between studs), floors, insulated plasterboard, 7-star walls.

**Pros:** Thinnest per R-value, fibre-free core, high performance.
**Cons:** Premium cost, combustible, needs performance solution for external application.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1; CodeMark; made in Australia.

---

## 6. XPS (Extruded Polystyrene)

**Description:** Closed-cell rigid foam, high compressive strength, waterproof.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R3.5–R4.0 |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.025–0.030 W/mK |
| Compressive strength | High (300–700 kPa) |
| Moisture | Waterproof (closed-cell) |
| Vapour permeance | Low |

**Key manufacturers:** Kingspan (GreenGuard), Foilboard (Slabmate), Foamex.

**Best applications:** Under-slab, slab edge, below-grade, inverted roofs, high-load areas.

**Pros:** Waterproof, high compressive strength, durable.
**Cons:** Higher cost, combustible, lower R than PIR/phenolic.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1.

---

## 7. EPS (Expanded Polystyrene)

**Description:** Bead foam, lower cost than XPS, some moisture absorption.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R2.8–R3.3 |
| Thermal conductivity (λ) | ~0.030–0.035 W/mK |
| Compressive strength | Lower than XPS |
| Moisture | Some absorption (bead structure) |

**Key manufacturers:** Foamex (Expol), Foilboard, Kingspan (Aerobord).

**Best applications:** Underfloor panels, cavity walls, slab, panel cores.

**Specific products:**
- Expol White R1.4, Expol Black R1.8 (graphite-infused, +30%)

**Pros:** Low cost, lightweight, DIY-friendly, recyclable.
**Cons:** Combustible, some moisture absorption, lower R than XPS/PIR.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1.

---

## 8. Reflective Foil Insulation (RFL)

**Description:** Foil-faced membranes/blankets that reflect radiant heat. Requires adjacent still airspace.

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Emissivity | ≤ 0.05 (low-e foil) |
| R-value | Depends on airspace, heat flow direction, temp difference |
| Vapour permeance | Low (foil = vapour barrier) |
| Electrical | Conductive (safety critical) |

**Key manufacturers:** Fletcher (Sisalation), Ametalin, Kingspan (Air-cell), Foilboard.

**Best applications:** Under metal roofs, sarking, sub-floor, hot climates (radiant heat rejection).

**Pros:** Excellent radiant heat rejection, lightweight, condensation control (foil blanket).
**Cons:** No bulk R-value (needs airspace), electrically conductive, vapour barrier (condensation risk in cool climates if misused).

**Compliance:** AS 4200.1 (membranes); AS/NZS 3000 (electrical safety); reflective R-value NOT added to bulk R-value.

---

## 9. Spray Foam (PU)

**Description:** Expanding polyurethane foam, applied in-situ. Open-cell or closed-cell.

| Property | Open-cell | Closed-cell |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| R-value per 100mm | ~R3.0 | ~R4.5 |
| Vapour permeance | High | Low (vapour barrier) |
| Air sealing | Excellent | Excellent |
| Structural | No | Some rigidity |

**Best applications:** Air sealing, irregular cavities, premium residential/commercial.

**Pros:** Seamless air seal, fills irregular spaces, high R (closed-cell).
**Cons:** Professional installation required, higher cost, combustible, niche in Australia.

**Compliance:** AS/NZS 4859.1 (where applicable).

---

## 10. Natural Insulation (Wool, Cellulose, Cotton/Denim)

| Material | R-value per 100mm | Notes |
|----------|-------------------|-------|
| Sheep's wool | ~R2.5–R3.0 | Hygroscopic (moisture-buffering), natural, premium |
| Cellulose (loose-fill) | ~R2.0–R3.5 | Recycled paper, borax-treated, can settle |
| Cotton/Denim | ~R2.0–R2.5 | Recycled, non-irritant, niche (Jeanius) |

**Best applications:** Eco-conscious builds, heritage, loose-fill retrofit.

**Pros:** Natural, high recycled content, moisture-buffering (wool).
**Cons:** Higher cost, combustible, cellulose can settle.
