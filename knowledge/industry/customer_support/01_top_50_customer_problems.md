# Top 50 Customer Problems & Solutions — Australian Insulation

*Supplier-facing troubleshooting playbook. Each problem includes: what the customer says, root cause, how to tackle it, and prevention. Organised by category.*

---

## A. Thermal Performance (Problems 1–8)

### Problem 1: "My house is freezing in winter"
**Root cause:** Under-insulated ceiling/walls, or insulation below NCC minimum for the climate zone. Heat loss through ceiling (up to 45%) and walls.
**How to tackle:** Check current R-value and climate zone. Recommend upgrading ceiling insulation (e.g., R2.5 → R5.0+ in Melbourne). Check walls and floors too. Explain that ceiling is the highest priority (biggest surface, hottest air rises).
**Prevention:** Specify correct R-value at build/renovation time per NCC + 7-star assessment.

### Problem 2: "My house is boiling in summer"
**Root cause:** Heat gain through ceiling (up to 70%), dark roof, no reflective insulation, poor ventilation.
**How to tackle:** Recommend reflective foil/sarking under roof, lighter roof colour (SA ≤ 0.23), higher ceiling R-value, and roof space ventilation. Explain radiant heat vs conduction.
**Prevention:** Light-coloured roof, reflective insulation in hot climates (zones 1–3).

### Problem 3: "My energy bills are still high after insulating"
**Root cause:** Insulation is only one part — gaps, thermal bridging, unsealed draughts, single-glazed windows, old HVAC.
**How to tackle:** Explain insulation addresses conduction, but air leakage (draughts) and glazing are separate. Recommend draught sealing, checking for gaps/compression in installed insulation, and window upgrades.
**Prevention:** Whole-of-home approach — insulation + sealing + glazing together.

### Problem 4: "Rooms above the garage are cold"
**Root cause:** Uninsulated floor over the garage (a common thermal bypass).
**How to tackle:** Recommend underfloor insulation (polyester or EPS) between the garage ceiling joists. Explain this is a separate zone from the main ceiling.
**Prevention:** Insulate floors over unconditioned spaces (garage, subfloor) at build time.

### Problem 5: "Ceiling insulation isn't working"
**Root cause:** Compressed batts, gaps, insufficient R-value, or insulation displaced by trades.
**How to tackle:** Inspect for compression (batts squashed under trusses), gaps, and missing sections. Recommend topping up or replacing to full thickness and correct R-value.
**Prevention:** Correct installation (no compression), protect insulation during construction.

### Problem 6: "New home still cold despite R4 ceiling"
**Root cause:** Wall insulation missing/inadequate, or thermal bridging (esp. steel frame), or air leakage.
**How to tackle:** Check wall insulation (R2.0–R2.5 for zones 5–6), thermal breaks on steel framing, and draught sealing. Explain Total R-value vs product R-value.
**Prevention:** Specify walls + thermal breaks, not just ceiling.

### Problem 7: "Insulation has settled/sagged"
**Root cause:** Loose-fill cellulose settling over time, or batts sagging in walls/underfloor due to poor install.
**How to tackle:** For loose-fill, top up to restore R-value. For sagging batts, re-secure or replace with rigid/self-supporting product (e.g., polyester, EPS panels).
**Prevention:** Use batts/rigid products rather than loose-fill where settling is a concern.

### Problem 8: "Downlights are making insulation ineffective"
**Root cause:** Recessed downlights create holes in the insulation + air leakage + fire risk.
**How to tackle:** Recommend IC-rated downlight covers or insulation-compatible fittings, maintain clearance, and explain the fire/electrical risk of covering non-rated lights.
**Prevention:** Specify insulation-compatible downlights and covers at design stage.

## B. Condensation & Moisture (Problems 9–18)

### Problem 9: "Mould on ceiling and walls"
**Root cause:** Condensation from high indoor humidity + cold surfaces + poor ventilation. Often worsened by airtight homes without ventilation.
**How to tackle:** Identify moisture source (cooking, drying clothes, unflued heaters). Recommend ventilation (exhaust fans, opening windows), and check insulation is keeping surfaces above dew point. Explain mould needs moisture + warmth + food.
**Prevention:** Balanced insulation + ventilation + moisture management.

### Problem 10: "Condensation on windows every morning"
**Root cause:** High indoor humidity condensing on cold glass (coldest surface).
**How to tackle:** Explain windows are the coldest surface — condensation there is normal but indicates high humidity. Recommend ventilation, reduce moisture sources, consider double glazing for comfort.
**Prevention:** Manage indoor humidity (exhaust fans, ventilation).

### Problem 11: "Water dripping from the roof/ceiling"
**Root cause:** Condensation under metal roof (not a leak) — warm moist air hitting cold roof sheeting.
**How to tackle:** Recommend foil-faced blanket (Anticon/Permastop) directly under metal roofing, and roof space ventilation. Distinguish condensation from an actual leak.
**Prevention:** Insulated foil blanket under metal roofs + ventilation.

### Problem 12: "Insulation is wet/damp"
**Root cause:** Roof leak, or condensation soaking the insulation.
**How to tackle:** Fix the source first (leak or condensation). Replace wet insulation (wet insulation loses 50–90% R-value and grows mould). Explain water vs air conductivity.
**Prevention:** Fix leaks, manage condensation, keep insulation dry.

### Problem 13: "Mould in wardrobe/corners"
**Root cause:** Cold spots from thermal bridging (corners, behind wardrobes) + stagnant air.
**How to tackle:** Improve insulation continuity, address thermal bridges, improve airflow (leave gaps behind furniture), reduce humidity.
**Prevention:** Continuous insulation, avoid thermal bridges, ventilate.

### Problem 14: "Condensation in the roof space"
**Root cause:** Unventilated roof space + moisture from living space rising through ceiling penetrations.
**How to tackle:** Recommend roof space ventilation (eaves/ridge/gable vents), seal ceiling penetrations, ensure exhaust fans vent to outside (not into roof space).
**Prevention:** Ventilated roof space + sealed ceiling + exhaust to outside.

### Problem 15: "Foil sarking is causing condensation"
**Root cause:** Foil sarking is a vapour barrier — in cool climates (zones 6–8) it traps moisture on the exterior side, causing interstitial condensation.
**How to tackle:** Replace with vapour-permeable membrane (Class 4, ≥1.14 µg/N·s) on the exterior side of insulation. Explain the NCC requirement.
**Prevention:** Use vapour-permeable membrane in cool climates, not foil sarking.

### Problem 16: "Mould appeared after I upgraded insulation"
**Root cause:** New insulation + airtightness trapped existing moisture; or vapour barrier installed on wrong side.
**How to tackle:** Check vapour management (membrane class + position), add ventilation, address moisture sources. Explain that insulation changes moisture dynamics.
**Prevention:** Balance insulation with ventilation + correct vapour management.

### Problem 17: "Damp subfloor / musty smell"
**Root cause:** Poor subfloor ventilation, moisture from ground, or blocked vents.
**How to tackle:** Ensure subfloor vents are clear, recommend ground vapour barrier, and moisture-resistant underfloor insulation (polyester/EPS). Don't block ventilation with insulation.
**Prevention:** Ventilated subfloor + ground moisture barrier.

### Problem 18: "Condensation on walls in bathroom/laundry"
**Root cause:** High-humidity rooms + cold walls + inadequate exhaust.
**How to tackle:** Recommend exhaust fans vented to outside, wall insulation, and moisture-resistant linings. Explain these rooms produce the most moisture.
**Prevention:** Exhaust ventilation + insulation in wet areas.

## C. Acoustic (Problems 19–26)

### Problem 19: "Can hear neighbours through the wall"
**Root cause:** Party wall below NCC acoustic requirement (Rw+Ctr < 50), or flanking/gaps.
**How to tackle:** Recommend acoustic insulation in cavity (Soundscreen, Soundbreak, rockwool), check for gaps/penetrations, consider discontinuous construction for new builds. Explain Rw+Ctr.
**Prevention:** Acoustic-grade insulation + discontinuous construction in party walls.

### Problem 20: "Can hear footsteps from upstairs"
**Root cause:** Impact sound through floor — no resilient layer/floating floor.
**How to tackle:** Explain impact vs airborne sound. Recommend resilient underlay, floating floor, or acoustic ceiling below. Carpet + underlay is the classic fix.
**Prevention:** Impact-rated floor system (floating floor + resilient layer) at build.

### Problem 21: "Traffic noise through windows"
**Root cause:** Glazing, not insulation — single-glazed windows are the weak point for external noise.
**How to tackle:** Explain NCC doesn't regulate external noise, and insulation won't fix window noise. Recommend double/secondary glazing, acoustic seals.
**Prevention:** Acoustic glazing for noisy sites.

### Problem 22: "Noise between rooms in my house"
**Root cause:** Internal walls uninsulated (standard practice — internal walls often have no insulation).
**How to tackle:** Recommend acoustic batts in internal walls (esp. bedrooms, home office, media room). Explain this must be done during construction/renovation.
**Prevention:** Insulate internal walls at build for acoustic comfort.

### Problem 23: "Can hear plumbing/toilet noise"
**Root cause:** Pipes uninsulated, or wall cavity transmits pipe noise.
**How to tackle:** Recommend pipe lagging/insulation around pipes, acoustic insulation in walls near plumbing, and sealing penetrations.
**Prevention:** Insulate pipes + acoustic walls near wet areas.

### Problem 24: "Home theatre bass disturbing neighbours"
**Root cause:** Low-frequency sound (bass) is hardest to block — needs mass + decoupling.
**How to tackle:** Explain bass needs mass (double plasterboard) + decoupling (discontinuous construction), not just cavity batts. Manage expectations — bass is very hard to fully contain.
**Prevention:** Dedicated acoustic treatment for media rooms (mass + decoupling).

### Problem 25: "Echo/reverberation in the room"
**Root cause:** Sound absorption issue (not insulation) — hard surfaces reflect sound.
**How to tackle:** Explain absorption vs insulation. Recommend acoustic panels/absorbing materials (Quietspace, AAB), soft furnishings, rugs.
**Prevention:** Acoustic treatment for large/hard rooms.

### Problem 26: "Noise through ceiling from upstairs apartment"
**Root cause:** Floor/ceiling system below NCC requirement (Rw+Ctr < 50, Ln,w > 62).
**How to tackle:** Recommend acoustic ceiling system below, resilient mounts, and impact-rated floor above. Explain the combined floor/ceiling approach.
**Prevention:** Tested floor/ceiling system meeting NCC at construction.

## D. Product Selection (Problems 27–34)

### Problem 27: "Which R-value do I need?"
**Root cause:** Customer doesn't know their climate zone or NCC requirements.
**How to tackle:** Ask location → determine climate zone → recommend R-value (ceiling R2.5–R5.0+ depending on zone). Explain 7-star may need more. Offer to check with energy assessor.
**Prevention:** Provide climate zone R-value guide.

### Problem 28: "Glasswool vs polyester vs rockwool?"
**Root cause:** Customer confused by material options.
**How to tackle:** Explain trade-offs — glasswool (cheap, non-combustible, slightly itchy), polyester (non-irritant, moisture-resistant, lower R/mm), rockwool (fire + acoustic premium, higher cost). Match to their priorities.
**Prevention:** Material comparison guide.

### Problem 29: "Is foil insulation better than batts?"
**Root cause:** Misunderstanding of reflective vs bulk insulation.
**How to tackle:** Explain they do different jobs — foil reflects radiant heat (needs airspace), batts resist conduction. In hot climates foil is valuable; in cool climates bulk is essential. Often used together.
**Prevention:** Explain reflective vs bulk clearly.

### Problem 30: "Do I need sarking under my roof?"
**Root cause:** Confusion about membrane purpose.
**How to tackle:** Explain sarking = condensation control + weather barrier + (foil) radiant heat. Recommend vapour-permeable sarking in cool climates, foil in hot climates. Required in many cases.
**Prevention:** Climate-appropriate sarking guidance.

### Problem 31: "What insulation for a metal shed/garage?"
**Root cause:** Metal buildings have condensation + radiant heat issues.
**How to tackle:** Recommend foil-faced blanket (Anticon/Permastop) directly under metal roofing — handles both condensation and radiant heat. Explain metal roof specifics.
**Prevention:** Foil blanket under metal roofs.

### Problem 32: "What's the best insulation for underfloor?"
**Root cause:** Underfloor has moisture + access challenges.
**How to tackle:** Recommend moisture-resistant products — polyester (Optimo, GreenStuf) or EPS panels (Expol). Explain moisture resistance and DIY-friendliness.
**Prevention:** Moisture-resistant underfloor product.

### Problem 33: "Is spray foam worth it?"
**Root cause:** Customer heard about spray foam (US trend).
**How to tackle:** Explain spray foam is niche in Australia, expensive, needs professional install. For most homes, batts/boards are more cost-effective. Spray foam shines for air-sealing irregular spaces.
**Prevention:** Honest cost/benefit for spray foam.

### Problem 34: "Can I double up insulation?"
**Root cause:** Customer wants to top up existing insulation.
**How to tackle:** Explain you can add batts over existing (e.g., R2.5 over R2.5 = R5.0), but avoid compressing, avoid foil over foil (condensation), and check existing insulation condition first.
**Prevention:** Topping-up guidance.

## E. Installation (Problems 35–42)

### Problem 35: "Can I install insulation myself?"
**Root cause:** Customer wants to DIY.
**How to tackle:** Explain DIY is possible for batts (ceiling/walls) but requires PPE, care around electrical, and correct technique. Foil insulation should be professionally installed (electrical risk). Recommend certified installer for complex jobs.
**Prevention:** DIY safety guidance + PPE.

### Problem 36: "How do I insulate around downlights?"
**Root cause:** Electrical/fire safety concern.
**How to tackle:** Explain must maintain clearance around non-rated downlights, use IC-rated fittings or approved covers, and never cover non-rated lights. Refer to AS/NZS 3000.
**Prevention:** Insulation-compatible downlights.

### Problem 37: "Insulation won't stay in place"
**Root cause:** Wrong size, or product sagging in walls/underfloor.
**How to tackle:** Check joist/stud spacing matches product width. For underfloor, use staples/saddles/clips. For walls, use self-supporting product (polyester, rigid).
**Prevention:** Correct sizing + securing method.

### Problem 38: "How do I cut/install batts correctly?"
**Root cause:** Technique question.
**How to tackle:** Explain cut slightly oversize for snug fit, use sharp knife + straight edge, don't compress, butt batts together, wear PPE. Provide manufacturer instructions.
**Prevention:** Installation instructions + video.

### Problem 39: "Do I need to remove old insulation?"
**Root cause:** Renovation question.
**How to tackle:** Explain remove if damaged/wet/mouldy/vermin-infested. Otherwise can top up. Don't mix incompatible types (e.g., foil over foil). Check condition first.
**Prevention:** Condition assessment before topping up.

### Problem 40: "How do I insulate a cathedral/raked ceiling?"
**Root cause:** No attic space — special construction.
**How to tackle:** Explain need insulation between/under rafters with ventilation gap, or insulated plasterboard (Kooltherm K17). More complex — recommend professional.
**Prevention:** Cathedral ceiling design guidance.

### Problem 41: "Gaps between batts — does it matter?"
**Root cause:** Customer thinks small gaps are fine.
**How to tackle:** Explain gaps reduce performance 20–50% (and acoustic 5–10 dB). Emphasise snug fit, no gaps, butt joints.
**Prevention:** Emphasise gap-free installation.

### Problem 42: "Insulation is itchy — how do I handle safely?"
**Root cause:** Glasswool mechanical irritation.
**How to tackle:** Recommend PPE (gloves, long sleeves, mask, eye protection), or non-irritant polyester product. Explain it's mechanical irritation, not a health hazard (bio-soluble fibres).
**Prevention:** PPE guidance + polyester option.

## F. Compliance & Safety (Problems 43–50)

### Problem 43: "Do I need a building permit for insulation?"
**Root cause:** Compliance question.
**How to tackle:** Explain insulation generally doesn't need a permit, but energy efficiency compliance (7-star) applies to new builds/renovations. Check state/territory rules.
**Prevention:** Compliance guidance.

### Problem 44: "Will this insulation meet NCC/energy rating?"
**Root cause:** Customer needs compliance assurance.
**How to tackle:** Confirm product meets AS/NZS 4859.1, provide declared R-value, explain Total R-value vs product R-value, recommend energy assessor for 7-star confirmation.
**Prevention:** Provide compliance documentation (test reports, CodeMark).

### Problem 45: "Is foil insulation safe around wiring?"
**Root cause:** Electrical safety concern.
**How to tackle:** Explain foil is conductive — never install over cables/light fittings, use non-conductive staples, require electrical safety assessment (AS 3999). Strongly recommend professional install for foil.
**Prevention:** Foil electrical safety guidance.

### Problem 46: "Insulation for a bushfire zone?"
**Root cause:** BAL-rated construction.
**How to tackle:** Determine BAL rating. Recommend non-combustible insulation (glasswool/rockwool). Explain combustible insulation (polystyrene, polyester) is restricted at higher BAL.
**Prevention:** BAL-appropriate product recommendation.

### Problem 47: "Is my installer certified?"
**Root cause:** Quality assurance question.
**How to tackle:** Explain EEC Certified Insulation Installer (CII) certification, recommend certified installers, explain it's required for some government programs.
**Prevention:** Recommend certified installers.

### Problem 48: "What warranty do I get?"
**Root cause:** Warranty question.
**How to tackle:** Explain warranties vary — Bradford 70-year, Foilboard 25-year, Expol 50-year. Clarify what's covered (product performance vs installation).
**Prevention:** Clear warranty information.

### Problem 49: "Insulation and termites/pests?"
**Root cause:** Pest concern.
**How to tackle:** Explain insulation doesn't attract termites, but can hide termite activity. Recommend termite barriers, regular inspection, and avoid blocking weep holes/vents. Some products (EPS) may need termite consideration.
**Prevention:** Termite management guidance.

### Problem 50: "Can insulation cause health issues?"
**Root cause:** Health concern (fibres, VOCs, asthma).
**How to tackle:** Explain modern glasswool is bio-soluble (FBS-1), no evidence of long-term harm, mechanical irritation only. For sensitive occupants recommend polyester (Sensitive Choice approved) or low-VOC products (Earthwool ECOSE, no formaldehyde).
**Prevention:** Health/safety reassurance + low-allergen options.
