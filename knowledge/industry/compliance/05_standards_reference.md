# Standards Reference — Australian Insulation Compliance

**Purpose:** Consolidated reference of all Australian/International standards relevant to insulation compliance.

---

## 1. Thermal insulation

| Standard | Title | Role |
|----------|-------|------|
| **AS/NZS 4859.1:2018** | Thermal insulation materials for buildings — Part 1: General criteria and technical provisions | Product testing, labelling, declared R-values |
| **AS/NZS 4859.2:2018** | Thermal insulation materials for buildings — Part 2: Determination of declared and design thermal values | Total R-Value calculation, thermal bridging |
| **AS 3999:2015** | Bulk thermal insulation — installation | Installation requirements, electrical safety assessment |

## 6. Vapour Control Membrane (VCM) classes — AS 4200.1

| Class | Vapour permeance | NCC application |
|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| Class 1 | Lowest (vapour barrier) | — |
| Class 2 | Low | — |
| Class 3 | Moderate (≥ 0.143 µg/N·s) | Climate zones 4–5 |
| Class 4 | High (≥ 1.14 µg/N·s) | Climate zones 6–8 |

*Vapour permeance tested per ASTM-E96 Procedure B (Water Method) at 23°C, 50% RH.*
