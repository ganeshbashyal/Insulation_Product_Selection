# Glossary — Australian Insulation Terminology

*Comprehensive terminology reference for bot training. Terms are defined in the Australian NCC/standards context.*

---

## Thermal terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **R-Value** | Thermal resistance (m²·K/W). Higher = better insulation. R = thickness ÷ thermal conductivity. |
| **Total R-Value** | Sum of thermal resistances of all layers in a building element, including air films, airspaces, framing, and thermal bridging corrections. |
| **Product R-Value** | Declared R-value of a single insulation product (tested per AS/NZS 4859.1). |
| **U-Value** | Thermal transmittance (W/m²·K). U = 1/R. Lower = better. |
| **Thermal conductivity (λ)** | Material property (W/m·K) — how readily a material conducts heat. Lower = better insulator. |
| **Thermal resistivity (r)** | Reciprocal of conductivity (m·K/W). r = 1/λ. |
| **Thermal bridging** | Heat flow through a path of lower resistance (framing, fixings, penetrations) bypassing insulation. |
| **Thermal break** | Low-conductivity material interrupting a thermal bridge. |
| **Thermal mass** | Ability of a material to store heat (volumetric heat capacity). Distinct from insulation. |
| **Solar absorptance (SA)** | Fraction of solar radiation absorbed by a surface. Light roof ≈ 0.23, dark ≈ 0.85+. |
| **Emissivity (ε)** | Ability of a surface to emit thermal radiation. Foil ≈ 0.03–0.05, most materials ≈ 0.9. |
| **Conduction** | Heat transfer through a solid by molecular vibration. |
| **Convection** | Heat transfer by bulk fluid (air) movement. |
| **Radiation** | Heat transfer by electromagnetic waves (infrared). |
| **Dew point** | Temperature at which air becomes saturated and water vapour condenses. |
| **Interstitial condensation** | Condensation within the building fabric (between layers). |
| **Surface condensation** | Visible condensation on interior surfaces. |
| **Hygrothermal** | Combined effects of moisture and heat on building materials. |
| **Thermal drift** | Decline in foam insulation R-value over time (blowing agent diffusion). |
| **Decrement factor** | Ratio of internal to external temperature swing amplitude. |
| **Time lag** | Delay between peak external and peak internal temperature. |

## Condensation & moisture terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Vapour permeance** | Degree water vapour can diffuse through a material (µg/N·s). |
| **Vapour control membrane (VCM)** | Membrane controlling vapour diffusion. Classified Class 1–4 per AS 4200.1. |
| **Pliable building membrane** | Water barrier classified per AS 4200.1. |
| **Water control layer** | Pliable membrane, or exterior cladding where no membrane present. |
| **Air control layer** | Layer stopping air leakage (carries ~100× more moisture than diffusion). |
| **Primary insulation layer** | The most interior insulation layer of a wall or roof. |
| **Drained cavity** | Cavity that allows water to drain out. |
| **Ventilated cavity** | Cavity with airflow to promote drying. |
| **Mould Growth Index (MGI)** | 0–6 scale quantifying mould growth (per AIRAH DA07). |
| **Relative humidity (RH)** | Moisture in air relative to max at that temperature (%). |
| **Vapour pressure** | Pressure at which vapour is in equilibrium with condensed state. |
| **Sarking** | Membrane under roof cladding (foil or vapour-permeable). |

## Acoustic terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Rw** | Weighted sound reduction index (airborne, laboratory). Higher = better. |
| **Rw + Ctr** | Rw with low-frequency spectrum adaptation term. |
| **Ctr** | Spectrum adaptation term for low frequencies (negative). |
| **DnT,w** | Weighted standardised level difference (airborne, field). |
| **Ln,w** | Weighted normalised impact sound pressure level (lab). Lower = better. |
| **LnT,w** | Weighted standardised impact sound pressure level (field). Lower = better. |
| **STC** | Sound Transmission Class (US rating, similar to Rw). |
| **NRC** | Noise Reduction Coefficient — average absorption at 250–2000 Hz. |
| **Absorption coefficient (α)** | Fraction of incident sound absorbed (0–1). |
| **Reverberation time (RT60)** | Time for sound to decay 60 dB in a room. |
| **Mass law** | Sound insulation increases with mass and frequency (~5–6 dB per doubling). |
| **Coincidence effect** | Dip in insulation at critical frequency (bending wavelength = airborne wavelength). |
| **Mass-air-mass resonance** | Resonant frequency of a double-leaf wall. |
| **Discontinuous construction** | Wall with ≥20mm cavity between 2 leaves, no mechanical linkage (except periphery). |
| **Flanking transmission** | Sound travelling around a separating element via adjacent structure. |
| **Impact sound** | Sound from physical impact (footsteps) transmitted through structure. |
| **Airborne sound** | Sound travelling through air (speech, music). |
| **Decibel (dB)** | Logarithmic unit of sound level. 10 dB ≈ "twice as loud." |
| **Sole-occupancy unit (SOU)** | A room/space for one occupant (apartment, hotel room). |
| **Habitable room** | Room for normal activities (bedroom, living, kitchen). |

## Building classification terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Class 1** | Detached or attached dwellings (houses, townhouses). |
| **Class 2** | Apartments (SOUs above/beside each other). |
| **Class 3** | Residential buildings (hotels, motels, backpackers). |
| **Class 4** | Dwelling within a commercial building (caretaker's flat). |
| **Class 9c** | Residential care (aged care) buildings. |
| **Class 10** | Non-habitable (garages, sheds, carports). |
| **NCC** | National Construction Code. |
| **BCA** | Building Code of Australia (Volumes One & Two of NCC). |
| **DtS** | Deemed-to-Satisfy (prescriptive compliance). |
| **Performance Solution** | Alternative compliance method. |
| **JV3** | Verification method — whole-building energy modelling. |
| **NatHERS** | Nationwide House Energy Rating Scheme (star ratings). |

## Climate zone terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **Climate zone 1** | Hot humid summer, warm winter (Darwin, Cairns). |
| **Climate zone 2** | Warm humid summer, mild winter (Brisbane). |
| **Climate zone 3** | Hot dry summer, warm winter (Alice Springs). |
| **Climate zone 4** | Hot dry summer, cool winter (Broken Hill, Mildura). |
| **Climate zone 5** | Warm temperate (Sydney, Perth, Adelaide). |
| **Climate zone 6** | Mild temperate (Melbourne, Canberra). |
| **Climate zone 7** | Cool temperate (Hobart). |
| **Climate zone 8** | Alpine (alpine VIC/NSW/TAS). |
| **Diurnal range** | Day–night temperature difference. |

## Installation & compliance terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **BAL** | Bushfire Attack Level (AS 3959). |
| **Certified Insulation Installer (CII)** | EEC certification for installers. |
| **Bio-soluble fibre** | Fibre that dissolves in the body (FBS-1 certified glasswool). |
| **Non-combustible** | Material that doesn't burn (AS 1530.1). |
| **CodeMark** | Product certification scheme (ABCB). |
| **EPD** | Environmental Product Declaration. |
| **VOC** | Volatile Organic Compound. |
| **Sensitive Choice** | National Asthma Council approval program. |
