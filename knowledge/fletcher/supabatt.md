# supaBATT External Wall Barrier

Rigid glasswool external-wall board supplied unfaced or with a Class 4 vapour-permeable facing. Confirm whether the VP4 option is required, compatibility with adjacent membranes, wall R-value and condensation design. It contributes to a wall system but does not alone establish NCC compliance.
