# SupaBATT Insulation

## Canonical description

SupaBATT is Fletcher's glasswool batt family for thermal and acoustic contribution in suitable building cavities. Treat each wall/ceiling/application and R-value as a distinct SKU even when the brand is shared. Manufacturer-supported claims such as recycled content, fire performance, warranty or certification must be checked against the current product page/TDS scope.

## Selection position

Use SupaBATT where the project or channel specifies this range and its dimensions/R-values suit the cavity. Do not assume it is interchangeable with Pink Batts, Soundbreak or Pink Partition: compare application, density, thickness, width, certification, region and commercial availability. For acoustic-first separating walls, Soundbreak is the stronger purpose-led starting point.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Fix
supaBATT boards horizontally or vertically to the outside face of timber or
steel stud framing.
2. Secure boards mechanically with approved insulation
fixings or brick tie brackets into framing.
3. For supaBATT VP4, ensure the
vapour-permeable membrane faces outward towards the brick cavity.
4. Overlap the
integrated membrane flap of upper rows over lower rows and tape with 3M Seaming
Tape / VapaSeam Tape.
5. Where unfaced supaBATT is used, butt boards tightly
together and install an approved external wall wrap over the face.
6. Tape and
seal all cuts, penetrations, and corners to maintain air and water barrier
integrity.
7. Fasten brick ties through supaBATT into structural studs,
maintaining required cavity clearance to brickwork.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- Must be installed on
external face of stud framing behind masonry or cladding with a drained cavity.
- Cladding/brickwork should be installed without prolonged delay to prevent
weather damage.
- Must not be compressed into internal stud bays designed for
soft batts.
