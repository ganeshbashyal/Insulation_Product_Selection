# Pink Partition Insulation

## Canonical description

Pink Partition is Fletcher's glasswool family for thermal and acoustic contribution inside internal partition systems, particularly commercial/lightweight steel-stud construction. Fletcher documents non-combustibility, up to 80% recycled content, Australian manufacture and CodeMark CM30006 within scope. The current range contains multiple densities, thicknesses, widths and product codes; density/size must not be treated as an acoustic system rating.

## Selection logic

Choose Pink Partition for specified internal partition build-ups and commercial cavity formats. Choose Soundbreak for residential/separating-wall acoustic comfort where that system evidence and batt sizing fit. Choose Pink Batts Wall when thermal wall performance is primary. The plasterboard layers, stud type, cavity, seals, doors, penetrations and flanking determine Rw/Rw+Ctr.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Ensure framing studs are
erected and cavity spaces are dry and clear of debris.
2. Select batt dimensions
matching stud width (450mm or 600mm).
3. Friction-fit Pink Partition batts snugly
between steel or timber studs.
4. Ensure batts butt tightly against top and
bottom tracks and adjacent batts with zero gaps.
5. Cut neatly around electrical
conduits, plumbing, and wall penetrations using a serrated knife.
6. Maintain
batt thickness to match cavity depth without crushing or over-compressing.
7. Fasten plasterboard or wall lining according to fire and acoustic system
specifications.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- For internal wall cavities and commercial partitions; not for
direct external weather exposure.
- Avoid severe compression beyond cavity
thickness.
- Must not be installed over unsealed damp surfaces.
