# Pink Partition Insulation

## Canonical description

Pink Partition is Fletcher's glasswool family for thermal and acoustic contribution inside internal partition systems, particularly commercial/lightweight steel-stud construction. Fletcher documents non-combustibility, up to 80% recycled content, Australian manufacture and CodeMark CM30006 within scope. The current range contains multiple densities, thicknesses, widths and product codes; density/size must not be treated as an acoustic system rating.

## Selection logic

Choose Pink Partition for specified internal partition build-ups and commercial cavity formats. Choose Soundbreak for residential/separating-wall acoustic comfort where that system evidence and batt sizing fit. Choose Pink Batts Wall when thermal wall performance is primary. The plasterboard layers, stud type, cavity, seals, doors, penetrations and flanking determine Rw/Rw+Ctr.
