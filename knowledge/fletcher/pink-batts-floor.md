# Pink Batts Floor Insulation

Glasswool thermal insulation for suitable suspended floors. Confirm underfloor access, joist spacing, retention/support, moisture exposure and the current product code before selection. It is not an impact-noise underlay and should not be recommended solely for footsteps through a finished floor.
