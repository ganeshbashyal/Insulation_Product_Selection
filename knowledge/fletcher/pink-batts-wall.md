# Pink Batts Wall Insulation

Glasswool thermal batts for internal and external residential wall cavities. Recommend this family when wall energy efficiency is primary; prefer Soundbreak when sound transfer is primary. Confirm stud spacing, cavity depth, material R-value, climate/condensation design and current regional SKU. Product R-value is not the complete wall Total R-value.
