# Soundbreak Acoustic Insulation

Higher-density glasswool for reducing sound transfer through designed walls, ceilings and floors while contributing thermal resistance. Use for cavity absorption, not as a substitute for a limp-mass barrier. A batt's material R-value and absorption data do not establish the Rw of the completed construction; use tested-system evidence for acoustic targets.
