# Permastop Building Blanket

## Canonical description

Permastop is a faced glasswool building blanket for suitable metal-roof and commercial/residential constructions. Fletcher positions it for thermal performance, condensation management and reduction of rain/noise effects, with low-, medium- and heavy-duty foil-facing options in the current range. Facing grade, blanket thickness, mesh/support and roof system must match the job.

## Selection logic

Use Permastop when a flexible roof blanket beneath metal cladding is required. Use Pink Batts Ceiling for ceiling-level cavity insulation, Pink Thermal Slab for semi-rigid soffit/concrete applications, or Vapawrap Metal Roof when a vapour-permeable membrane—not bulk glasswool—is required. A blanket R-value is not the roof Total R-value; rain-noise improvement is system-specific.

## Installation and clearances

Extracted from the manufacturer datasheet; the bot may share this context but must not issue project-specific installation instructions or override the human-reviewed install guide.

1. Roll out Permastop over roof
framing purlins or safety wire mesh with reflective foil facing downward towards
building interior.
2. Ensure 150mm factory overlap flap extends over the adjacent
roll.
3. Tape all foil lap joints securely with 72mm reinforced aluminium foil
tape to form a continuous vapour barrier.
4. Allow a nominal 40mm sag between
purlins to promote condensation drainage towards the eaves if unmeshed.
5. Fasten
metal roof sheeting directly over the glasswool blanket through to purlins.
6. Ensure blanket terminates neatly at the gutter line without obstructing
stormwater flow.
7. Use Roof Razor spacers in commercial roofs to avoid blanket
compression over purlins.

**Manufacturer-stated limitations**

- Must not be left exposed to
rain or weather prior to roof sheet installation.
- Foil lap joints must be
taped continuously to maintain vapour barrier performance.
- For tropical
high-humidity climates, specify Permastop Tropic with outward-facing foil.
