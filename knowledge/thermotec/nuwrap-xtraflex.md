# Thermotec NuWrap XtraFlex

## Bot-ready summary

NuWrap XtraFlex is a lightweight, fibre-free and highly flexible composite acoustic lagging for waste pipes, PVC pipes and fittings, ductwork and related services. It combines a visco-elastic polymer barrier, reinforced fire-resistant aluminium foil facing and acoustic foam.

It is intended where easier handling, reduced material usage, tight clearances or small-diameter pipework make a thinner and more flexible solution desirable.

## Published formats

- Full roll: 3 m × 1.35 m
- Half roll: 5 m × 675 mm

## Limitations

- NuWrap XtraFlex and NuWrap 5 are separate product families.
- Do not assume their thickness, mass, roll coverage or acoustic results are interchangeable.
- The manufacturer refers to an `Rw + Ctr 40` development result; use the exact notation and application only after checking the current TDS/test report.
- Final compliance depends on the complete pipe/fitting installation and sealing details.
